## Understanding Volumetric Information: Beyond the Surface

Volumetric information, or volumetric data, goes beyond simple two-dimensional images or even surface-level 3D models. It provides a complete, three-dimensional representation of an object or space, capturing its internal structure and properties as well as its external form.  Think of it as a digital twin, a perfect replica in the virtual world. This is achieved through techniques like 3D scanning, computed tomography (CT), and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI).  The key difference?  Volumetric data contains information about *volume*, allowing for far more detailed analysis and application than surface-only data.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  Don't confuse volumetric data with simple 3D models.  While 3D models can be *derived* from volumetric data, they often lack the richness of the underlying information.  Volumetric data is the raw, unprocessed information, offering far greater potential for analysis.
