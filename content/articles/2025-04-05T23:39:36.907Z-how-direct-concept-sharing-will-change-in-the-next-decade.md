## Common Challenges and Solutions in Implementing Direct Concept Sharing

Implementing direct concept sharing can present several challenges:

*   **Resistance to Change:** Some individuals may be resistant to adopting new tools and processes.
    *   **Solution:** Communicate the benefits of direct concept sharing clearly and provide ample training and support. Involve employees in the implementation process to gain their buy-in.

    *   **Solution:** Invest in reliable and user-friendly tools. Provide technical support and training to help users troubleshoot problems.
