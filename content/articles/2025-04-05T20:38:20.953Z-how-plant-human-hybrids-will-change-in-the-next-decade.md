### Human-Plant Chimeras: Growing Organs in Greenhouses?

One of the most promising, yet ethically complex, areas of research involves creating human-plant chimeras. A chimera, in this context, is an organism composed of cells from two or more distinct individuals. The hope is to grow human organs within plants, addressing the critical shortage of donor organs. Scientists are exploring methods to introduce human stem cells into plant embryos, guiding their development into specific organs. This approach, while still in its early stages, could potentially revolutionize transplantation medicine. Imagine greenhouses filled with plants cultivating human hearts, livers, or kidneys.

> **DID YOU KNOW?** The first successful human-animal chimera was created in 2017, paving the way for more complex research into human-plant chimeras.
