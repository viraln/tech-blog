Space technology's influence extends far beyond rocket launches and astronaut training.  Its applications are diverse and increasingly integrated into our everyday lives.  Consider these examples:

* **Navigation & Positioning:**  Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS), like GPS, Galileo, and GLONASS, are fundamental to modern navigation, impacting everything from transportation and logistics to precision agriculture and emergency services.
* **Communication:** Satellite communication networks provide global connectivity, bridging geographical divides and enabling seamless communication across vast distances, particularly crucial in remote areas.
* **Earth Observation:** Satellites equipped with advanced sensors monitor our planet's health, providing crucial data for weather forecasting, climate change research, disaster management, and environmental monitoring.
* **Scientific Research:** Space-based telescopes, like the James Webb Space Telescope, push the boundaries of astronomical observation, enabling groundbreaking discoveries about the universe's origins and evolution.
