## Understanding Past-Future Arbitrage: A Conceptual Overview

Past-future arbitrage, at its core, involves exploiting price differences between the present and predicted future values of an asset.  It's not about predicting the future perfectly; rather, it’s about identifying situations where the current market undervalues or overvalues an asset relative to its anticipated future price, considering factors like anticipated growth, market sentiment shifts, or technological advancements. ![Graph showing price divergence between present and future prediction](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1485740112426-0c2549fa8c86?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
