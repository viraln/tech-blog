## 2. Quantum Tunneling:  Explaining Unexpected Species Distributions and Adaptations

Quantum tunneling, another cornerstone of quantum mechanics, describes the ability of a particle to pass through a potential energy barrier even if it doesn't possess enough energy to overcome it classically.  This seemingly improbable event has profound implications for ecological processes.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  While direct observation of quantum tunneling in macroscopic ecological systems is challenging, its theoretical implications are significant, particularly when considering the movement and dispersal of organisms.

Consider the dispersal of plant seeds across seemingly insurmountable barriers – mountains, rivers, or even vast distances.
