## What is an NFT? Demystifying the Meaning

The simplest explanation for "NFT meaning" is this: an NFT is a unique digital asset, verifiable on a blockchain.  Unlike cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, which are fungible (interchangeable), each NFT is one-of-a-kind. Think of it like a digital certificate of authenticity for a piece of art, a collectible item, or even a virtual piece of land in a metaverse.  This uniqueness is what gives NFTs their value.  The blockchain ensures provenance and ownership, making it virtually impossible to duplicate or forge. ![A simple graphic explaining the concept of an NFT and its uniqueness on a blockchain.](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1652858319770-860b97ebe5b2?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
