**Step 1:** **Data Preparation:** Gather a large dataset of simulations or observational data to train the network. This data must be preprocessed and formatted in a way that is compatible with the neural network architecture.

**Step 2:** **Network Architecture Design:** Choose an appropriate neural network architecture, such as a convolutional neural network (CNN) or a recurrent neural network (RNN), depending on the specific task.

**Step 3:** **Training:** Train the network using the prepared data. This involves adjusting the network's parameters to minimize the difference between its predictions and the actual values.

**Step 4:** **Validation and Testing:** Evaluate the network's performance on a separate validation dataset to ensure that it is generalizing well and not overfitting to the training data.

**Step 5:** **Deployment:** Deploy the trained network to perform simulations or analyze astronomical data.
