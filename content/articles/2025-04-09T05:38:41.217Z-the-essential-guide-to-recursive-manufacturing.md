## Understanding Recursive Manufacturing: The Self-Replicating Factory

Recursive manufacturing, at its core, is a system where automated manufacturing processes create the very machines and tools required for their own operation and expansion.  It's a self-replicating system, akin to biological reproduction, but on an industrial scale.  Instead of relying on external suppliers for equipment upgrades or repairs, a recursively manufactured system produces these elements autonomously, leading to a continuously evolving and optimizing production environment.
