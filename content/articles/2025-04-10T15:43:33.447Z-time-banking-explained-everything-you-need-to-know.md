## What is Time Banking?

Time banking is a community-based system where individuals contribute their skills and talents, receiving credits for their time. These credits can then be redeemed for services offered by other members within the network. Unlike traditional volunteering, time banking operates on a reciprocal basis, fostering a sense of mutual benefit and empowerment.  Think of it as a social currency, where your time is valued equally regardless of the type of service offered.  ![A diverse group of people participating in various time banking activities - gardening, tutoring, etc.](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1434030216411-0b793f4b4173?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
