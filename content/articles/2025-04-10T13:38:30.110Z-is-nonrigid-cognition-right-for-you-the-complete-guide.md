## What is Nonrigid Cognition? Understanding Flexible Thinking

Nonrigid cognition, also known as flexible thinking, cognitive flexibility, or adaptable thinking, is the mental ability to shift perspectives, adapt to new information, and switch between different tasks or thought processes efficiently.  It's the opposite of rigid thinking, which is characterized by inflexibility, resistance to change, and a tendency to stick to pre-conceived notions.  Think of it as your brain's ability to be a nimble dancer, rather than a stiff statue.  ![A brain image depicting flexible neural pathways](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1702353531290-8fe0bfbf2732?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
