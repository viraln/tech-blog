## The Core Principles of Folded Space Design

Folded space design, at its heart, is about maximizing utility and minimizing footprint. It's an interdisciplinary field that blends architecture, interior design, engineering, and even robotics to create dynamic and adaptable living environments. The core principles revolve around:

*   **Multi-functionality:** One object serves multiple purposes. Think a coffee table that transforms into a dining table, or a bed that folds away to become a desk.
*   **Transformability:** Spaces and objects can change their configuration based on user needs. This goes beyond simple folding; it can involve sliding, rotating, or even telescoping elements.
*   **Compactness:** Reducing the overall size and volume of objects without sacrificing functionality or comfort.
*   **Efficiency:** Optimizing the use of every square inch, eliminating wasted space and creating seamless transitions between different activities.
*   **Integration:** Blending different functions and elements into a cohesive and harmonious whole.
