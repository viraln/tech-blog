## Quantum Mechanics and Universe Superposition

The foundation of universe superposition lies firmly in the principles of quantum mechanics. The superposition principle, a cornerstone of quantum theory, states that a quantum system can exist in a combination of multiple states until measured.  This is famously illustrated by Schrödinger's cat thought experiment, where a cat inside a box is simultaneously alive and dead until the box is opened.

Applying this principle to cosmology is a significant leap, but some physicists believe it's necessary to reconcile observations like the Big Bang and the seemingly fine-tuned constants of nature.
