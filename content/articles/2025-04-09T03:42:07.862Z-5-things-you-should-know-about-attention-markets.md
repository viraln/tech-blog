## 2. How to Monetize Attention: Strategies and Tactics

Monetizing attention involves leveraging various strategies to transform captured attention into revenue.  This isn't just about displaying ads; it's about creating valuable experiences that keep users engaged.  Here are some key approaches:

* 🔑 **Premium Content & Subscriptions:** Offering exclusive content behind a paywall. Examples include Netflix, Spotify, and many news publications.
* ⚡ **Targeted Advertising:**  Using data to deliver highly relevant ads to specific user segments.
* ✅ **Affiliate Marketing:** Earning commissions by promoting other companies' products or services.
* 📈 **Data Sales (with user consent):**  Anonymized and aggregated user data can be valuable to market research firms.
* 🤝 **Freemium Models:** Offering a basic service for free while charging for premium features.
