## What is Mineral-Based AI?

Mineral-based AI, also known as Mineral AI or AI powered by minerals, departs from traditional silicon-based computing by utilizing minerals as the fundamental building blocks for AI hardware. Instead of silicon chips, these systems leverage the inherent physical and chemical properties of minerals to perform computations. This approach promises significant advantages in terms of energy efficiency, sustainability, and potentially, computational power. Imagine AI systems that are not only smarter but also greener.

## Key Minerals in AI Development: Exploring the Possibilities

Several minerals are currently being investigated for their potential in AI applications. Here are a few prominent examples:

*   **Perovskites:** These materials exhibit excellent optoelectronic properties, making them suitable for solar cells and light-based computing. Their tunable structure allows for fine-tuning their electrical and optical characteristics.
*   **Oxides (e.g., Titanium Dioxide, Zinc Oxide):** Certain oxides possess memristive properties, meaning they can "remember" past electrical signals. This makes them ideal for building neuromorphic computing systems that mimic the human brain.
*   **Ferroelectric Materials (e.g., Barium Titanate):** These materials exhibit spontaneous electric polarization, which can be switched by an external electric field. This property can be used to create non-volatile memory and logic devices.
*   **2D Materials (e.g., Molybdenum Disulfide):** Similar to graphene, these atomically thin materials offer unique electronic properties and potential for miniaturization in AI hardware.
