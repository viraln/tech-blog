## What is Qualia Computing?

Qualia Computing, unlike classical computing which relies on binary code, attempts to simulate subjective experiences – qualia – like the redness of red or the feeling of pain.  It aims to bridge the gap between objective data and subjective understanding.  Instead of processing information through logic gates, Qualia Computing utilizes novel architectures inspired by neural networks and cognitive science.  This allows for a more holistic and context-aware approach to problem-solving, moving beyond mere pattern recognition to genuine comprehension. ![A stylized graphic depicting the difference between classical and qualia computing architectures](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1484807352052-23338990c6c6?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
