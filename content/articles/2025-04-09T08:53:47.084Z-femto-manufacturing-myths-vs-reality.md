## What is Femto-Manufacturing?

Femto-manufacturing utilizes femtosecond lasers, emitting pulses of light lasting only quadrillionths of a second (10⁻¹⁵ seconds).  These incredibly short pulses deliver highly localized energy, enabling the precise ablation, cutting, and structuring of almost any material with minimal heat-affected zones.  Unlike traditional manufacturing methods, femtosecond laser processing offers unparalleled control over the material's microstructure, leading to superior quality and performance in the final product.
