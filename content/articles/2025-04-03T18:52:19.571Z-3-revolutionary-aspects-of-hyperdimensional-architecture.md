Hyperdimensional architecture uses these concepts to explore spatial relationships that are impossible to realize in 3D space. This involves:

*   **Non-Euclidean Geometries:** Embracing geometries where parallel lines can converge or diverge, leading to spaces that warp and bend in ways that defy intuition. This can be achieved conceptually by manipulating the rules of space within a design.
*   **Topology:** Focusing on the properties of shapes that remain unchanged under continuous deformations (like stretching, twisting, or bending), allowing for the creation of unexpected adjacencies and connections within a structure.
*   **Visual Illusions:** Creating designs that play with perspective and perception to simulate the feeling of being in a space that extends beyond three dimensions. This often involves carefully placed mirrors, distorted proportions, and anamorphic art.
