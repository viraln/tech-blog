## 6. Common Challenges and Solutions in Sensory Design

One major challenge is accessibility.  Sensory design must be inclusive, catering to users with diverse needs and abilities.  For example, providing alt text for images, captions for videos, and avoiding flashing animations are essential for users with visual impairments.  Another challenge is the cost and complexity of implementing advanced sensory technologies.  However, cost-effective solutions are emerging, making sensory design more accessible to businesses of all sizes.
