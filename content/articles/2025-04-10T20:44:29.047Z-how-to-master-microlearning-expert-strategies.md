## What is Microlearning?  Understanding the Fundamentals

Microlearning, at its core, is the delivery of learning content in short, focused bursts.  Think bite-sized chunks of information, typically lasting between 1-5 minutes. This contrasts sharply with traditional eLearning, which often involves lengthy modules and hours of study.  Instead of overwhelming learners with vast amounts of information, microlearning focuses on a single, specific learning objective.  This approach leverages the principles of spaced repetition and focused attention to maximize learning effectiveness.

## Microlearning vs. eLearning: A Comparative Analysis

| Feature          | Microlearning                               | eLearning                                    |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Content Length   | Short, focused modules (1-5 minutes)        | Longer modules (30 minutes or more)         |
| Delivery Method  | Mobile-friendly, various formats             | Primarily online, often desktop-based         |
| Learning Style   | Best for bite-sized learning, quick updates | Better for in-depth learning, complex topics |
| Engagement       | High                                       | Can be lower, particularly with long modules |
| Cost             | Generally lower                              | Often higher                                  |
