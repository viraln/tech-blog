## Relativity Engineering Principles:  Beyond E=mc²

Relativity engineering isn't simply about plugging Einstein's famous equation into design calculations.  It involves a deeper understanding of:

* **Special Relativity:**  Focuses on the relationship between space and time for objects moving at high speeds relative to each other.  Key concepts include time dilation (time slows down at high speeds) and length contraction (objects appear shorter in the direction of motion).
* **General Relativity:**  Explores the relationship between gravity and the curvature of spacetime.  This is crucial for systems operating in strong gravitational fields, such as those near black holes or orbiting planets.
* **Lorentz Transformations:**  Mathematical tools used to transform measurements between different inertial frames of reference, accounting for relativistic effects.
* **Tensor Calculus:**  A sophisticated mathematical framework essential for describing and manipulating quantities in curved spacetime, as required in general relativity.
