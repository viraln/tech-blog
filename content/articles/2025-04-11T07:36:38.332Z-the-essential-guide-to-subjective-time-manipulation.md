## Understanding Subjective Time and its Mechanisms

Subjective time, unlike objective time (measured by clocks), is a deeply personal and fluctuating experience.  Numerous factors influence it, including:

* 🔑 **Attentional Focus:**  When deeply engrossed in an activity, time seems to fly. Conversely, boredom stretches time out.
* ⚡ **Emotional State:**  Positive emotions often compress time, while negative emotions expand it.  Think back to a thrilling rollercoaster ride versus a period of intense anxiety.
* ✅ **Memory Consolidation:**  Vivid memories make a time period feel longer, while less memorable events compress.

##  The Science Behind Time Distortion

Neuroscience sheds light on the brain regions involved in time perception, primarily the cerebellum, basal ganglia, and prefrontal cortex.  These areas interact to estimate time intervals, but the process is far from perfect and easily influenced by internal and external factors.

Current research (2025) explores the roles of neurotransmitters like dopamine and norepinephrine in time perception.  Elevated dopamine levels, for example, are linked to an underestimation of time intervals, making time feel faster.
