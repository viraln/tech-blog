## 4.  Securing Our Digital World with Cryptography

In today's interconnected world, cybersecurity is paramount. Cryptography, the science of secure communication, relies heavily on advanced mathematical concepts like number theory, abstract algebra, and probability.  Public-key cryptography, a cornerstone of online security, utilizes prime numbers and modular arithmetic to ensure secure data transmission.  Without the underlying mathematical principles, our online transactions, communications, and sensitive data would be highly vulnerable.
