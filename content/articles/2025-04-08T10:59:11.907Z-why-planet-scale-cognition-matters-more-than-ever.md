## Planet-Scale Computing for AI: The Infrastructure Challenge

Implementing planet-scale cognition requires a massive investment in computing infrastructure. We need to be able to process and analyze data at unprecedented scales, which means building new types of computing platforms.

*   **Edge Computing:** Processing data closer to the source, reducing latency and bandwidth requirements.
*   **Cloud Computing:** Providing scalable and on-demand computing resources.
*   **Federated Learning:** Training AI models on distributed data without sharing the data itself, preserving privacy and security.
*   **Quantum Computing:** Offering the potential to solve problems that are currently intractable for classical computers.
