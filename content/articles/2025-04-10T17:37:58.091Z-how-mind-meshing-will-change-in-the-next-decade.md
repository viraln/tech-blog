## What is Mind Meshing and How Does it Work?

Mind meshing, also known as brain-to-brain interfacing, refers to the direct communication and exchange of information between two or more brains without the use of traditional communication methods like speech or writing.  While still in its nascent stages, current research focuses primarily on non-invasive methods.  This typically involves using electroencephalography (EEG) to detect brainwave patterns, decoding them into digital signals, and then transmitting these signals to another brain via a similar interface, usually stimulating specific brain regions through transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) or other techniques. ![Illustration of EEG and TMS devices connected to two brains.](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1667681362383-d9239c8fa2b7?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
