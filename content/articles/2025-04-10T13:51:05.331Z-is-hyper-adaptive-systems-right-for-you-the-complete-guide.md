## What are Hyper-Adaptive Systems?

Hyper-adaptive systems represent a significant leap beyond traditional adaptive systems.  While adaptive systems react to changes, hyper-adaptive systems *anticipate* and *predict* them. They leverage advanced technologies like AI, machine learning, and predictive analytics to not only respond to disruptions but also proactively optimize themselves for optimal performance in dynamic environments.
