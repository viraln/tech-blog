## Open Source vs. Closed Source: Weighing the Pros and Cons

The choice between open source and closed source software often depends on specific needs and priorities.  Let's examine the key differences:

| Feature          | Open Source                               | Closed Source                               |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| **Source Code**  | Publicly available                         | Proprietary and private                     |
| **Cost**         | Typically free (but may have paid support)| Usually requires a license fee             |
| **Customization**| Highly customizable                       | Limited customization options                |
| **Transparency** | High                                      | Low                                        |
| **Community**    | Large, active community                   | Limited or no community interaction        |
| **Security**     | Often considered more secure (due to scrutiny)| Security vulnerabilities may be hidden      |

> **EXPERT TIP:** While open source is generally free, remember to factor in the potential costs of support, customization, and integration.
