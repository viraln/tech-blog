## 1. Redefining Evolution: From Passive Observation to Active Creation

Traditional evolutionary biology focuses on observing and understanding how species arise naturally through processes like natural selection, genetic drift, and mutation. Artificial speciation flips this script entirely. It's about actively *driving* the formation of new species in a controlled environment, using tools like genetic engineering, directed evolution, and environmental manipulation. This shift from passive observation to active creation marks a fundamental change in our relationship with evolution.
