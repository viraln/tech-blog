## What is Gravitational Engineering?

Gravitational engineering, in its simplest form, is the application of scientific principles to manipulate or utilize gravitational forces for practical purposes.  Unlike traditional engineering that largely works *against* gravity, this field aims to harness or modify its effects. While we're not yet building anti-gravity devices, the pursuit involves exploring technologies and methodologies to influence gravitational interactions on a smaller, more controlled scale.
