## Understanding Speciation: A Biology Definition

Before we dive into artificial speciation, let's clarify what speciation means. In its simplest form, **speciation definition biology** refers to the evolutionary process by which populations evolve to become distinct species. This typically involves reproductive isolation, where populations can no longer interbreed and exchange genetic material.  ![A diagram illustrating different modes of speciation (allopatric, sympatric, parapatric)](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1535378917042-10a22c95931a?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

Artificial speciation aims to accelerate and direct this process through human intervention.  Instead of relying on chance and environmental pressures, scientists employ various techniques to induce speciation. These methods often involve:
