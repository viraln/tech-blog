## Designing Dark Mode Interfaces for Accessibility

Accessibility remains a paramount concern.  While dark mode offers benefits for users with photosensitive epilepsy, proper design is crucial for those with visual impairments.  Key considerations include:

* **Sufficient contrast:**  Ensure adequate contrast between text and background elements to meet WCAG guidelines.
* **Font selection:**  Choose fonts that are highly legible in low-light conditions.
* **Alternative text for images:**  Always provide descriptive alternative text for images to aid screen reader users.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  Always test your dark mode designs with users who have visual impairments to ensure accessibility.
