## What is a Digital Passport?

A digital passport, also known as a digital identity passport, is a secure, electronic version of your traditional paper passport.  It typically stores your biometric data (fingerprint, facial recognition), passport details, and potentially other relevant information like visa details, all encrypted and secured within a mobile app or a secure digital wallet.  Unlike a physical passport, which can be lost, stolen, or damaged, a digital passport offers enhanced security and convenience.  It's not just a scanned copy of your physical passport; it's a digitally verifiable and tamper-proof identity document. ![A smartphone displaying a digital passport app interface](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1642543349642-0d04e91511c9?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## How to Get a Digital Passport (2025 Perspective)

The process of obtaining a digital passport varies depending on your country of citizenship.  Some countries are pioneers in this technology, while others are still in the pilot phase or planning stages.  Generally, the process might involve:

1. **Applying Online:**  You'll likely need to apply through a government-approved online portal.
2. **Biometric Data Capture:** You might need to visit a designated center to have your biometric data captured (fingerprints, facial scan).
3. **Verification and Approval:** Your application will undergo thorough verification before approval.
4. **Digital Passport Issuance:** Once approved, your digital passport will be issued and accessible through a dedicated app or digital wallet.
5. **Linking to Physical Passport:** In many cases, the digital passport will be linked to your existing physical passport, acting as a supplementary document.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Keep your device secure and update your app regularly to maintain the integrity of your digital passport.
