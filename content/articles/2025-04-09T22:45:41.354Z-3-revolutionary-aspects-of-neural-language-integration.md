## Common Challenges and Solutions in Neural Language Integration

Despite the advancements, challenges remain.  One common hurdle is the **cost of neural language integration**, particularly for resource-intensive models.  Solutions include using smaller, more efficient models or leveraging cloud-based services to distribute computational costs.

Another challenge is **data bias**.  Neural language models are trained on massive datasets, which may contain biases that are reflected in the model's output.  Mitigating this requires careful data curation and the use of techniques to detect and correct biases.
