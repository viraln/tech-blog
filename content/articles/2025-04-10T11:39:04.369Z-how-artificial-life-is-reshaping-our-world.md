## What is Artificial Life?

Artificial Life, often abbreviated as ALife, is an interdisciplinary field that explores life as a computational process.  It involves creating and studying artificial systems that exhibit characteristics associated with natural living systems, such as reproduction, adaptation, evolution, and self-organization.
