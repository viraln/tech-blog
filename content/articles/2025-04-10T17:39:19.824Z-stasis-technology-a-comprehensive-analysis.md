## What is Stasis Technology?

Stasis technology, in its broadest sense, refers to the controlled suspension of biological processes in living organisms.  This isn't simply slowing down metabolism; it's about achieving a state where cellular activity is significantly reduced, preventing degradation and damage, and allowing for revival at a later time.  Unlike cryogenics, which focuses on freezing at extremely low temperatures, stasis aims for a more controlled, potentially reversible state, often at temperatures closer to normal body temperature.  This distinction is crucial, as the damage caused by ice crystal formation in cryogenics remains a significant hurdle.

> **DID YOU KNOW?**  Research into stasis-like states has been ongoing for decades, with some success observed in certain invertebrate species, laying the groundwork for future human applications.
