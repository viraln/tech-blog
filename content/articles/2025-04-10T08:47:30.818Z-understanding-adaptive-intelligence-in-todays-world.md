## What is Adaptive Intelligence?

Adaptive intelligence, a subset of artificial intelligence (AI), refers to systems capable of autonomously adjusting their behavior and decision-making processes in response to changing environments and new information. Unlike traditional AI, which operates on pre-programmed rules and fixed datasets, adaptive intelligence systems dynamically learn, adapt, and improve their performance over time without explicit human intervention. ## Adaptive AI vs. Artificial Intelligence: Key Differences

While adaptive intelligence is a type of artificial intelligence, there's a crucial distinction.  Traditional AI often relies on static models and pre-defined rules.  Adaptive AI, however, goes beyond this, exhibiting the capacity for continuous learning and self-improvement.

| Feature          | Adaptive Intelligence                               | Traditional AI                                   |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| **Learning**     | Continuous, dynamic learning from experience        | Primarily based on pre-programmed rules and data  |
| **Adaptation**   | Autonomous adjustment to changing environments       | Limited adaptability to unforeseen circumstances |
| **Flexibility**  | High flexibility and adaptability                   | Lower flexibility, requires reprogramming for changes |
| **Decision-Making** | Dynamic, context-aware decision-making             | Based on pre-defined logic and algorithms         |
