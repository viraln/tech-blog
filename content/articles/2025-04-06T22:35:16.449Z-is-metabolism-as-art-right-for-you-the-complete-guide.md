## What Exactly is Metabolism as Art? Unveiling the Concept

Metabolism art, or art inspired by metabolism, is a diverse and evolving field that explores the body's biochemical processes – the sum of all chemical reactions that occur within a living organism to maintain life – through artistic mediums.
