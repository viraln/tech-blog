## What is Cognitive Concrete?

Cognitive concrete, also known as self-healing concrete or smart concrete, represents a significant leap forward in construction materials. It's not just about increased strength and durability; it's about creating a material with an inherent "intelligence."  This intelligence is achieved through the integration of various sensors, actuators, and self-healing mechanisms embedded within the concrete matrix.  These components allow the concrete to monitor its own condition, detect damage, and even initiate self-repair processes.
