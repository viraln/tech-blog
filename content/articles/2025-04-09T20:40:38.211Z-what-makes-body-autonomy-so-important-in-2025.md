## What is Body Autonomy?  A Comprehensive Definition

Body autonomy, at its core, is the right of every individual to make their own decisions about their body and health.  This encompasses a broad spectrum of choices, including:

* 🔑 **Medical Decisions:**  Choosing treatments, refusing procedures, and selecting healthcare providers.
* ⚡ **Reproductive Rights:**  Deciding whether or not to have children, utilizing contraception, and accessing abortion services.
* ✅ **Personal Appearance:**  Making choices about clothing, body modifications, and personal expression.

It's crucial to understand that body autonomy isn't absolute.  It must be balanced with the rights and safety of others.  However, the presumption should always be in favor of individual choice, unless there's a demonstrably compelling reason to override it.  ![A diverse group of people making different choices about their bodies](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1532200846567-1bd8bd5b23aa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
