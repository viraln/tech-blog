## What is Programmable Matter?

Programmable matter refers to materials whose properties, such as shape, density, stiffness, and conductivity, can be dynamically altered in a controlled manner.  This isn't about simply changing the state of a material (like melting ice); it's about precise, on-demand control at a microscopic or macroscopic level.  Imagine a material that can reconfigure itself to form a chair, then a table, then a staircase – all without human intervention.  That's the core concept behind programmable matter.
