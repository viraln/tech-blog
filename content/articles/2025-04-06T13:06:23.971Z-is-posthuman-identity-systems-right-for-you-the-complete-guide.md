Transhuman identity systems represent a more radical vision of posthuman identity. These systems involve the integration of technology directly into the human body or mind, blurring the lines between human and machine.

*   **Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs):** BCIs allow for direct communication between the brain and external devices, potentially enabling individuals to control technology with their thoughts and access information directly from the internet.
