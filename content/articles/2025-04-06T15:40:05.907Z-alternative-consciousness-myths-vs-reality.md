## What is Alternative Consciousness? Defining Non-Ordinary States

Alternative consciousness, also known as altered states of consciousness or non-ordinary states of consciousness, refers to any state of mind that deviates significantly from normal waking consciousness. This can encompass a wide range of experiences, from the subtle shifts in awareness experienced during meditation to the more profound and transformative experiences associated with psychedelic substances or near-death experiences.

It's crucial to understand that "alternative" doesn't necessarily imply "better" or "worse," but simply *different*. These states offer unique perspectives, insights, and experiences that can be valuable for personal growth, creativity, and spiritual exploration. The key is to approach these states with awareness, intention, and respect.

## Meditation for Altered States: A Practical Guide

Meditation is a safe and accessible gateway to alternative consciousness. Here's a practical guide to get you started:

**Step 1:** Find a quiet and comfortable space where you won't be disturbed.
**Step 2:** Sit in a comfortable posture, either on a cushion or in a chair, with your spine straight but relaxed.
**Step 3:** Close your eyes gently and bring your awareness to your breath. Notice the sensation of the air entering and leaving your body.
**Step 4:** When your mind wanders (and it will!), gently redirect your attention back to your breath. Don't judge yourself for getting distracted; simply acknowledge the thought and let it go.
**Step 5:** Start with just 5-10 minutes of meditation per day and gradually increase the duration as you become more comfortable.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Experiment with different types of meditation to find what resonates best with you. Guided meditations can be particularly helpful for beginners. Apps like Headspace and Calm offer a wide variety of guided meditations.

## Lucid Dreaming and Alternative Consciousness: Controlling Your Inner World

Lucid dreaming offers a unique opportunity to explore alternative realities from the comfort of your own mind. Here's how to increase your chances of having lucid dreams:

*   **Reality Checks:** Throughout the day, perform reality checks to question whether you are dreaming. For example, try pushing your finger through your palm or looking at a clock twice to see if the time changes.
*   **Dream Journaling:** Keep a dream journal by your bedside and record your dreams as soon as you wake up. This will help you become more aware of your dream patterns and increase your dream recall.
*   **Mnemonic Induction of Lucid Dreams (MILD):** Before falling asleep, repeat a phrase like "I will realize I am dreaming" and visualize yourself becoming lucid in a dream.
*   **Wake-Back-to-Bed (WBTB):** Set an alarm for a few hours after you fall asleep. When you wake up, stay awake for 30-60 minutes, then go back to sleep while focusing on your intention to have a lucid dream.

⚡ **Lucid Dreaming Tip:** Once you become lucid, try to stabilize the dream by focusing on your senses. Rub your hands together, look closely at objects, or ground yourself in the environment.
