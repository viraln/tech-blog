## What is Digital Genetic Drift?

Digital genetic drift mirrors the biological process of genetic drift, where random fluctuations in gene frequencies alter the genetic makeup of a population. In the digital realm, this translates to the random propagation of certain digital "genes"—bits of code, algorithms, or data structures—within a system, regardless of their adaptive value.  Think of it as a digital roulette wheel influencing the evolution of software and algorithms. ![A stylized image depicting a digital roulette wheel with binary code instead of numbers](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1526374965328-7f61d4dc18c5?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
