## What is Fractal Learning?

Fractal learning mimics the patterns found in nature – fractals.  These are self-similar patterns that repeat at different scales.  Think of a fern: each tiny frond mirrors the structure of the entire plant.  Similarly, fractal learning involves breaking down complex subjects into smaller, manageable units, each containing the essence of the whole.  This allows for deep understanding and mastery at multiple levels, fostering true expertise. ## Fractal Learning Techniques and Strategies

Several techniques facilitate fractal learning:

2. **Break Down the Subject:** Divide the subject into smaller, self-similar modules.
3. **Explore Connections:**  Identify the relationships between these modules.
4. **Iterative Learning:**  Cycle through the modules, deepening your understanding with each iteration.
