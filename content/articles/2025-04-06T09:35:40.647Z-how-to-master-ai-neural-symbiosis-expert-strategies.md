## Navigating the Landscape: Types of Neural AI Interfaces

Neural AI Interfaces come in various forms, each with its own strengths and limitations. Understanding these differences is essential for navigating the landscape of AI Neural Symbiosis.

Here's a breakdown of the main types:

*   **Non-Invasive BCIs:** These interfaces, such as EEG (electroencephalography) caps, measure brain activity from the scalp. They are relatively safe and easy to use, but offer limited spatial resolution and signal quality. They are mostly used for research and basic control applications.
*   **Minimally Invasive BCIs:** These interfaces involve implanting electrodes just beneath the skull or on the surface of the brain. They offer better signal quality than non-invasive BCIs, but still carry some risk of infection and tissue damage.
*   **Invasive BCIs:** These interfaces involve implanting electrodes directly into the brain tissue. They provide the highest signal quality and spatial resolution, allowing for precise control and stimulation of specific brain regions. However, they also carry the highest risk of complications. The Neural Implant AI is at the forefront of this category.

The choice of interface depends on the specific application and the level of invasiveness that is acceptable. As technology advances, we can expect to see the development of less invasive and more effective interfaces.
