## The Theoretical Landscape: Quantum Entanglement and Consciousness

One of the most intriguing areas linking action at a distance mind to established science is the exploration of quantum entanglement. Quantum entanglement describes a phenomenon where two or more particles become linked in such a way that they share the same fate, no matter how far apart they are.
