## What is Symbiotic Technology?

Symbiotic technology refers to systems where humans and technology work together in a mutually beneficial relationship. Unlike traditional technology that operates independently, symbiotic technology is designed to be integrated, intuitive, and responsive to human needs. This integration can range from wearable devices that monitor our health to AI systems that anticipate our needs and assist in complex tasks.  The key is the seamless and natural interaction, fostering a collaborative partnership rather than a master-slave dynamic. ![A futuristic illustration depicting a human seamlessly interacting with technology](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1488590528505-98d2b5aba04b?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
