## Cost-Effective Timestream Conservation Strategies

Reducing Timestream storage costs involves a combination of techniques. Let's explore some key strategies:

* **Tiered Storage:**  Utilize Timestream's tiered storage options to store less frequently accessed data in a more cost-effective tier.
* **Data Sampling:**  For certain use cases, sampling your data can significantly reduce storage needs without sacrificing valuable insights.
* **Data Aggregation:**  Aggregate data at various levels to reduce granularity and storage requirements.
* **Automated Data Expiration:**  Implement automated policies to automatically delete or archive data that has reached its retention period.
