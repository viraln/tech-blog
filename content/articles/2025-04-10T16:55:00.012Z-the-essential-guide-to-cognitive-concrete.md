## What is Cognitive Concrete?

Cognitive concrete, unlike traditional concrete, incorporates embedded sensors and actuators within its matrix.  These intelligent components allow the material to monitor its own structural integrity, environmental conditions (temperature, humidity, stress levels), and even detect potential damage. ![Microscopic image of cognitive concrete showing embedded sensors](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1622137276463-5d2b374cad11?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

Furthermore, some advanced formulations include self-healing capabilities, where micro-capsules containing repair agents are activated upon crack detection, autonomously mending minor damage.
