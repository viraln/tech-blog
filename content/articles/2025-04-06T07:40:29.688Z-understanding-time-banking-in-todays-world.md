## What is Time Banking and How Does it Work?

Time banking, at its core, is a reciprocal service exchange system. Instead of using traditional money, participants exchange services and skills, earning "time credits" for each hour of service provided. These credits can then be used to "purchase" services from other members within the network. It's a peer-to-peer system built on the principles of equality and community collaboration. ![Time Banking visualization](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1434030216411-0b793f4b4173?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
