## Understanding the Core Components of Cognitive Structures

Cognitive structures are the mental frameworks we use to organize, interpret, and make sense of information. These structures aren't static; they evolve and adapt throughout our lives.  Key components include:

* 🔑 **Schemas:** Mental representations of concepts, events, and situations.
* ⚡ **Mental Models:** Simplified representations of complex systems, used to predict behavior and make decisions.
* ✅ **Belief Systems:** Deeply held convictions that influence our perceptions and actions.
* 🧠 **Cognitive Biases:** Systematic errors in thinking that can distort our judgment.
