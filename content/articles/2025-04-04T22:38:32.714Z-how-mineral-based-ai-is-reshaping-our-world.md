## What is Mineral-Based AI? Unveiling the Fundamentals

Mineral-based AI, or "Mineral AI," refers to the development and utilization of artificial intelligence systems that leverage minerals as the primary computational substrate. Instead of relying solely on silicon-based chips, Mineral AI explores the potential of minerals with unique electrical, optical, and magnetic properties to perform computational tasks.

This approach can manifest in several ways:

*   **Mineral-enhanced silicon chips:** Integrating specific minerals into existing chip designs to improve performance and energy efficiency.
*   **Mineral-based memristors and neuromorphic computing:** Using minerals to create artificial synapses and neurons, mimicking the structure and function of the human brain. This allows for more energy-efficient and parallel processing.
*   **Mineral-based sensors and data storage:** Developing sensors and storage devices that utilize the unique properties of minerals to detect and process information.
