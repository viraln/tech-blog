## 1. Taming Gravity: Graviton Field Manipulation for Advanced Propulsion

At its core, Graviton Engineering revolves around the manipulation of gravitons, the hypothetical elementary particles that mediate the force of gravity. While direct detection of gravitons remains elusive, indirect methods and theoretical models are providing increasingly detailed insights into their behavior and interaction with spacetime. The most tantalizing application of this knowledge lies in **Graviton Propulsion Systems**.

Traditional propulsion relies on expelling mass to generate thrust, a process inherently limited by fuel requirements and exhaust velocity. Graviton Propulsion, however, aims to directly manipulate the gravitational field around a spacecraft, creating a "warp bubble" or localized distortion of spacetime.
