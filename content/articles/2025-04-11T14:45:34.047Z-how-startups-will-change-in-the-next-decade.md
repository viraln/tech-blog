## Funding for Startups: Beyond Venture Capital

While venture capital (VC) will remain a significant funding source, startups will increasingly explore alternative funding options:

* **Crowdfunding:** Platforms like Kickstarter and Indiegogo will continue to grow in popularity, allowing startups to directly engage with their potential customer base and secure early funding.
* **Angel Investors:**  High-net-worth individuals will continue to play a crucial role in providing seed funding and mentorship to promising startups.
* **Government Grants and Incubators:**  Government initiatives and incubator programs will provide crucial support to startups, particularly those in high-growth sectors.
* **Decentralized Finance (DeFi):**  Startups will explore blockchain-based financing solutions, offering new avenues for raising capital.
