## What is Cryptocurrency?

At its core, cryptocurrency is a digital or virtual currency designed to work as a medium of exchange.  Unlike traditional currencies issued and controlled by central banks, cryptocurrencies operate on a decentralized technology known as blockchain.  This means no single entity controls it; instead, transactions are verified and recorded across a distributed network of computers. This decentralization is a key feature, offering potential benefits like increased security and transparency. ![Illustration of a blockchain network](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1518546305927-5a555bb7020d?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

> **Did you know?** The first cryptocurrency, Bitcoin, was created in 2009 by an anonymous individual or group known as Satoshi Nakamoto.

Cryptocurrencies utilize cryptography to secure and verify transactions, ensuring their integrity and preventing fraud.  Each transaction is encrypted and added to the blockchain, creating a permanent and auditable record. This process makes it incredibly difficult to alter or counterfeit transactions.

## Cryptocurrency Mining Explained

Cryptocurrency mining is the process of verifying and adding transactions to the blockchain.  Miners use powerful computers to solve complex mathematical problems, and the first to solve the problem gets to add the next block of transactions to the blockchain and receives a reward in cryptocurrency. ![Illustration of cryptocurrency mining process](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1621761191319-c6fb62004040?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

This process is crucial for maintaining the security and integrity of the blockchain network.  However, it's also energy-intensive, leading to environmental concerns.  The increasing popularity of proof-of-stake consensus mechanisms, which require less energy than proof-of-work, addresses this issue.
