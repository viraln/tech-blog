Our perception of time is surprisingly malleable. It's influenced by a multitude of factors, including:

*   **Attention Span:** Focused attention can make time seem to fly, while boredom can make it drag.
*   **Biological Rhythms:** Our circadian rhythms influence our energy levels and perception of time throughout the day.
*   **External Stimuli:** The constant barrage of notifications, advertisements, and deadlines can significantly alter our sense of time.

Manufacturers of temporality leverage these vulnerabilities to influence our behavior.
