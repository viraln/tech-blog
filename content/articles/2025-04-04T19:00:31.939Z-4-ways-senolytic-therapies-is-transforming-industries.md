## 1. The Science Behind Senescence: Understanding the Cellular Basis of Aging

Before we can understand how senolytic therapies are transforming industries, we need to understand *cellular senescence*. It's a fundamental process linked to aging and age-related diseases.

Senescence occurs when cells stop dividing but don't die. These "zombie cells," as they're sometimes called, accumulate with age and release harmful substances that damage surrounding tissues and promote inflammation. This chronic inflammation contributes to a wide range of age-related conditions, including:

*   Arthritis
*   Cardiovascular disease
*   Neurodegenerative diseases (like Alzheimer's)
*   Type 2 diabetes
*   Cancer
