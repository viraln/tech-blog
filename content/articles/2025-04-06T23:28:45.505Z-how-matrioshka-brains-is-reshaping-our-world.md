## Understanding the Matrioshka Brain Concept: A Stellar-Sized Computer

A Matrioshka Brain is a hypothetical megastructure – a massive artificial structure built on an astronomical scale. It's essentially a sophisticated form of a Dyson sphere, designed not just to capture a star's energy but to use that energy to power an incredibly powerful and efficient computer. The name "Matrioshka" comes from the Russian nesting dolls, reflecting the idea of multiple layers of structures built around a star. Each layer captures and utilizes a portion of the star's energy, with the waste heat from one layer becoming the energy source for the next.
