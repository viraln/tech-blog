## The Core Principles: Building Blocks of Murmuration Algorithms

Understanding the underlying principles of bird murmurations is crucial for effectively applying them to engineering problems. Key principles include:

*   **Separation:** Individuals avoid collisions with their neighbors. This prevents clumping and ensures the flock maintains a cohesive structure.
*   **Alignment:** Individuals align their direction and speed with their neighbors. This promotes coordinated movement and a sense of unity.
*   **Cohesion:** Individuals tend to move towards the center of the local group. This maintains the flock's overall integrity and prevents fragmentation.

These three simple rules, when applied iteratively to a population of agents, can produce complex and emergent behaviors. Furthermore, real-world murmurations also incorporate elements of:

*   **Predator Avoidance:** Collective responses to potential threats.
*   **Resource Seeking:** Coordinated searching for food or other resources.
