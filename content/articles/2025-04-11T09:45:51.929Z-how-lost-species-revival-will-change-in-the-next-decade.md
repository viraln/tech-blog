##  ## The Technological Leap Forward: Beyond Cloning

The early 2020s saw a focus on cloning as the primary method for de-extinction. While still relevant, the next decade will witness a shift towards more sophisticated techniques.  Genome editing tools like CRISPR-Cas9 are refining the process, allowing scientists to meticulously reconstruct extinct animal genomes from fragmented DNA extracted from ancient remains. This precision minimizes the reliance on closely related extant species as surrogates, enhancing the accuracy of the resurrected species.  Furthermore, advancements in stem cell technology and artificial wombs are paving the way for more successful pregnancies and births of cloned animals.
