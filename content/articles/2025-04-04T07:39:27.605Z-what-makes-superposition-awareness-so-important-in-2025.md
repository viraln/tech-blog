## Understanding Superposition: Quantum Superposition Explained

At the heart of quantum mechanics lies the principle of superposition. Simply put, a quantum system, like an electron, can exist in multiple states *simultaneously* until measured. Think of it like a coin spinning in the air: it's neither heads nor tails until it lands. This "both/and" state is superposition. Quantum superposition explained, therefore, isn't about multiple realities existing; it's about the *potential* for multiple realities until a definitive choice is made.
