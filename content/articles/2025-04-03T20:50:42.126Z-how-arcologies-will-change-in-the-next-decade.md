## The Arcology Concept: A Brief Overview

Arcologies, a portmanteau of "architecture" and "ecology," are massive, three-dimensional urban structures designed to house entire communities. The core principle behind arcology design is to minimize environmental impact by concentrating human activity within a compact, self-sufficient ecosystem. This reduces land consumption, minimizes transportation needs, and promotes resource conservation. The concept was pioneered by architect Paolo Soleri, who envisioned arcologies as a way to create more sustainable and harmonious living environments.
