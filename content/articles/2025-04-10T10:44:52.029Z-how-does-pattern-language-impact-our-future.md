## What is Pattern Language?

Pattern language, pioneered by Christopher Alexander in his seminal work, *A Pattern Language: Towns, Buildings, Construction* (which you can *buy pattern language book* at most online retailers), is a design methodology that emphasizes the interconnectedness of elements within a system.  Instead of focusing on isolated components, pattern language identifies recurring solutions to common design problems – patterns – and organizes them into a coherent, interconnected system.  These patterns aren't rigid blueprints but rather flexible guidelines, adaptable to specific contexts and cultural nuances.  Think of it as a recipe book for building, not a set of strict instructions.  It encourages iterative design and collaborative problem-solving.  Understanding *what is pattern language* is the first step to harnessing its power.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  Don't view pattern language as a rigid methodology. It's a flexible framework for thinking about design, adaptable to diverse contexts.
