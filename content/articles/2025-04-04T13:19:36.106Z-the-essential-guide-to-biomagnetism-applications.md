## Biomagnetic Pair Therapy (BMP): A Closer Look

Biomagnetic Pair Therapy (BMP) is a specific type of biomagnetism application developed by Dr. Isaac Goiz Durán. BMP involves identifying "biomagnetic pairs" – two specific points on the body that are in energetic imbalance. These imbalances are believed to be caused by the presence of pathogens, toxins, or other stressors.

The therapist uses kinesiology (muscle testing) to identify these pairs. Once identified, magnets of opposite polarities (north and south) are placed on these points for a period of time, usually 15-30 minutes. The goal is to neutralize the pH imbalance and eliminate the underlying cause of the condition.
