## Foglet Swarm Prevention Tips: Proactive Measures

Prevention is always better than cure. Here are some proactive measures you can take to prevent foglet swarms from forming on your property:

1.  **Eliminate Standing Water:** Foglets breed in standing water. Eliminate potential breeding sites by draining puddles, emptying containers, and cleaning gutters.
2.  **Maintain Vegetation:** Keep vegetation trimmed and well-maintained. Overgrown vegetation provides shelter and breeding grounds for foglets.
3.  **Use Insect Screens:** Install screens on windows and doors to prevent foglets from entering your home.
4.  **Install Fans:** Foglets are weak fliers. Installing fans can disrupt their flight patterns and discourage them from swarming in certain areas.
5.  **Use Insect Traps:** Insect traps can help reduce foglet populations.
6.  **Introduce Natural Predators:** Encourage the presence of natural predators, such as birds and bats, by providing them with habitat and food sources.
7.  **Consider Professional Treatment:** In severe cases, professional pest control services may be necessary.
