## 2. Enhancing Model Reliability and Reducing Errors: Trustworthy AI

One of the biggest challenges with ML models is ensuring their reliability and accuracy over time.  Data drift, concept drift, and unforeseen edge cases can lead to inaccurate predictions and even catastrophic failures. MLOps addresses this through robust monitoring and feedback loops.  ![A diagram illustrating MLOps monitoring and feedback mechanisms](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1484417894907-623942c8ee29?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

* 🔑 **Automated Monitoring:**  MLOps tools continuously monitor model performance, flagging anomalies and potential issues.
* ⚡ **Real-time Alerts:**  Teams receive immediate alerts when performance drops below a predefined threshold.
* ✅ **Automated Retraining:**  Models can be automatically retrained with new data to maintain accuracy.
