## What Exactly *Is* the Spatial Internet?

The Spatial Internet, often discussed in conjunction with the Metaverse, is the next evolution of the internet, shifting from a two-dimensional experience to a three-dimensional, immersive environment. It's built upon technologies like Augmented Reality (AR), Virtual Reality (VR), and Mixed Reality (MR), along with the AR Cloud, Digital Twins, and advancements in spatial computing.

Unlike the traditional internet, which primarily exists on screens, the Spatial Internet anchors digital content to physical locations and objects. Think of it as adding a digital layer onto the real world, accessible through devices like smartphones, AR glasses, and VR headsets.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Understanding the difference between the Metaverse and the Spatial Internet is crucial. The Metaverse is a *place*, a digital world. The Spatial Internet is the *technology* that enables immersive experiences and connects digital information to the physical world, and it is a key component of the Metaverse vision.

How does the Spatial Internet work? At its core, the Spatial Internet relies on:

*   **Spatial Computing:** This involves understanding and interacting with the physical world through digital devices. It enables devices to perceive their surroundings, understand spatial relationships, and respond accordingly.
*   **Augmented Reality (AR):** Overlays digital information onto the real world, enhancing our perception of reality.
*   **Digital Twins:** Virtual representations of physical objects or systems, allowing for remote monitoring, analysis, and control.
*   **AR Cloud:** A persistent, shared digital replica of the real world, enabling seamless AR experiences across multiple devices and users.
*   **Sensor Technology:** Data from GPS, cameras, and other sensors is used to understand the environment and the user's place within it.
