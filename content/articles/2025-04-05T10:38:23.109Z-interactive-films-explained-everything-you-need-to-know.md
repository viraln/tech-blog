## What Are Interactive Films? Defining the Genre

Interactive films, also known as choose your own adventure movies or interactive movie games, are films that allow viewers to make choices that affect the storyline and outcome. Unlike traditional linear movies, interactive films offer branching narratives, where the plot progresses based on the viewer's decisions. These choices can range from simple yes/no questions to more complex moral dilemmas, each leading to different scenes, outcomes, and even multiple endings. The core concept is to provide a more engaging and immersive viewing experience, blurring the lines between passive observation and active participation.

## A Brief History of Interactive Storytelling

The concept of interactive storytelling predates cinema itself. Think about the "choose your own adventure" books that gained popularity in the 1980s. These books allowed readers to make choices at the end of each chapter, leading them to different sections of the book and ultimately shaping their own unique story.

Interactive films are a natural extension of this concept. The first attempts at interactive cinema appeared in the early days of DVD technology, with limited branching options. However, the rise of streaming platforms and advancements in digital technology have paved the way for more sophisticated and ambitious interactive films.

Netflix has been a major player in popularizing the genre, with titles like *Bandersnatch* (from the *Black Mirror* series) and *Unbreakable Kimmy Schmidt: Kimmy vs the Reverend* gaining widespread attention and critical acclaim. These projects demonstrated the potential of interactive storytelling and opened the door for other platforms and filmmakers to experiment with the format. In 2025, we are seeing even more platforms, like YouTube and Vimeo, incorporating interactive features into their video players.

## How to Make an Interactive Film: A Practical Guide

Creating an interactive film requires careful planning and execution. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you get started:

**Step 1:** **Develop a Compelling Story:** Start with a strong narrative that lends itself to branching storylines. Consider the themes, characters, and potential conflicts that can be explored through viewer choices.

**Step 2:** **Map Out the Narrative Branches:** Create a detailed flowchart or diagram that outlines all the possible paths and outcomes based on the viewer's decisions. This will help you visualize the structure of the interactive film and ensure that all branches are logically connected.

**Step 3:** **Write the Script:** Write the script for each scene, taking into account the different choices and outcomes. Be sure to include clear instructions for the actors and crew on how to handle the interactive elements.

**Step 4:** **Shoot the Film:** Film each scene, ensuring that you capture all the necessary footage for each branch of the narrative. This may require shooting multiple versions of the same scene with different outcomes.

**Step 5:** **Edit the Film:** Edit the footage together, creating a seamless and engaging interactive experience. Use editing software that supports branching narratives and interactive elements.

**Step 6:** **Choose an Interactive Film Platform:** Select a platform that supports interactive video. Options include Netflix (for larger productions), Eko, Vimeo, and specialized interactive film platforms.

**Step 7:** **Test and Refine:** Thoroughly test the interactive film to ensure that all the choices and outcomes work as intended. Gather feedback from viewers and make any necessary adjustments to improve the experience.
