## Mythbusting: Common Misconceptions about Intelligence Propagation

Many believe propagating intelligence is simply about throwing data at the wall and hoping something sticks. This is a dangerous misconception. Let's debunk some common myths:

**Myth 1: More data equals better intelligence.**  Quantity doesn't equate to quality.  Overwhelming recipients with irrelevant information can be counterproductive.

**Myth 2:  Technology alone solves the problem.**  While technology plays a crucial role, human analysis, interpretation, and contextualization remain essential.

**Myth 3:  Once shared, intelligence is automatically acted upon.**  Passive dissemination is ineffective.  Active engagement and feedback loops are crucial.
