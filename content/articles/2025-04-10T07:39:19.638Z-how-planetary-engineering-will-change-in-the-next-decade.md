## Defining Planetary Engineering: Beyond Geoengineering

Planetary engineering, often confused with geoengineering, encompasses a far broader scope.  While geoengineering primarily focuses on Earth's climate manipulation, planetary engineering tackles the manipulation and modification of planetary systems—including Earth—to achieve specific goals. This might involve terraforming Mars, mitigating asteroid impacts, or even creating artificial habitats in space. ![A wide shot depicting various planetary engineering concepts, including terraforming Mars and asteroid mining](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1581092583537-20d51b4b4f1b?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## The Expanding Landscape of Planetary Engineering Projects

The next decade will see a surge in ambitious planetary engineering projects.  Several initiatives are already underway:

* **Carbon Capture and Storage (CCS) advancements:**  Beyond simple sequestration, we'll see increased exploration of direct air capture (DAC) technologies with enhanced efficiency and scalability.  
* **Space-based solar power (SBSP):**  Research and development will accelerate, aiming for economically viable and environmentally friendly energy solutions.
* **Asteroid mining:**  Companies are actively pursuing the extraction of valuable resources from asteroids, a crucial step toward sustainable space exploration and resource management.
* **Lunar resource utilization (ISRU):**  The Moon will become a key testing ground for in-situ resource utilization, crucial for establishing self-sustaining lunar bases and supporting future deep-space missions.
