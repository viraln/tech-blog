## Risks and Ethical Considerations of Body Augmentation Collectives

While the benefits of body augmentation collectives are significant, it's crucial to acknowledge the potential risks.  DIY collectives, in particular, pose significant health risks due to the potential for infection, improper technique, and the lack of sterile environments.  Other risks include:

* **Infection:** Unsanitary practices can lead to serious infections.
* **Improper Technique:** Lack of training can result in botched procedures, scarring, and other complications.
* **Allergic Reactions:**  Reactions to pigments, materials, or implants are possible.
* **Long-Term Health Complications:** Some modifications may have long-term health consequences that are not fully understood.
* **Social Stigma:**  Certain body modifications may still face societal stigma and discrimination.

> **DID YOU KNOW?**  The rise of biohacking and citizen science is influencing the DIY body modification movement, pushing the boundaries of self-experimentation and raising ethical questions about informed consent and long-term health consequences.
