## Common Sleep Disorders and Their Treatments

Many people struggle with sleep disorders, including insomnia, sleep apnea, and restless legs syndrome.

* **Insomnia:** Difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or experiencing non-restorative sleep.  Treatment options include cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I), relaxation techniques, and in some cases, medication.
* **Sleep Apnea:**  Characterized by pauses in breathing during sleep.  Treatment often involves a CPAP (continuous positive airway pressure) machine or oral appliances.
* **Restless Legs Syndrome:**  An overwhelming urge to move the legs, often accompanied by uncomfortable sensations.  Treatment may include medication, lifestyle changes, and iron supplements.

Seeking professional help is crucial if you suspect a sleep disorder.  A sleep study can accurately diagnose the problem and guide treatment.
