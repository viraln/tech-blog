## 1. The Foundation: Understanding Spacetime Fabric

Before we discuss processing, it's vital to grasp what "spacetime fabric" actually *is*. This isn't just science fiction; it's a cornerstone of Einstein's theory of General Relativity. Spacetime is the four-dimensional continuum that combines the three spatial dimensions (length, width, height) with time. Mass and energy warp this fabric, causing what we perceive as gravity.

Think of it like a trampoline. A bowling ball placed on the trampoline creates a dip. This dip represents the curvature of spacetime caused by a massive object. A marble rolling nearby will be drawn towards the bowling ball, mimicking gravity.

*   **Key Concept:** General Relativity dictates that gravity isn't a force, but a curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy.
