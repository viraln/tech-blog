## 4.  Virtual Reality vs. Synthetic Reality: Key Differences

While often confused, VR and synthetic reality are distinct:

| Feature          | Virtual Reality (VR)                               | Synthetic Reality                                      |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| **Immersion**     | High, but limited interaction with the physical world | Extremely high, with seamless physical-digital blending |
| **Interaction**   | Primarily through controllers and headsets          | Through controllers, headsets, and potentially BCIs, plus physical interaction |
| **Realism**       | High, but often lacks physical interaction realism | Aims for photorealism and realistic physical interaction |
| **Applications**   | Gaming, entertainment, training                    | Broader applications across multiple industries        |
