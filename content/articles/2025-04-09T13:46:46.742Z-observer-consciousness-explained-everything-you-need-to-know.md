## Observer Effect in Quantum Physics

The observer effect is most famously demonstrated in the double-slit experiment.  When electrons are fired at a screen with two slits, they create an interference pattern, behaving like waves. However, when we attempt to *observe* which slit each electron passes through, the interference pattern collapses, and the electrons behave like particles. This implies that the act of observation fundamentally alters the outcome.  ![A diagram illustrating the double-slit experiment](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1635497611324-129442752063?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
