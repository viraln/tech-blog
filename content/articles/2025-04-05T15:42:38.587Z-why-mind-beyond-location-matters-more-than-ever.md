## 5. Remote Viewing Training: A Deeper Dive

Remote viewing, as mentioned, is a specific technique used to attempt to perceive distant locations or objects. It has gained significant attention due to its use by the U.S. military. Here's a more in-depth look at remote viewing training:

*   **Controlled Remote Viewing (CRV):** This is a structured protocol developed by Ingo Swann and Harold Puthoff for the Stargate Project. CRV involves a series of steps designed to systematically access information about a target.
*   **Associative Remote Viewing (ARV):** This technique uses remote viewing to predict future events, such as stock market fluctuations or sporting events. ARV involves associating different targets with different outcomes and using remote viewing to determine which target is most likely to be associated with the desired outcome.
