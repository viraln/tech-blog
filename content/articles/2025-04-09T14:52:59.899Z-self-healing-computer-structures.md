## Mechanisms of Self-Healing: From Redundancy to Adaptation

Several key mechanisms underpin self-healing capabilities.  Redundancy is foundational, providing backup components to take over when primary systems fail.  However, self-healing goes beyond simple backups.  It incorporates:

* 🔑 **Automatic Error Correction:**  Sophisticated algorithms detect and correct errors in data and code in real-time.
* ⚡ **Dynamic Resource Allocation:**  Systems automatically adjust resource allocation based on demand and system health.
* ✅ **Adaptive Reconfiguration:**  Components can be reconfigured or replaced on the fly to maintain functionality.
* 🔄 **Self-Diagnosis & Repair:**  Systems can diagnose their own problems and initiate repair processes automatically.
