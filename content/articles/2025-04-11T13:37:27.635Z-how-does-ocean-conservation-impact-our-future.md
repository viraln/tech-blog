## The Pressing Threats Facing Our Oceans in 2025

The challenges facing our oceans are multifaceted and interconnected.  Ocean acidification, driven by increased carbon dioxide absorption, threatens marine organisms with calcium carbonate shells and skeletons.  Plastic pollution, a pervasive problem, continues to accumulate in our oceans, harming wildlife through entanglement and ingestion.  Overfishing depletes fish stocks, disrupting marine ecosystems and threatening food security.  ![A powerful image showcasing the devastating effects of plastic pollution on marine life](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1600418725276-a399595aac45?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
