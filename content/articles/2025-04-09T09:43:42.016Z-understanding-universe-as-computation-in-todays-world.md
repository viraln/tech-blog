## What is the Universe as Computation Theory?

The "Universe as Computation" theory, also known as digital physics or the computational universe hypothesis, proposes that the universe itself is fundamentally a computational process.  Instead of being governed by continuous, deterministic laws as classical physics suggests, reality is viewed as a massive, incredibly complex computation, akin to a gigantic computer program running on a substrate we don't yet fully understand.  This doesn't necessarily imply a conscious programmer or a simulated reality, although that's a popular interpretation (the Simulation Hypothesis).  Instead, it suggests that the fundamental laws of physics can be expressed as computational algorithms, and the universe's evolution is the execution of those algorithms. ![A stylized representation of data streams flowing through a complex network](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1444703686981-a3abbc4d4fe3?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
