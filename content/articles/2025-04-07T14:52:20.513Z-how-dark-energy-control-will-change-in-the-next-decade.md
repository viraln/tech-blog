## Understanding the Enigma: What is Dark Energy?

Before we discuss controlling dark energy, it's crucial to understand what it *is*. Dark energy, currently estimated to comprise roughly 68% of the universe's total energy density, is a mysterious force driving the accelerated expansion of the universe.
