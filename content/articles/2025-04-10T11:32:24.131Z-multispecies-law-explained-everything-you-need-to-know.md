## What is Multispecies Law? A Definition and Conceptual Framework

Multispecies law, at its core, is a legal framework that acknowledges the intrinsic rights and interests of non-human species. It moves beyond the traditional human-centric approach, recognizing the agency and moral standing of other living beings.
