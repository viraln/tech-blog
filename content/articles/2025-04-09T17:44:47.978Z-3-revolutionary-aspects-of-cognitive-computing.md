## Cognitive Computing vs. AI: Understanding the Nuances

While often used interchangeably, cognitive computing and artificial intelligence (AI) are distinct but related concepts.  AI is a broader field encompassing various techniques for creating intelligent systems. Cognitive computing is a *subset* of AI that focuses on simulating human cognitive processes, such as learning, reasoning, and problem-solving.
