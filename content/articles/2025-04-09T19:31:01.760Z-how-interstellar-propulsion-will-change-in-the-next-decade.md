##  The Current Landscape of Interstellar Propulsion

Currently, chemical rockets, while effective for near-Earth missions, are woefully inadequate for interstellar voyages.  The distances are simply too vast.  Travel times to even the nearest star systems using current technology would span millennia.  Therefore, the focus is shifting towards more advanced propulsion methods, including:

* **Nuclear Fusion:**  Harnessing the power of nuclear fusion, mimicking the energy source of stars, offers a significantly more energy-dense fuel source than chemical rockets.  ![Illustration of a fusion-powered spacecraft](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1633774082999-d914f7e73b5b?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
