## What are Physical Voxels? A Tangible Revolution

The term "voxel" is a portmanteau of "volumetric pixel." Just as a pixel represents a point in a 2D image, a voxel represents a value on a regular grid in three-dimensional space. While traditionally displayed on screens, physical voxels take this concept into the real world. They are tangible, physical blocks that can be arranged to create three-dimensional objects and displays. These blocks can be made from various materials, including plastic, wood, and even smart materials that can change color or shape. The ability to hold, manipulate, and interact with these voxel-based physical models opens up entirely new possibilities.

## Voxel Modeling Physical: From Digital Design to Tangible Reality

Creating physical voxel models typically involves a combination of digital modeling and physical fabrication techniques. The process usually follows these steps:

1.  **Digital Voxel Modeling:** The first step is to create a 3D model using voxel-based software. Popular software options include MagicaVoxel, Goxel, and specialized CAD programs with voxel capabilities. This stage involves designing the object and assigning colors or properties to each voxel.
2.  **Slicing and Exporting:** Once the digital model is complete, it needs to be sliced into individual layers. This process divides the model into horizontal slices, each representing a layer of voxels to be fabricated. The sliced data is then exported in a format compatible with the chosen fabrication method.
3.  **Physical Fabrication:** The physical voxels can be created using various techniques, including:
    *   **3D Printing:** 3D printing is a common method for creating physical voxels. Each voxel is printed individually or in small batches and then assembled to form the complete model.
    *   **CNC Machining:** CNC machining can be used to carve voxels from larger blocks of material. This method is suitable for creating voxels from wood, metal, or plastic.
    *   **Injection Molding:** For mass production of physical voxels, injection molding is a cost-effective option. This process involves injecting molten plastic into molds to create the desired voxel shapes.
    *   **Manual Assembly:** In some cases, physical voxels can be created manually by cutting and assembling individual blocks of material. This method is suitable for small-scale projects and artistic applications.
4.  **Assembly and Finishing:** The final step involves assembling the individual voxels to create the complete model. This may involve gluing, snapping, or other connection methods. The model can then be finished with paint, coatings, or other decorative elements.
