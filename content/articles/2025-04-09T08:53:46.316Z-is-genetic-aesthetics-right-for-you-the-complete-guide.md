## Types of Genetic Tests for Beauty Enhancement

Several types of genetic tests are available, each focusing on different aspects of your aesthetic profile:

* **Comprehensive Genome Analysis:** This provides the most extensive data, covering a broad range of genes related to skin, hair, and aging.  It offers the most personalized insights but is typically more expensive.
* **Targeted Gene Panels:** These focus on specific genes associated with particular traits, such as collagen production or sun sensitivity.  They are more affordable but offer less comprehensive information.
* **Skin Microbiome Analysis:** While not strictly genetic testing, analyzing your skin's microbiome provides valuable information about its health and can inform skincare choices.

The choice depends on your budget and the specific areas you want to address.  Consult with a qualified genetic counselor or dermatologist to determine the best approach for your needs.
