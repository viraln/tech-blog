## Transfinite Numbers and Their Computation

Understanding transfinite numbers is crucial to grasping transfinite computation.  Cardinal numbers describe the size of sets, even infinite ones.  For instance, the cardinality of natural numbers (ℵ₀, aleph-null) represents the "size" of the set of all natural numbers. Ordinal numbers, on the other hand, describe the order of elements in a well-ordered set, extending beyond finite sequences into infinite ones.  Transfinite computation utilizes these numbers within algorithms, allowing for operations on infinite sequences and sets.  This requires entirely new computational paradigms, moving beyond traditional Turing machines.
