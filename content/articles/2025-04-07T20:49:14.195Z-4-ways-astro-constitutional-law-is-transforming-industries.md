## 1. Establishing a Legal Foundation for Space Resource Utilization

One of the most immediate and impactful areas where Astro-Constitutional Law is making waves is in the realm of space resource utilization. As we look beyond Earth for resources like water ice on the Moon or valuable minerals on asteroids, the question of who owns these resources and how they can be legally extracted becomes paramount. The Outer Space Treaty of 1967, while foundational, leaves many of these questions unanswered.

The core principle of the Outer Space Treaty is that "outer space, including the Moon and other celestial bodies, is not subject to national appropriation by claim of sovereignty, by means of use or occupation, or by any other means." However, this doesn't explicitly prohibit the *extraction* of resources.

This has sparked international debate and highlights the need for a globally recognized legal framework.
