## AI Postmortem Tools: A Look at the Landscape in 2025

The market for AI postmortem tools is rapidly evolving, with a growing number of vendors offering a wide range of solutions. Here are some key categories of tools and examples of what they offer:

Here's how the role of humans will evolve in the age of AI postmortems:

*   **Incident Managers:** Will focus on managing the overall incident response process, coordinating teams, and communicating with stakeholders. They will leverage AI insights to make informed decisions.

The human element will always be essential for critical thinking, contextual understanding, and ethical considerations.

## Improving AI Safety with Postmortems

Postmortems are not just about fixing bugs; they're about learning from mistakes and preventing future failures. In the context of AI, this is particularly important, as AI systems can be complex and unpredictable. Thorough postmortem analysis can help us understand:

*   **Why AI models make certain decisions:** Understanding the reasoning behind AI decisions is crucial for identifying and addressing potential biases or vulnerabilities.
*   **How AI systems interact with their environment:** AI systems can have unintended consequences when deployed in real-world environments. Postmortems can help us understand these interactions and mitigate risks.
*   **The limitations of AI models:** AI models are not perfect, and they can fail in unexpected ways. Postmortems can help us identify the limitations of AI models and develop strategies to overcome them.
*   **Ethical implications of AI systems:** AI systems can raise ethical concerns, such as bias, fairness, and privacy. Postmortems can help us identify and address these concerns.

By rigorously analyzing AI failures, we can build safer, more reliable, and more ethical AI systems.
