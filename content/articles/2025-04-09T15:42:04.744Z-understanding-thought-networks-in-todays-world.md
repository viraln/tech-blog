## What Are Thought Networks?

Thought networks represent a paradigm shift in how we approach knowledge sharing and idea generation.  They are dynamic, interconnected groups of individuals – experts, enthusiasts, and innovators – who collaborate and share ideas to solve complex problems or generate novel solutions.
