## Optimizing Your Cognizant Network Infrastructure

The foundation of any successful network strategy is a well-designed and optimized infrastructure. Cognizant network infrastructure solutions focus on building a reliable, scalable, and high-performing network that can support your business needs.

**Key Strategies for Infrastructure Optimization:**

1.  **Assess Your Current Infrastructure:** Conduct a thorough assessment of your existing network infrastructure to identify bottlenecks, vulnerabilities, and areas for improvement. This includes evaluating hardware, software, and network configurations.
2.  **Right-Size Your Infrastructure:** Ensure your network infrastructure is appropriately sized to meet your current and future needs. Avoid over-provisioning, which can lead to unnecessary costs, and under-provisioning, which can impact performance.
3.  **Implement Network Segmentation:** Divide your network into smaller, isolated segments to improve security and performance. This can help prevent the spread of malware and reduce the impact of network outages.
4.  **Leverage Software-Defined Networking (SDN):** Implement SDN to centralize network control and improve agility. SDN allows you to programmatically manage network resources, making it easier to adapt to changing business needs. Cognizant SD-WAN solutions are a critical component of this.
5.  **Embrace Cloud-Native Networking:** Adopt cloud-native networking principles to build a scalable and resilient network that can support your cloud applications. This includes using containerization, microservices, and serverless technologies.

✅ **Example:** A large retail chain used Cognizant network consulting to assess their existing network infrastructure. The assessment revealed that their network was over-provisioned in some areas and under-provisioned in others. By right-sizing their infrastructure and implementing network segmentation, they were able to improve performance, reduce costs, and enhance security.
