## What are Living Medicines?

Living medicines encompass a broad spectrum of therapeutic agents derived directly from living organisms, including plants, fungi, bacteria, and even certain animals.  Unlike synthetic pharmaceuticals, these medicines leverage the complex biochemical processes within these organisms to produce therapeutic effects. This includes traditional herbal remedies, probiotics for gut health, mycotherapy using medicinal mushrooms, and even phage therapy using viruses to combat bacterial infections. ![A diverse array of plants, mushrooms, and other organisms used in living medicine](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512867957657-38dbae50a35b?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
