## The Rebellion Against Right Angles: What is Non-Euclidean Design?

For centuries, Euclidean geometry, based on the work of the ancient Greek mathematician Euclid, has been the bedrock of architectural design. Think straight lines, parallel planes, and predictable angles. Non-Euclidean geometry, however, challenges these fundamental assumptions. It explores spaces where these rules simply don't apply. This includes curved spaces, hyperbolic planes, and geometries where the shortest distance between two points isn't necessarily a straight line.

🔑 **Key Concept:** Non-Euclidean geometry isn't about throwing out all the rules; it's about exploring a different set of rules that govern space and shape. It allows for distortions, curves, and connections that are impossible within the confines of Euclidean space.

This shift opens up exciting possibilities for architects and designers to create truly unique and immersive experiences. Instead of being limited by rigid structures, they can leverage the fluidity of Non-Euclidean geometry to craft spaces that defy expectations and spark the imagination.
