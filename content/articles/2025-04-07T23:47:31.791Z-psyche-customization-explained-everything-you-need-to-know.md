2.  **Emotional Regulation:** The ability to manage and modulate your emotions effectively. This doesn't mean suppressing or avoiding difficult emotions, but rather learning to experience them in a healthy and adaptive way.

4.  **Behavior Modification:** Changing your behaviors to align with your goals and values. This can involve setting specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) goals and implementing strategies to overcome obstacles.

6.  **Values Alignment:** Ensuring that your thoughts, emotions, and behaviors are congruent with your core values. This can lead to a greater sense of purpose, meaning, and fulfillment.
