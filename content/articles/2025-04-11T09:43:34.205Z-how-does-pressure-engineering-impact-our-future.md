Pressure engineering is the scientific discipline focused on the design, analysis, and safe operation of systems containing pressurized fluids (liquids or gases). ## Pressure Relief Valve Sizing: Ensuring Safety

Pressure relief valves (PRVs) are critical safety devices designed to protect pressure vessels and systems from overpressure.  Precise sizing of these valves is paramount; undersizing can lead to catastrophic failures, while oversizing can result in unnecessary costs and operational inefficiencies.  Calculations involve considering factors such as fluid properties, vessel volume, and allowable pressure limits.  ![A cutaway diagram of a pressure relief valve showing its internal components](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1612663155099-9dc5efc9c6da?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
