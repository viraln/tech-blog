## Existential Risk Assessment Methods

Assessing existential risks is a complex, multidisciplinary endeavor.  It requires drawing upon expertise from various fields, including science, technology, economics, political science, and philosophy.  Several methods are employed:

1. **Scenario Planning:**  This involves constructing plausible scenarios of future events, considering various factors and their potential interactions.
2. **Quantitative Risk Assessment:**  This uses statistical models and data to estimate the probability and potential impact of different risks.
3. **Expert Elicitation:**  Gathering insights from leading experts in relevant fields through surveys, interviews, and workshops.
4. **Bayesian Networks:**  These probabilistic graphical models can represent complex relationships between variables and update probabilities based on new evidence.
5. **Agent-Based Modeling:**  Simulating the behavior of individual agents (humans, organizations, systems) to understand the emergent properties of a system.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  No single method is perfect.  A robust existential risk assessment typically combines several approaches for a more comprehensive understanding.
