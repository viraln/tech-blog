## Structural DNA Origami: The Art of DNA Folding

DNA origami is a powerful technique within structural DNA nanotechnology. It involves folding a long single-stranded DNA molecule into a desired shape using a carefully designed set of short "staple" strands. This method allows for the creation of complex two- and three-dimensional structures with unprecedented control over their shape and functionality. ![Illustration of DNA origami, showcasing a complex shape formed by folding a long single strand](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1578496479914-7ef3b0193be3?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
