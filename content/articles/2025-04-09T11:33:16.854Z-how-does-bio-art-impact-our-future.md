## What is Bio Art? A Definition and Historical Overview

Bio art, also known as bio-art or life art, is an interdisciplinary art practice that uses living organisms and biological processes as its medium.  It's a fusion of art, science, and technology, blurring the lines between the natural and the artificial.  ![A collage showcasing diverse bio art pieces from different eras.](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1579187707643-35646d22b596?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## Bio Art Examples: A Diverse Landscape of Creation

The beauty of bio art lies in its incredible diversity.  It's not confined to a single style or technique.  We find artists working with:

* 🔑 **Genetic engineering:** Modifying the genes of organisms to create unique visual effects or functionalities.
* ⚡ **Tissue culture:** Growing and manipulating living tissues to create sculptures or installations.
* ✅ **Microbial art:** Using bacteria and fungi to create patterns and images on various surfaces.
