##  The Importance of Reproducibility and Transparency:  Open Science Practices

Reproducibility – the ability to replicate research findings – is crucial for ensuring the reliability and validity of research.  Open science practices, such as sharing data and code openly, are essential for promoting reproducibility and transparency.

* **Pre-registration of studies:**  Registering research plans before conducting the study to reduce publication bias and improve transparency.
* **Data sharing and repositories:**  Making research data publicly available to allow others to verify findings.
* **Open-source software:**  Using open-source software for data analysis and other research tasks to promote transparency and collaboration.
