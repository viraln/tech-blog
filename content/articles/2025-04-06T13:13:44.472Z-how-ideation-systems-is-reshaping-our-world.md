## Ideation Techniques for Product Development and Beyond

Ideation systems aren't just about software; they are about leveraging powerful techniques to spark creativity. Here are some proven methods:

*   **Brainstorming:** The classic approach – gather a group, set a clear objective, and encourage free-flowing ideas without judgment.
*   **Reverse Brainstorming:** Instead of focusing on solutions, identify potential problems or weaknesses with existing products or processes.
*   **SCAMPER:** A checklist that prompts you to Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to other uses, Eliminate, and Reverse elements of an existing product or service.
*   **Mind Mapping:** A visual technique that helps you explore different aspects of a problem or idea by branching out from a central topic.
*   **Six Thinking Hats:** A structured approach that encourages participants to consider a problem from six different perspectives (emotional, logical, creative, etc.).
*   **TRIZ (Theory of Inventive Problem Solving):** A systematic approach to problem-solving that identifies and resolves contradictions.
*   **Design Thinking:** A human-centered approach that focuses on understanding user needs and creating solutions that meet those needs.
