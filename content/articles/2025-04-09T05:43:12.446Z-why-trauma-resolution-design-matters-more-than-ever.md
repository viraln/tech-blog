##  Key Principles of Trauma Resolution Design: Creating Healing Spaces

Effective trauma-informed design isn't just about aesthetics; it's about creating environments that are inherently safe, predictable, and empowering. Key principles include:

* 🔑 **Safety and Security:**  Well-lit spaces, clear sightlines, and easily accessible exits reduce feelings of vulnerability.
* ⚡ **Control and Choice:**  Offering options for personalizing the environment (adjustable lighting, temperature control) empowers individuals.
* ✅ **Predictability and Routine:**  Consistent layouts, familiar objects, and regular routines promote a sense of stability.
* 🌿 **Natural Elements:**  Incorporating natural light, plants, and natural materials can reduce stress and promote relaxation. ![A picture of a calming, nature-inspired healing space](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1534670007418-fbb7f6cf32c3?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

##  Expert Tips and Recommendations for Designing Trauma-Informed Spaces
