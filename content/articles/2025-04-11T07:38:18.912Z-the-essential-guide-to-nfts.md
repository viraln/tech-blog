## What is an NFT? Understanding the NFT Meaning

At its core, an NFT (Non-Fungible Token) is a unique digital asset that represents ownership of something.  Unlike cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, which are fungible (interchangeable), each NFT is one-of-a-kind. Think of it like a digital certificate of authenticity for a piece of digital art, a collectible item, a piece of music, or even a virtual piece of real estate in the metaverse.  This uniqueness is verified and secured using blockchain technology, providing transparency and immutability.  The NFT meaning, therefore, boils down to verifiable ownership of a unique digital item.

> **DID YOU KNOW?** The first known NFT, a GIF called "Rain Drop (Dear Moon)", was sold in 2021 for $500,000, demonstrating the early potential of this technology.
