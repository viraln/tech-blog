## Understanding the Quantum Realm in Genetics

Quantum mechanics, the physics governing the subatomic world, introduces concepts like superposition (a particle existing in multiple states simultaneously) and entanglement (two particles linked regardless of distance).  These seemingly bizarre phenomena are now being investigated for their potential role in biological processes. While classical genetics focuses on the deterministic interactions of genes, quantum genetics explores the possibility that quantum effects influence gene expression, mutation, and even the very structure of DNA.  ![Microscopic image of DNA strands](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1579154392128-bf8c7ebee541?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## Applications of Quantum Mechanics in Genetics: Beyond Therapy

The applications of quantum mechanics in genetics extend far beyond gene therapy.  Researchers are exploring its role in:
