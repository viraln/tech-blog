## 1. What is Exponential Technology? Unpacking the Concept

Exponential technology isn't just about fast growth; it's about *accelerating* growth.  Think of a snowball rolling downhill – it starts small, but its size increases exponentially as it gathers more snow.  These technologies double in power or capability not linearly, but at an ever-increasing rate. This creates a disruptive force unlike anything seen before.

What distinguishes exponential technologies?  They often rely on network effects, meaning their value increases as more people use them.  Furthermore, they frequently involve self-improvement cycles, where the technology itself improves its performance over time.  This self-reinforcing loop is a hallmark of truly exponential growth.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Don't confuse exponential technology with simply "fast-growing" technology. The key differentiator is the accelerating rate of improvement, leading to dramatic, transformative impacts.
