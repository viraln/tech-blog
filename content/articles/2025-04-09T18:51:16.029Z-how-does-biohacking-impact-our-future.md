## What is Biohacking? A Comprehensive Overview

Biohacking, at its core, is the art and science of optimizing your biology to improve your physical and cognitive performance.  It's about understanding your body's intricate mechanisms and using that knowledge to enhance its capabilities.  This isn't about genetic engineering or radical surgeries (though those are elements of advanced biohacking); rather, it's about making informed choices regarding lifestyle, nutrition, supplementation, and technology to achieve peak performance.  From simple changes like improving sleep hygiene to utilizing cutting-edge technologies like neurofeedback, biohacking offers a spectrum of approaches for self-improvement.  This makes it accessible to beginners and experts alike, offering a personalized journey of self-discovery.

> **DID YOU KNOW?** The term "biohacking" was initially coined in the 1980s, referencing the early days of computer hacking, but its meaning has evolved to encompass the sophisticated methods we see today.

## Biohacking for Beginners: A Step-by-Step Guide

**Step 1:**  Track your baseline. Use a journal or app to monitor your sleep, energy levels, mood, and diet.
