## 1. What is Mutualistic Engineering?  A Deep Dive into Symbiotic Design

Mutualistic engineering, at its core, is the intentional design and implementation of systems that exploit mutualistic relationships—where two or more organisms benefit from their interaction.  Unlike traditional engineering that often views nature as a resource to be exploited, mutualistic engineering sees nature as a partner.  It's about fostering beneficial interactions between organisms, such as plants and microbes, to achieve a shared goal. This could involve anything from enhancing crop yields to creating self-healing materials.
