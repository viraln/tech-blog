## Understanding Nanobots: The Basics

Nanobots, also known as nanorobots, are microscopic machines typically measured in nanometers (one billionth of a meter).  They are engineered at the molecular level, often using advanced materials like carbon nanotubes and DNA.  Unlike the fantastical, self-replicating robots of science fiction, current nanobots are far simpler, focusing on specific tasks.  Their minuscule size allows them to access areas inaccessible to larger machines, offering unprecedented capabilities in various fields. ![Microscopic image of a nanobot prototype](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
