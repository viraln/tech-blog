## What is Planetary Engineering?  A Clear Definition

Planetary engineering, in its broadest sense, involves the application of engineering principles to manipulate planetary-scale systems.  This goes beyond traditional engineering disciplines, encompassing aspects of geology, climatology, biology, and even sociology to address global challenges.  It differs from geoengineering, which primarily focuses on Earth's climate, by encompassing a wider range of planetary-scale interventions, including terraforming other celestial bodies.  Think of it as the ultimate form of large-scale problem-solving, pushing the boundaries of human ingenuity to address issues impacting entire planets.  The field requires an interdisciplinary approach, drawing expertise from various scientific and technological domains.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  Understanding the nuances between planetary engineering and geoengineering is crucial. While geoengineering often focuses on mitigating climate change, planetary engineering takes a broader perspective, encompassing a wider range of planetary-scale modifications.
