## What is Transhumanism?

Transhumanism is a philosophical and intellectual movement that advocates for the use of technology to enhance human intellectual, physical, and psychological capacities.  It's not about replacing humanity but rather augmenting it, pushing the boundaries of what it means to be human.  This involves exploring and implementing technologies like genetic engineering, nanotechnology, artificial intelligence, and advanced computing to overcome limitations imposed by our biology.  ![A futuristic image depicting human augmentation technologies](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1707353004969-bddabf327b18?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## Transhumanism Ethics: Navigating the Moral Maze

The rapid advancements in transhumanist technologies raise significant ethical concerns:

## Transhumanism vs. Posthumanism: Understanding the Nuances

While often used interchangeably, transhumanism and posthumanism are distinct concepts. Transhumanism focuses on enhancing existing human capabilities, while posthumanism envisions a future where humans transcend their biological limitations entirely, potentially merging with technology to become something fundamentally different.
