Think of it as a global brain, where individual components contribute to a larger, more complex cognitive process. 🔑 **Key Components:**

*   **Distributed Data:** Data is generated and stored across numerous locations, creating a rich and diverse information landscape.
*   **Federated Learning:** AI models are trained on decentralized data without directly sharing the data itself, preserving privacy and security.
*   **Edge Computing:** Processing is moved closer to the data source, reducing latency and bandwidth requirements.
*   **Interoperability:** Different AI systems and platforms can communicate and collaborate seamlessly.
*   **Collective Intelligence Systems:** Combining human and artificial intelligence to solve complex problems.
