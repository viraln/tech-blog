## What is Artificial Life?

Artificial Life, or ALife, is an interdisciplinary field encompassing computer science, biology, physics, and chemistry.  It focuses on creating and studying artificial systems that exhibit characteristics of natural living systems.  This isn't about creating exact replicas of existing life forms, but rather about understanding the fundamental principles of life and replicating those principles in artificial systems.  These systems can exist as computer simulations, robotic entities, or even chemically synthesized organisms. ## Artificial Life vs. Artificial Intelligence: Key Differences

While both ALife and Artificial Intelligence (AI) are concerned with creating intelligent systems, their approaches and goals differ significantly.  AI focuses on creating systems that can perform specific tasks intelligently, often mimicking human cognitive abilities.  ALife, however, focuses on creating systems that exhibit characteristics of living organisms, regardless of whether they exhibit intelligence.

| Feature          | Artificial Life                               | Artificial Intelligence                             |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| **Goal**         | Understand and replicate life's principles     | Create intelligent systems capable of problem-solving |
| **Approach**     | Bottom-up, focusing on fundamental processes | Top-down, focusing on specific tasks and goals     |
| **Systems**      | Simulations, robots, synthetic organisms       | Software algorithms, neural networks                 |
