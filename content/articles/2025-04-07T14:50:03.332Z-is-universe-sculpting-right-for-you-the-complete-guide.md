## What Exactly is Universe Sculpting?

Universe Sculpting, at its core, refers to the theoretical and experimental endeavor of creating, simulating, or manipulating universes, or aspects thereof, within a controlled environment. This isn't about physically building a universe the size of our own. Instead, it involves using advanced technologies like powerful supercomputers, quantum computing, and novel materials to simulate conditions similar to the early universe, or to create miniature universes with different physical laws.
