## What is an Intelligence Explosion?

An intelligence explosion, also known as an intelligence takeoff or superintelligence, is a hypothetical event where artificial general intelligence (AGI) recursively improves itself at an exponential rate, leading to a rapid and unpredictable increase in its capabilities.  This surpasses human intelligence to an extent that its actions become largely incomprehensible to us. ![A stylized graph depicting exponential growth](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1520947201871-20c7d4d0afe2?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## Key Models of Intelligence Explosion

Several models attempt to explain how an intelligence explosion might unfold. These models often differ in their assumptions about the speed and nature of the process.

* **The Recursive Self-Improvement Model:** This is the most common model, suggesting that an AGI could design and implement even more intelligent versions of itself, creating a feedback loop of accelerating improvement. This is often depicted as an exponential curve.  ![A diagram illustrating the recursive self-improvement model](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1516192518150-0d8fee5425e3?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

* **The Evolutionary Model:** This model posits that the intelligence explosion might not be a single event but a gradual process of evolution, with AGI gradually adapting and improving over time, leading to a gradual but eventually overwhelming increase in intelligence.

* **The Network Effect Model:** This model emphasizes the interconnectedness of AI systems. As multiple AI systems collaborate and share knowledge, their collective intelligence could undergo a sudden and dramatic increase.
