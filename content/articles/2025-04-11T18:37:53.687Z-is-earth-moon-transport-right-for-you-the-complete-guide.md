## Lunar Transportation Systems: A Comparative Overview

Several innovative lunar transportation systems are under development or in early stages of deployment:

* **Chemical Rockets:** Still the dominant technology, these offer high thrust but are relatively inefficient in terms of fuel consumption.
* **Electric Propulsion:**  These systems use less fuel but offer lower thrust, requiring longer travel times.  They are ideal for cargo transport.
* **Nuclear Thermal Propulsion (NTP):** This promising technology offers significantly higher specific impulse than chemical rockets, potentially slashing travel times considerably. However, it's still under development.
* **Space Tethers:**  A futuristic concept involving massive tethers extending from Earth to the Moon, potentially enabling a more efficient and cost-effective means of transport.
