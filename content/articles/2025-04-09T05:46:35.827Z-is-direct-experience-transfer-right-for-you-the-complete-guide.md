## Understanding Direct Experience Transfer: A Clear Definition

Direct experience transfer (DET) refers to the systematic process of leveraging past experiences – both successes and failures – to improve future performance in similar or related situations. It's about consciously identifying transferable skills, knowledge, and insights from previous projects, roles, or even life experiences, and applying them to new contexts.  Unlike traditional training methods that often focus on theoretical knowledge, DET emphasizes practical application and real-world learning.  It's about recognizing patterns, adapting strategies, and accelerating learning through conscious reflection and application.  This isn't simply about remembering what you did; it's about understanding *why* you did it and how that knowledge can be repurposed.
