## What Are Living Computers? A Comprehensive Definition

Living computers leverage biological systems – cells, proteins, DNA, and even entire organisms – to perform computations. Unlike traditional silicon-based computers, these systems operate on different principles, offering unique advantages and limitations.  Instead of electricity, they use biochemical reactions as their computational engine.  This means they can process information in fundamentally different ways, potentially solving problems that are intractable for conventional computers.  The "living" aspect refers to the inherent self-organizing and self-repairing capabilities of biological systems, offering a resilience unseen in traditional hardware.

> **DID YOU KNOW?** Some scientists believe that the human brain, with its estimated 100 trillion connections, is the most complex living computer ever known!
