## Understanding the Fundamentals: What Are Temporal Fields?

At its core, a temporal field is a data field that explicitly represents a time period or point in time when a particular value is valid. Unlike traditional databases that typically store only the current value of an attribute, temporal databases track the history of changes over time. This allows us to ask questions like, "What was the price of this product on January 1, 2024?" or "When did this customer first become a member?"
