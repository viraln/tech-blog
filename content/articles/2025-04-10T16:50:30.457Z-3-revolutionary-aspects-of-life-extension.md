## 1.  The Rise of Senolytics: Targeting Senescent Cells

The accumulation of senescent cells – cells that have stopped dividing but don't die – is a significant contributor to aging.  These "zombie cells" release inflammatory molecules that damage surrounding tissues, accelerating age-related diseases.  Senolytics, a new class of drugs, are designed to selectively eliminate these harmful cells. ![Microscopic image of senescent cells](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1518895949257-7621c3c786d7?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
