## What are Fundamental Force APIs?

Fundamental Force APIs are software interfaces that allow developers to interact with models and simulations of the four fundamental forces of nature:

*   **Gravitational Force:** The force of attraction between objects with mass.
*   **Electromagnetic Force:** The force between electrically charged objects, responsible for electricity, magnetism, and light.

These APIs abstract away the complex mathematical equations and computational challenges involved in simulating these forces, providing a simplified interface for users to incorporate realistic physics into their applications.
