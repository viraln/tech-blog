Several powerful techniques can be employed to gain greater control over your reality. These methods require consistent practice and a willingness to challenge your existing beliefs.

5.  **Reality Shifting Methods:** Reality shifting involves consciously shifting your awareness to a different reality. This often involves deep meditation, visualization, and the use of scripts or affirmations. It's a relatively new concept gaining traction online.

6.  **Belief Re-framing:** This involves actively identifying and challenging limiting beliefs that hold you back. Once identified, you can re-frame these beliefs into more empowering and supportive ones.
    *   **Example:** Changing "I always fail at relationships" to "I am learning and growing with each relationship experience."
