## Understanding Intelligence Amplification: Beyond the Hype

Intelligence Amplification (IA) encompasses a broad range of technologies and techniques designed to augment human cognitive abilities.  Unlike artificial intelligence (AI), which aims to create artificial minds, IA focuses on enhancing existing human intelligence.
