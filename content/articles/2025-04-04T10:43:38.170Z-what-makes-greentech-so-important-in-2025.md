## Defining GreenTech: Beyond Renewable Energy Sources

While renewable energy sources like solar and wind power are certainly a cornerstone of GreenTech, the field encompasses a much broader range of innovations. GreenTech encompasses any technology designed to reduce environmental impact and promote sustainable practices. This includes, but is not limited to:

*   **Renewable Energy:** Solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, and biomass energy sources.
*   **Energy Efficiency:** Technologies that reduce energy consumption in buildings, transportation, and industrial processes.
*   **Waste Management:** Innovative solutions for recycling, waste reduction, and waste-to-energy conversion.
*   **Sustainable Agriculture:** Practices that minimize environmental impact while maximizing food production.
*   **Water Management:** Technologies for water conservation, purification, and wastewater treatment.
*   **Sustainable Transportation:** Electric vehicles, alternative fuels, and smart transportation systems.
*   **Air Pollution Control:** Technologies that reduce emissions from industrial and transportation sources.
*   **Green Building:** Construction and design practices that minimize environmental impact and maximize energy efficiency.

The scope of GreenTech is constantly expanding as new innovations emerge to address pressing environmental challenges.
