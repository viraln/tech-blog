## 6. Common Challenges and Solutions

Creating a purpose-driven space comes with its challenges:

**Challenge:** High initial investment cost.
**Solution:**  Phased implementation, exploring cost-effective design solutions, and prioritizing key areas for improvement.

**Challenge:** Resistance to change from employees.
**Solution:**  Involving employees in the design process, providing clear communication, and offering training on new technologies and work practices.

**Challenge:** Maintaining the space and ensuring its long-term effectiveness.
**Solution:**  Establishing a clear maintenance plan, incorporating flexible design elements, and regularly evaluating the space's effectiveness.

## 7. Expert Tips and Recommendations

1. **Start with your company's purpose:** Clearly define your company's mission and values.
2. **Involve employees in the design process:** Gather feedback and input from employees to ensure the space meets their needs.

5. **Regularly evaluate and improve:**  Continuously assess the space's effectiveness and make adjustments as needed.
