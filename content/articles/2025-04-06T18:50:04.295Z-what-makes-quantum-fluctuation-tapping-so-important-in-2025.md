**Step 2:** **Rate Your Intensity:** On a scale of 0 to 10, with 10 being the highest, assess the intensity of any negative emotions associated with your intention (or lack thereof).

**Step 3:** **The Setup:** While tapping on the Karate Chop point (the fleshy part of the side of your hand), repeat three times: "Even though I feel [negative emotion] about [intention], I deeply and completely accept myself."

**Step 4:** **The Tapping Sequence:** Tap on each of the following points while repeating a short phrase related to your intention or the negative emotion. (Approximately 5-7 taps per point)

    *   **Eyebrow (EB):** The beginning of the eyebrow, closest to the nose.
    *   **Side of the Eye (SE):** On the bony area to the side of the eye.
    *   **Under the Eye (UE):** On the bony area under the eye.
    *   **Under the Nose (UN):** The space between the nose and the upper lip.
    *   **Chin Point (CP):** The crease in the chin.
    *   **Collarbone Point (CB):** Where the collarbone meets the sternum.
    *   **Under the Arm (UA):** About four inches below the armpit.
    *   **Top of the Head (TH):** The very top of the head.

**Step 5:** **Quantum Visualization:** While tapping on the Top of the Head point, visualize your desired outcome as vividly as possible. Imagine yourself already experiencing it. Feel the emotions associated with it.

**Step 6:** **Re-Rate Your Intensity:** After completing the tapping sequence, re-rate the intensity of your negative emotions. If it's still high, repeat the process.

**Step 7:** **Gratitude and Integration:** End the session by expressing gratitude for the opportunity to connect with the quantum field and integrate your intention into your reality.
