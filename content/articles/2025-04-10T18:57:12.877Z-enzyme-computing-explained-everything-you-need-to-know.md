## What is Enzyme Computing?

Enzyme computing leverages the catalytic power of enzymes to solve computational problems. Unlike traditional silicon-based computers that rely on electronic signals, enzyme computers utilize biochemical reactions to process information.  Enzymes, highly specific proteins, act as biological catalysts, accelerating chemical reactions without being consumed themselves.  These reactions, carefully orchestrated, represent computational steps, leading to a solution. ![Microscopic image of enzymes at work](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

The core principle involves designing a network of enzymatic reactions where the presence or absence of specific molecules acts as binary code (0 and 1).  The output of these reactions, often a measurable change in color, fluorescence, or other detectable signal, represents the computational result.

Despite its potential, enzyme computing faces several challenges:
