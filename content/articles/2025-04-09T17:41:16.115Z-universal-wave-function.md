## Universal Wave Function Collapse: A Paradox?

The concept of wave function collapse, a cornerstone of traditional quantum mechanics, presents a challenge to the UWF.  In traditional quantum mechanics, measurement causes a wave function to "collapse" into a single definite state.  But if the entire universe is described by a single wave function, what would cause *that* to collapse? This remains a central open question in the field, leading to ongoing debates and alternative interpretations. Some physicists propose that collapse might not be a physical process but rather an epistemological one, a reflection of our limited access to information about the universe's true state.
