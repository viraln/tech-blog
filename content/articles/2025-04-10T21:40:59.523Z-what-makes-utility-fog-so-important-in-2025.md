## What is Utility Fog?

Utility fog, also known as aerosolized mist, is a finely dispersed mist of water droplets or other fluids, controlled and manipulated using advanced technology. Unlike traditional cooling methods, utility fog doesn't merely cool the air; it actively shapes and modifies the microclimate within a defined space. This is achieved through precise control over droplet size, density, and distribution, offering unparalleled versatility and efficiency.  ![Microscopic image of utility fog droplets](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1574105760219-acd338b7e0cc?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
