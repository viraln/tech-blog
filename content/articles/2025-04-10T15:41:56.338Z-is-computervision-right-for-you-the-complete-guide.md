## Demystifying ComputerVision Algorithms: A Beginner's Guide

ComputerVision algorithms are the brains behind the technology.  They use various techniques to process and analyze visual data, including:

* **Image Classification:** Assigning labels to images (e.g., "cat," "dog," "car").
* **Object Detection:** Locating and identifying objects within an image.
* **Image Segmentation:**  Partitioning an image into meaningful regions (e.g., separating the foreground from the background).
* **Facial Recognition:** Identifying and verifying individuals based on their facial features.
* **Optical Character Recognition (OCR):** Extracting text from images.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  Understanding the strengths and limitations of different algorithms is crucial for selecting the right tool for your specific application.
