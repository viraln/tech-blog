## Quantum Entanglement and Consciousness: The Science (and Speculation)

The potential link between quantum entanglement and consciousness is a fascinating and highly debated area of scientific inquiry. While there's no definitive proof that human consciousness directly utilizes quantum entanglement, several theories explore this possibility. One prominent theory is Orch-OR (Orchestrated Objective Reduction), proposed by physicist Sir Roger Penrose and anesthesiologist Stuart Hameroff. This theory suggests that quantum computations occur within microtubules inside brain neurons, and that these computations could be linked through quantum entanglement.

⚡ **Did you know?** Microtubules are structural components of cells, including neurons, and play a role in cell division, intracellular transport, and potentially, quantum processing.
