## What is Spatial Computing?

Spatial computing refers to technologies that allow computers to understand, interpret, and interact with the physical world. Unlike traditional computing which primarily deals with abstract data, spatial computing focuses on the spatial relationships between objects and users within a three-dimensional environment. This involves a combination of technologies like augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR), computer vision, sensor fusion, and artificial intelligence (AI) to create immersive and interactive experiences.
