##  DNA Metabarcoding: Unlocking Biodiversity's Secrets

DNA metabarcoding is a revolutionary technique that allows scientists to identify multiple species from a single environmental sample (e.g., soil, water).  This dramatically increases the efficiency of biodiversity assessments, particularly for smaller organisms like insects and microorganisms which are often difficult to identify visually.  ![Diagram illustrating the process of DNA metabarcoding](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1526374965328-7f61d4dc18c5?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
