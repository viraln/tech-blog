## What is Self-Similar Cognition? Unveiling the Fractal Mind

Self-similar cognition, often referred to as "fractal thinking," describes the way our minds use recurring patterns and structures at different scales to process information. Think of a fractal image – a snowflake, a coastline, or a Romanesco broccoli. These structures exhibit self-similarity; they contain smaller versions of themselves within their larger forms. Similarly, our cognitive processes often mirror this pattern.
