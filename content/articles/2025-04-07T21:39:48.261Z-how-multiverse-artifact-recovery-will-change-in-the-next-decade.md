To address these challenges, international agreements and ethical guidelines are crucial. These should cover:

*   **Artifact Acquisition Protocols:** Establishing clear rules for the acquisition and retrieval of artifacts, prioritizing minimal impact on alternate realities.
*   **Transparency and Accountability:** Ensuring that all artifact recovery operations are conducted with transparency and are subject to independent oversight.
*   **Benefit Sharing:** Establishing mechanisms for sharing the benefits of artifact recovery with the alternate realities from which they were obtained.
