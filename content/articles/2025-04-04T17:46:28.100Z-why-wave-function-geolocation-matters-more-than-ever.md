**Step 2:** The presence of an object alters the quantum properties detected by the sensors. This alteration could be a change in the interference pattern of entangled particles or a subtle shift in the measured field strength.

**Step 3:** The sensors transmit their data to a central processing unit. This unit analyzes the data using sophisticated algorithms based on quantum mechanics and signal processing.
