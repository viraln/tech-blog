## Essential Stellar Husbandry Techniques & Technologies

Several key techniques are at the forefront of stellar husbandry in 2025:

* 🔑 **Controlled Stellar Fusion:**  This involves carefully managing the fusion process within stars, potentially increasing energy output and extending their lifespan.
* ⚡ **Stellar Radiation Harvesting:**  This focuses on capturing and converting stellar radiation into usable energy through advanced solar collectors and energy conversion systems.
* ✅ **Planetary Engineering:**  Modifying planetary atmospheres and orbits to optimize energy capture and resource extraction from the host star.

These techniques require sophisticated technologies, including advanced materials capable of withstanding extreme temperatures and radiation, highly efficient energy conversion systems, and sophisticated robotic systems for maintenance and repair in the harsh environments of space.
