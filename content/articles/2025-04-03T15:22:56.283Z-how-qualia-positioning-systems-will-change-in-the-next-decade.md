## High-Accuracy Indoor Positioning Systems: A Deep Dive into the Technology

The foundation of Qualia positioning technology lies in the integration of multiple technologies, each contributing unique capabilities:

*   **Ultra-Wideband (UWB):** UWB offers exceptional accuracy and robustness in challenging environments, making it ideal for high-precision tracking. It transmits short pulses of radio energy over a wide frequency band, allowing for accurate time-of-flight measurements and minimizing interference.
*   **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE):** BLE beacons provide a cost-effective solution for proximity detection and location-based services. They are often used in conjunction with UWB to provide a more comprehensive positioning solution.
*   **Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs):** IMUs combine accelerometers and gyroscopes to track movement and orientation, providing valuable data for dead reckoning and sensor fusion. They are particularly useful in environments where radio signals may be unreliable.
*   **Sensor Fusion:** Advanced algorithms combine data from multiple sensors to create a highly accurate and reliable positioning solution. This allows QPS to overcome the limitations of individual technologies and provide a seamless indoor-outdoor experience.
