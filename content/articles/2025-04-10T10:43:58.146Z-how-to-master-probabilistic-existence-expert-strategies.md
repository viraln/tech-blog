## Probabilistic Existence in Quantum Mechanics

Quantum mechanics provides the most compelling empirical evidence for probabilistic existence.  The uncertainty principle, Heisenberg's famous contribution, states that we cannot simultaneously know both the position and momentum of a particle with perfect accuracy. This inherent uncertainty isn't a limitation of our measurement tools; it's a fundamental property of the universe.  Particles exist in a probabilistic cloud of possibilities until measured, at which point the wave function "collapses" into a single definite state.
