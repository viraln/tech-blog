## Quantum Mechanics in Genetics: Key Concepts

Several quantum phenomena are considered crucial in quantum genetics:

* 🔑 **Quantum Superposition:**  A quantum system can exist in multiple states simultaneously until measured. In genetics, this could mean a gene existing in multiple expression states before its activity is determined.
* ⚡ **Quantum Entanglement:** Two or more quantum systems become linked, sharing the same fate regardless of the distance separating them.  This could imply that entangled molecules within a cell could influence each other's behavior in unexpected ways, impacting gene expression.
* 🕳️ **Quantum Tunneling:**  A particle can pass through a potential energy barrier, even if it doesn't have enough energy to overcome it classically. This could explain the seemingly improbable occurrences of mutations or efficient enzymatic reactions.
* 🔄 **Quantum Coherence:**  The ability of a quantum system to maintain its superposition or entanglement over time. Maintaining coherence is essential for quantum effects to play a significant role in biological processes.
