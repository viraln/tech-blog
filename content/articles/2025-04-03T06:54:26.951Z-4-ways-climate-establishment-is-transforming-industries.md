## 4. Driving Technological Innovation: The Green Tech Revolution

The climate establishment is a major driver of technological innovation in the green tech sector. *   **Renewable Energy Technologies:** The development and deployment of renewable energy technologies, such as solar power, wind power, and geothermal energy, are crucial for transitioning to a low-carbon economy.
