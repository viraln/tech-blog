## What are Malleable Cognitive Systems?

Malleable cognitive systems represent a significant advancement in AI.  Unlike traditional AI systems that operate based on fixed rules and algorithms, malleable systems possess an inherent capacity for self-modification.  They can adjust their internal architecture, learning processes, and decision-making strategies in response to new information and changing circumstances. ## The Architecture of Malleable Cognitive Systems

The architecture of a malleable cognitive system is significantly different from traditional AI. It often involves:

* 🔑 **Modular Design:**  Components are interconnected but relatively independent, allowing for selective adaptation and replacement.
* ⚡ **Dynamic Learning:** Continuous learning mechanisms allow the system to update its knowledge and skills throughout its lifespan.
* ✅ **Self-Organization:**  The system can reorganize its internal structure to optimize performance based on experience.

These features allow for greater robustness, flexibility, and adaptability compared to static systems.  ![A diagram illustrating the modular architecture of a malleable cognitive system](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1581686676061-d4d717c7acc9?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
