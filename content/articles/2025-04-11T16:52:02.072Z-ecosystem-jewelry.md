## The Materials of Ecosystem Jewelry: A Deep Dive

The materials used in ecosystem jewelry are as diverse as the ecosystems they aim to protect.  Popular choices include:

* **Recycled Metals:**  Gold, silver, and platinum reclaimed from old jewelry or electronic waste are increasingly common.  This significantly reduces the environmental impact of mining new materials. 🔑
* **Upcycled Materials:** This involves transforming discarded materials into something new and valuable. * **Recycled Plastics:**  Innovative designers are finding creative ways to incorporate recycled plastics, contributing to waste reduction and showcasing the potential for upcycling.
