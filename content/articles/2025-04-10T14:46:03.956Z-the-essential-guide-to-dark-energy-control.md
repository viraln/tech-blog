## Understanding Dark Energy: The Theory Explained

Dark energy, first posited in the late 1990s, is a mysterious force accounting for approximately 68% of the universe's total energy density.  Unlike ordinary matter and dark matter, which exert gravitational attraction, dark energy exhibits a repulsive force, causing the accelerated expansion of the universe. ![Illustration of the universe expanding due to dark energy](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451847251646-8a6c0dd1510c?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
