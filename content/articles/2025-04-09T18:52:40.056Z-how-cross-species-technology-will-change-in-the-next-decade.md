##  Xenotransplantation: The Organ Shortage Solution?

The most immediate and impactful application of cross-species technology is xenotransplantation – the transplantation of animal organs into humans.  The global organ shortage is a crisis, with millions waiting for life-saving transplants.  Xenotransplantation offers a potential solution.  ![A microscopic image of pig cells being used for research](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1529988885170-6dc8ce34ce6d?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

> **DID YOU KNOW?**  Pigs are currently the most promising source of organs for xenotransplantation due to their organ size and physiology similarity to humans.

Recent breakthroughs have focused on genetic modification of pigs to minimize immune rejection and reduce the risk of zoonotic diseases.  Companies like Revivicor are making significant strides, producing genetically modified pigs with human-compatible organs.  However, significant hurdles remain, including the potential for viral transmission and the long-term effects of immunosuppressants.
