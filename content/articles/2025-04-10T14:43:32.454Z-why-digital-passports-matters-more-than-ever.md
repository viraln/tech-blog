## What is a Digital Passport?

A digital passport, or digital identity passport, is essentially a digital representation of your physical passport.  It's not just a scanned copy; it's a secure, verifiable digital identity document stored on a smartphone or other secure device. This digital version often incorporates biometric data, such as fingerprints or facial recognition, for enhanced security and authentication.  Think of it as a highly secure digital twin of your physical passport, accessible anytime, anywhere. ## How to Get a Digital Passport

The process of obtaining a digital passport varies depending on your country of citizenship.  Generally, it involves:

1. **Eligibility Check:**  Verify your eligibility for a digital passport based on your nationality and residency status.
2. **Application:**  Submit an application through the relevant government agency's online portal.
3. **Verification:**  Your identity will be verified using various methods, including biometric data.
4. **Issuance:**  Upon successful verification, your digital passport will be issued and linked to your secure device.
5. **Activation:**  Follow the instructions provided to activate your digital passport and start using it.
