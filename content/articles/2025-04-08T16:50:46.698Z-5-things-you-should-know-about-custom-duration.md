While **custom duration** offers numerous benefits, it's not without its challenges. Here's a look at some common hurdles and how to overcome them:

**Challenge 1: Inaccurate Time Estimation**

*   **Solution:** Track your time meticulously for a few weeks to get a better understanding of how long tasks actually take. Use historical data to inform your future estimations. Break down tasks into smaller, more manageable steps.

**Challenge 2: Distractions and Interruptions**

*   **Solution:** Minimize distractions by creating a dedicated workspace and turning off notifications. Communicate your availability to colleagues and set boundaries to protect your focus time. Use noise-canceling headphones or white noise to block out external sounds.

*   **Solution:** Break down large tasks into smaller, more manageable steps. Use the Pomodoro Technique (25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5-minute break) to overcome inertia. Reward yourself for completing tasks to stay motivated.

**Challenge 4: Scope Creep**

*   **Solution:** Clearly define the scope of each task and stick to it. Resist the temptation to add extra features or functionality that were not originally planned. If scope creep is unavoidable, adjust the **custom duration** accordingly.

**Challenge 5: Difficulty Adapting to Change**

*   **Solution:** Embrace a flexible mindset and be prepared to adjust your plans as needed. Regularly review your progress and identify areas where you may need to re-evaluate your **custom duration settings**.
