## Collective Unconscious Archetypes: Exploring Universal Patterns

Jungian archetypes are primordial images and patterns that shape our experiences and perceptions.  Understanding these archetypes is essential for mapping the collective unconscious:

* **The Self:**  Represents the unified whole of the personality, the integration of conscious and unconscious aspects.
* **The Shadow:**  The dark, repressed, and often unacceptable aspects of the personality.
* **The Anima/Animus:**  The feminine aspect in the male psyche and the masculine aspect in the female psyche.
* **The Persona:** The social mask we wear to interact with the world.
* **The Great Mother/Father:**  Representing nurturing, protection, and power.
