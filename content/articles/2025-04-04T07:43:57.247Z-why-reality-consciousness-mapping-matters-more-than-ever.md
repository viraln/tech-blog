## Reality Perception Mapping: Changing Your Lens

Reality perception mapping is a subset of reality consciousness mapping that focuses specifically on how you perceive the world. It involves identifying the filters, biases, and assumptions that shape your perception and consciously choosing to adjust them.

For example, if you tend to focus on the negative aspects of situations, you can consciously train yourself to look for the positive aspects as well. This doesn't mean ignoring the negative, but rather adopting a more balanced and objective perspective.

Here’s a simple exercise:

1.  **Identify a recurring negative thought pattern.** For example, "I'm not good enough."
2.  **Challenge the thought.** Ask yourself: Is this thought objectively true? What evidence supports it? What evidence contradicts it?
3.  **Reframe the thought.** Replace the negative thought with a more positive and empowering one. For example, "I am capable of learning and growing."
4.  **Practice this exercise regularly** to gradually shift your perception and create a more positive outlook.

> **DID YOU KNOW?** Studies have shown that our brains are wired for negativity bias, meaning we tend to pay more attention to negative stimuli than positive stimuli. This is an evolutionary survival mechanism, but it can also lead to a distorted perception of reality.
