## What is Ego Transfer?  Understanding the Basics of Ego Transfer Psychology

Ego transfer, also sometimes referred to as ego-contamination, describes the unconscious process of absorbing or internalizing the beliefs, emotions, and behaviors of another person.  It's not simply empathy; it's a deeper, more pervasive merging where the boundaries between your self and another become blurred.  This can manifest in subtle ways, like adopting a friend's anxieties, or in more pronounced ways, such as taking on a parent's unresolved trauma.  ![A graphic illustrating the blurring of boundaries between two individuals representing ego transfer](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1660075409120-151729435ece?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## Ego Transfer and Projection: Exploring the Interplay

Ego transfer is often intertwined with projection, a defense mechanism where individuals unconsciously attribute their own unacceptable thoughts or feelings onto others.  For example, someone harboring anger might project that anger onto a partner, perceiving them as aggressive even when they are not.  The individual then might unconsciously absorb that projected anger, experiencing it as their own emotion, thus exhibiting ego transfer.  Understanding this interplay is critical for navigating complex relationship dynamics.
