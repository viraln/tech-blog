## Types of Biohacking Offered in These Spaces

Biohacker spaces offer a diverse range of biohacking modalities. These can include:

* 🔑 **Quantified Self:** Tracking various physiological metrics (sleep, heart rate variability, etc.) using wearables and other devices.
* ⚡ **Nootropics and Cognitive Enhancement:** Experimenting with supplements and techniques to boost cognitive function and mental clarity.
* 🔬 **DIY Biology:** Engaging in hands-on projects involving genetic engineering, microbiology, and other biological techniques (often under strict safety regulations).
* 🩸 **Biofeedback and Neurofeedback:** Using technology to monitor and regulate physiological responses, such as heart rate and brainwave activity.
* 🏋️‍♂️ **Nutritional Biohacking:** Optimizing diet and nutrition based on individual needs and genetic predispositions.
