## Key AI Governance Models in 2025

Several models are emerging for Human-AI Co-Governance.  These include:

* **Human-in-the-loop AI:**  Humans retain ultimate control, overseeing AI decisions and intervening when necessary.  This is common in high-stakes applications like medical diagnosis and autonomous vehicles.
* **Human-on-the-loop AI:**  AI systems operate autonomously but are regularly audited and reviewed by humans to ensure compliance and ethical standards.
* **Human-over-the-loop AI:**  Humans set parameters and goals, and AI systems operate within those constraints, with minimal direct human intervention.  This model is suitable for tasks with clearly defined rules and predictable outcomes.
* **Shared-Control AI:**  Humans and AI systems share control, with each contributing their unique strengths to decision-making.  This approach is particularly relevant for complex tasks requiring both human intuition and AI's data processing capabilities.
