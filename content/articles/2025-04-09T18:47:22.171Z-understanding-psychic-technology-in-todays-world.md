##  Common Challenges and Solutions

One prevalent challenge is the "signal-to-noise" ratio in brainwave readings.  External factors and internal brain activity unrelated to the intended mental command can interfere with accurate interpretation.  Solutions include advanced signal processing techniques, improved sensor technology, and the development of more sophisticated algorithms to filter out noise and isolate relevant signals.

Another challenge lies in the subjective nature of psychic experiences.  Defining and measuring psychic phenomena objectively remains a significant obstacle.  Addressing this requires the development of standardized metrics and validated assessment tools.
