## Understanding the Science of Time Distortion

Time, as we perceive it, isn't a constant. It's a fluid, subjective experience heavily influenced by our emotions, attention, and cognitive processes. *Time distortion* refers to the phenomenon where our perception of the duration of an event differs significantly from its actual objective duration. This isn't science fiction; it's a well-documented psychological and neurological phenomenon.

Several factors contribute to time distortion:

*   **Emotional State:** High-stress situations often lead to time seeming to slow down, while joyful moments can feel fleeting. ![Time Distortion Control visualization](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

*   **Age:** As we age, our perception of time often accelerates. This is partly due to fewer novel experiences, which create fewer "mental markers" for our brains to use when recalling the past.
