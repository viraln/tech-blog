## How Does Quantum Positioning Work?

Unlike traditional GPS, which relies on radio signals susceptible to interference and atmospheric conditions, quantum positioning leverages the principles of quantum mechanics for unparalleled precision.  Several approaches are being explored, but a leading contender involves utilizing entangled photons. ![Illustration of entangled photons](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1651955784685-f969100bfc25?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
*Illustration of entangled photons*
*Photo by [Ben Wicks](https://unsplash.com/@profwicks) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/@profwicks)*

Two entangled photons, intrinsically linked regardless of distance, are generated. One photon remains at a known location (a base station), while the other travels to the receiver (e.g., a vehicle, drone, or mobile device). By measuring the quantum state of the photon at the base station, the receiver's position can be determined with astonishing accuracy.  This method is less susceptible to jamming or spoofing, a significant advantage over traditional GPS.  Another promising approach uses atomic clocks with exceptional precision, leading to improved timing accuracy for position calculations.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  The key to quantum positioning's accuracy lies in the inherent properties of quantum entanglement and the precision of quantum measurements.
