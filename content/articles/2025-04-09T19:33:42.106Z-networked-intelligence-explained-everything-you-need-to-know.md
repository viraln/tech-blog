## What is Networked Intelligence? A Comprehensive Definition

Networked intelligence isn't just about connecting multiple AI systems; it's about creating a synergistic network where individual intelligences collaborate, learn from each other, and achieve outcomes far beyond the capabilities of any single component.  Think of it as a hive mind, but with far greater sophistication and adaptability.  A precise definition of networked intelligence in 2025 encompasses:

* 🔑 **Interconnected Systems:** Multiple intelligent agents (AI systems, IoT devices, human experts) connected and communicating.
* ⚡ **Collaborative Problem Solving:** These agents work together to analyze data, identify patterns, and generate solutions collectively.
* ✅ **Adaptive Learning:** The network learns and evolves over time, improving its performance and adapting to changing conditions.  This is achieved through continuous feedback and information sharing.

## Networked Intelligence vs. AI: Understanding the Key Differences

While often used interchangeably, Networked Intelligence and Artificial Intelligence are distinct concepts:

| Feature          | Networked Intelligence                               | Artificial Intelligence                                  |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|

| **Focus**         | Collective problem-solving, synergy                    | Individual problem-solving, specific tasks                  |
| **Learning**      | Continuous, adaptive learning from shared experiences   | Primarily based on individual training data               |
