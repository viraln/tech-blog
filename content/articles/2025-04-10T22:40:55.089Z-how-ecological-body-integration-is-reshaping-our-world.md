## What is Ecological Body Integration?

Ecological Body Integration is a holistic therapeutic approach that recognizes the inseparable link between our internal world (body, mind, emotions) and the external environment. It builds upon the principles of somatic experiencing, ecological psychology, and trauma-informed care. Unlike traditional therapies that primarily focus on internal processes, EBI acknowledges that our physical, emotional, and psychological well-being is intricately woven into our relationship with nature and our surroundings. ![A person practicing mindful movement outdoors in a natural setting](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1532200846567-1bd8bd5b23aa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

EBI utilizes various techniques, including body awareness exercises, mindful movement, nature connection practices, and expressive arts, to help individuals reconnect with their bodies, process trauma, and foster a deeper sense of belonging within the ecosystem.  This approach views the body as a repository of both individual and collective trauma, recognizing that environmental degradation and social injustice can manifest as physical and emotional distress.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  EBI is not a one-size-fits-all approach.  A skilled practitioner will tailor the therapy to your specific needs and preferences, ensuring a safe and supportive environment for exploration and healing.
