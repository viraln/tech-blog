## IA vs AI: Understanding the Key Differences

While often conflated, IA and AI are distinct concepts.  The following table highlights their core differences:

| Feature          | Intelligence Augmentation (IA)                                  | Artificial Intelligence (AI)                                     |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Goal**         | Enhance human capabilities                                        | Replicate or surpass human intelligence                           |
| **Role of Humans** | Central; humans make final decisions                             | Often autonomous; may require minimal human intervention         |
| **Focus**        | Augmenting existing cognitive processes                          | Creating independent intelligent systems                          |
| **Decision-Making** | Human-driven, informed by IA tools                              | AI-driven, potentially with human oversight                     |
