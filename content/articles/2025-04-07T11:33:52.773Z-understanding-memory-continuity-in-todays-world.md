## Managing Digital Inheritance: A Practical Guide

"Managing digital inheritance" can be a complex process, requiring careful planning and execution. Here's a practical guide:

**Step 1:** Create a comprehensive inventory of your digital assets, including social media accounts, email accounts, online banking accounts, and digital documents.

**Step 2:** Draft a digital will that outlines how you want your digital assets to be managed after your passing. Consult with an attorney specializing in estate planning to ensure your digital will is legally sound.

**Step 3:** Appoint a digital executor who you trust to carry out the instructions in your digital will.

**Step 4:** Create a secure list of usernames and passwords for your online accounts and store it in a secure location. Consider using a password manager to protect this information.

**Step 5:** Regularly review and update your digital will and digital asset inventory to ensure they accurately reflect your current wishes and digital footprint.

**Step 6:** Inform your digital executor and family members about your digital will and where to find the necessary information to access and manage your digital assets.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Consider using a "vault" service that allows you to securely store sensitive information and grant access to designated individuals after your passing.
