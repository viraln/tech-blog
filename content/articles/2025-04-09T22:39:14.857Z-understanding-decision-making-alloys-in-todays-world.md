##  Corrosion Resistant Decision-Making Alloys: Mitigating Biases

Cognitive biases are like corrosion in our decision-making alloy, slowly weakening its integrity. To build corrosion resistance:

* **Awareness:**  Recognize common biases (confirmation bias, anchoring bias, availability heuristic).
* **Data Diversification:**  Seek diverse perspectives and information sources to counter narrow viewpoints.
* **Structured Decision-Making:**  Use frameworks like cost-benefit analysis or decision trees to minimize emotional influence.
* **Critical Thinking:**  Actively question assumptions and challenge your own reasoning.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Regularly reflect on past decisions, analyzing both successes and failures to identify areas for improvement in your decision-making process.
