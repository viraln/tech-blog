## Understanding Femtotechnology: A Journey to the Femtoscale

What is Femtotechnology? Femtotechnology deals with manipulating matter at the femtometer scale – that's 10<sup>-15</sup> meters, or one-quadrillionth of a meter. To put that in perspective, a femtometer is to a meter what a marble is to the size of the Earth. At this scale, we're dealing with the very structure of atomic nuclei and the fundamental forces that bind matter together. Femtoscale engineering is about understanding and controlling these forces to create materials and devices with unprecedented properties.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Don't confuse femtotechnology with nanotechnology. While both deal with extremely small scales, nanotechnology focuses on the nanometer scale (10<sup>-9</sup> meters), which is still significantly larger than the femtoscale. Femtotech goes deeper, manipulating the very building blocks of atoms.
