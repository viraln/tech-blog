## 2. The Kardashev Scale and Stellar Engineering

The Kardashev Scale, developed by Soviet astronomer Nikolai Kardashev, is a method of measuring a civilization's level of technological advancement based on the amount of energy it is able to utilize.

*   **Type I Civilization:** Can harness all the energy available on its planet.
*   **Type II Civilization:** Can harness all the energy of its star. This is where stellar manipulation comes into play.
*   **Type III Civilization:** Can harness the energy of its entire galaxy.

The scale is theoretical, but it provides a framework for understanding the potential motivations behind stellar engineering. A Type II civilization, for example, might construct a Dyson sphere – a hypothetical megastructure that completely encompasses a star to capture its energy output. While we haven't definitively detected a Dyson sphere, the search continues, fueled by the possibility that such structures could be detectable by their unique infrared signatures.

*   **Limited Technology:** Our current technology may not be sensitive enough to detect subtle signs of stellar manipulation.
