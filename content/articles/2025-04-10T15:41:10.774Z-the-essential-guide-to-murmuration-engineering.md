## Deconstructing the Murmuration Algorithm: The Building Blocks

The beauty of murmuration lies in its simplicity.  The core principles can be translated into elegant algorithms that govern the behavior of artificial agents, whether in simulations or physical robots.  These algorithms typically incorporate three key rules:

1. **Separation:** Avoid collisions with nearby agents.
2. **Alignment:** Steer towards the average heading of nearby agents.
3. **Cohesion:** Steer towards the average position of nearby agents.

These three rules, often referred to as the Boids model, form the foundation of many murmuration algorithms.  However, more sophisticated models incorporate additional factors like leader-follower dynamics, obstacle avoidance, and environmental influences. ![A diagram illustrating the three key rules of the Boids model](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1581092580497-e0d23cbdf1dc?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
