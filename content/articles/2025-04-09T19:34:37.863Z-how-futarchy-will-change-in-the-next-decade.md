## What is Futarchy? A Comprehensive Explanation

Futarchy, a term coined by economist Robin Hanson, is a decision-making system that blends the best aspects of democracy and markets.  Instead of directly voting on policies, citizens (or stakeholders) use prediction markets to forecast the outcomes of different policy options.  The policy with the highest predicted success rate, as determined by the market, is then implemented.  This allows for a data-driven approach to governance, leveraging the collective intelligence of a diverse group of individuals to make more informed decisions.  ![A graphic illustrating the futarchy decision-making process](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

> **EXPERT TIP:**  The key difference between futarchy and democracy lies in the *mechanism* of decision-making. Futarchy uses prediction markets to assess the likely success of different policies, rather than relying solely on direct votes.

## Futarchy Prediction Markets: The Engine of Futarchy

Prediction markets are the heart of futarchy. These markets allow individuals to buy and sell contracts based on the likelihood of future events.  In a futarchy context, these events are the outcomes of different policy options.  The price of a contract reflects the collective wisdom of the market participants regarding the probability of a specific outcome.  The higher the price, the greater the market's confidence in that outcome.

> **DID YOU KNOW?**  Prediction markets have successfully predicted election results, box office successes, and even scientific breakthroughs with remarkable accuracy.
