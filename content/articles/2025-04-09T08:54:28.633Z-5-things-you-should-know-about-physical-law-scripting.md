## 1. Understanding the Fundamentals: More Than Just Game Physics Scripting

Physical law scripting, at its core, is the art of writing code that governs the behavior of objects within a simulated environment according to the principles of physics. While often associated with game development (game physics scripting), its applications span far beyond, including scientific simulations, engineering design, and even artistic installations.  It's about translating complex mathematical equations and physical laws – like Newton's laws of motion, elasticity, and fluid dynamics – into executable code.  This isn't just about making things bounce; it's about creating accurate representations of the physical world.
