## How Does Hyperloop Work?  The Physics of Supersonic Travel

Hyperloop technology leverages a combination of established and cutting-edge principles to achieve near-supersonic speeds.  At its core, it's a system of sealed tubes, partially evacuated of air, through which pods carrying passengers or freight travel at incredibly high speeds.  ![Cross-section diagram of a Hyperloop pod inside a tube](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1564756631354-009c12675629?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

This near-vacuum environment significantly reduces aerodynamic drag, a major impediment to high-speed travel. The pods themselves are propelled forward using a combination of linear induction motors and air compressors.  These systems work in tandem to accelerate the pod to its cruising speed and maintain it throughout the journey.
