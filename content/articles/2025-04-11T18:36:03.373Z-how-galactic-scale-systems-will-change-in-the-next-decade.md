## Common Challenges and Solutions in Galactic-Scale System Modeling

Despite advancements, challenges remain:

* **Computational limitations:**  Simulating the entire universe remains computationally infeasible.  Researchers must rely on clever techniques, like zoom simulations, to focus on regions of particular interest.
* **Dark Matter and Dark Energy:**  The nature of dark matter and dark energy remains a mystery.  Simulations must incorporate these components, despite their unknown properties.
* **Subgrid Physics:**  Many physical processes occur on scales too small to be resolved directly in simulations.  Researchers must develop sophisticated subgrid models to account for these effects.
