## Freelance vs. Gig Economy:  Understanding the Nuances

While often used interchangeably, "freelance" and "gig economy" have subtle differences.  Freelancing typically refers to providing services on a project basis, often to a single client.  The gig economy encompasses a broader range of short-term engagements, often involving multiple platforms and clients.  ![A Venn diagram illustrating the overlap and differences between freelancing and the gig economy](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1562946777-a92221a54d2b?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
