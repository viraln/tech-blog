Understanding the nutritional content of food is crucial for maintaining optimal health. Macronutrients – carbohydrates, proteins, and fats – provide energy and building blocks for our bodies. Micronutrients – vitamins and minerals – are essential for various bodily functions.

*   **Carbohydrates:** The body's primary source of energy. Choose complex carbohydrates like whole grains, fruits, and vegetables over simple sugars for sustained energy release.
*   **Proteins:** Essential for building and repairing tissues. Good sources include lean meats, poultry, fish, beans, lentils, and tofu.
*   **Fats:** Crucial for hormone production, cell function, and nutrient absorption. Opt for healthy fats like those found in avocados, nuts, seeds, and olive oil.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Pay attention to portion sizes. Even healthy foods can contribute to weight gain if consumed in excess.

## Quick & Easy Recipes for the Busy Weeknight

In today's fast-paced world, finding time to cook can be a challenge. These quick dinner recipes are designed to be both delicious and efficient, perfect for busy weeknights.

**1. Sheet Pan Chicken Fajitas:**

*   **Step 1:** Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
*   **Step 2:** Slice chicken breast, bell peppers (various colors), and onions into strips.
*   **Step 3:** Toss with olive oil, fajita seasoning, and a squeeze of lime juice.
*   **Step 4:** Spread evenly on a baking sheet and bake for 20-25 minutes, or until chicken is cooked through.
*   **Step 5:** Serve with warm tortillas, salsa, guacamole, and sour cream.

**2. One-Pot Pasta Primavera:**

*   **Step 1:** In a large pot, combine pasta, vegetable broth, chopped vegetables (broccoli, carrots, peas, zucchini), garlic, and Italian seasoning.
*   **Step 2:** Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until pasta is cooked and vegetables are tender, about 15-20 minutes.
*   **Step 3:** Stir in grated Parmesan cheese and a drizzle of olive oil before serving.

**3. Salmon with Roasted Asparagus:**

*   **Step 1:** Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
*   **Step 2:** Toss asparagus with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet.
*   **Step 3:** Place salmon fillets on the same baking sheet. Season with salt, pepper, and lemon slices.
*   **Step 4:** Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until salmon is cooked through and asparagus is tender.

These are just a few examples. The key is to utilize simple ingredients and cooking methods to minimize prep and cleanup time.

## Meal Prep Ideas: Planning for a Healthier Week

Meal prepping is a powerful strategy for saving time, controlling portions, and maintaining a healthy diet.

**Steps to Successful Meal Prep:**

1.  **Plan your meals:** Choose recipes for breakfast, lunch, and dinner for the week.
2.  **Create a shopping list:** Based on your recipes, make a detailed list of all the ingredients you need.
3.  **Shop for groceries:** Purchase all the necessary ingredients.
4.  **Prepare the ingredients:** Wash, chop, and portion out vegetables, fruits, and proteins.
5.  **Cook the meals:** Cook your meals in batches, using efficient cooking methods like roasting, grilling, or slow cooking.
6.  **Portion and store:** Divide the meals into individual containers and store them in the refrigerator or freezer.
