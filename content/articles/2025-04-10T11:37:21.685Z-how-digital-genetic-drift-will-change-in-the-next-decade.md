## What is Digital Genetic Drift?

Digital genetic drift refers to the random changes in the frequency of digital "genes" – bits of code, algorithms, or design choices – within a digital population (e.g., software versions, websites, online communities). Unlike natural selection, where advantageous traits are preferentially passed on, digital genetic drift is driven by chance events, such as programmer preferences, accidental errors, or simply the popularity of certain features. ![A visual representation of digital genetic drift, showing diverging lines of code](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1526374965328-7f61d4dc18c5?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
