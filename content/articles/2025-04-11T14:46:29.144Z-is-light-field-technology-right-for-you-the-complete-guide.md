## What is Light Field Technology?

Light field technology captures not just the intensity of light at each pixel, like a traditional camera, but also its direction.  This is achieved by using microlenses positioned in front of the image sensor. These microlenses capture multiple perspectives of the scene simultaneously, creating a vast amount of data that encodes the light field itself. ![Microlenses on a light field sensor](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1668578841382-90d7099ee848?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
*Microlenses on a light field sensor*
*Photo by [Markus Spiske](https://unsplash.com/@markusspiske) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/@markusspiske)*

Think of it like this: a traditional camera records a 2D snapshot, while a light field camera records a 4D representation – three spatial dimensions plus the direction of light. This extra information allows for unprecedented post-capture control, enabling you to adjust focus, aperture, and even depth of field after the image is taken.  This fundamentally alters the creative possibilities for photographers and filmmakers alike.

## Light Field Photography: Beyond Traditional Imaging

Light field photography offers a revolutionary approach to image capture.  Instead of a single fixed focal point, you capture a multitude of perspectives. This means you can:

* 🔑 **Refocus after capture:** Adjust the focus point to highlight different subjects in your image, even after the fact.
* ⚡ **Control depth of field:** Achieve shallow or deep depth of field effects in post-processing, giving you unparalleled control over image aesthetics.
* ✅ **Create unique effects:** Generate novel visual effects like 3D models or augmented reality experiences directly from your light field captures.
