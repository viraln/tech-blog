## Understanding DNA Data Storage Technology

So, how does it work? DNA storage isn't about storing biological information. Instead, it leverages the four nucleotide bases of DNA – adenine (A), guanine (G), cytosine (C), and thymine (T) – to encode digital data. Think of A, G, C, and T as the 0s and 1s of the biological world. Binary code is translated into sequences of these bases, which are then synthesized into strands of artificial DNA.

**Here's a simplified breakdown of the process:**

**Step 1:** Convert digital data (0s and 1s) into a DNA code (A, G, C, and T).
**Step 2:** Synthesize DNA strands with the encoded information.
**Step 3:** Store the DNA in a dry, dark, and cool environment.
**Step 4:** To retrieve the data, sequence the DNA and decode it back into digital information.
