At the heart of quartz-based quantum computing lies the manipulation of quantum phenomena within the quartz crystal structure.  One promising approach involves using nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in quartz.  These NV centers are atomic-scale defects within the crystal lattice that exhibit quantum properties.  By using lasers and microwaves, researchers can control the spin of the electrons within these NV centers, encoding and manipulating quantum information. ![Diagram illustrating an NV center in a quartz crystal lattice](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1484417894907-623942c8ee29?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
