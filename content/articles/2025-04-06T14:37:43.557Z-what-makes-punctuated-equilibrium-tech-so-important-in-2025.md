## Understanding Punctuated Equilibrium: Beyond Gradualism

Punctuated equilibrium, originally a concept from evolutionary biology, describes a pattern of long periods of relative stability (equilibrium) punctuated by short bursts of rapid change (punctuation). In the context of technology, this means that periods of incremental improvement and refinement are occasionally disrupted by radical innovations that fundamentally alter the landscape. Think of the shift from horse-drawn carriages to automobiles, or from landline phones to smartphones. These weren't gradual transitions; they were sudden leaps that redefined entire industries.
