## The Casimir Effect: A Window into Vacuum Energy

The Casimir effect, first predicted in 1948 and experimentally verified since, provides compelling evidence of quantum vacuum energy.  This effect arises from the restrictions placed on virtual particles between two closely spaced conductive plates.  This restriction leads to a measurable attractive force between the plates. While the force is minuscule at macroscopic scales, it demonstrates the potential to manipulate and harness the energy of the quantum vacuum.  ![Diagram illustrating the Casimir effect between two plates](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1581092580497-e0d23cbdf1dc?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
