## Key Principles of Quantum Social Mechanics

This nascent field borrows several key concepts from quantum mechanics:

* **Superposition:** Individuals can hold multiple social identities or opinions simultaneously, much like a quantum particle exists in multiple states until measured.  This explains the fluidity and complexity of social roles.
* **Entanglement:** The actions and beliefs of one individual can instantaneously influence others within a network, mirroring the entangled states of quantum particles. This helps explain the rapid spread of social movements or trends.
* **Non-locality:**  Social influence can transcend geographical boundaries and traditional communication channels, similar to the non-local correlations observed in quantum mechanics.  This is evident in the global reach of social media.
* **Measurement Problem:** The act of observing or measuring social behavior can influence the outcome, much like the observer effect in quantum physics.  This highlights the challenges of conducting unbiased social research.
