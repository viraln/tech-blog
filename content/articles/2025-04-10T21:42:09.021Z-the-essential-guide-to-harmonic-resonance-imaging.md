## What is Harmonic Resonance Imaging?

Harmonic Resonance Imaging (HRI) is a novel medical imaging modality that leverages the principles of resonant frequencies to create high-resolution images of internal organs and tissues. Unlike traditional Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI), which relies on strong magnetic fields, HRI uses targeted, low-intensity electromagnetic fields to excite specific atomic nuclei within the body.  These nuclei then emit characteristic resonance signals, which are captured and processed to generate detailed images. ![A simplified diagram showing the principle of harmonic resonance imaging](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1619691249147-c5689d88016b?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
