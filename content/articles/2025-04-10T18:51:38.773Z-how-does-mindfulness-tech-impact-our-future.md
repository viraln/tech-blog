## Mindfulness Tech for Anxiety: Addressing a Growing Need

Anxiety disorders are increasingly prevalent, making mindfulness tech a valuable tool for managing symptoms.  Many apps offer specific programs for anxiety, including guided meditations, breathing exercises, and cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) techniques.  These tools help individuals develop coping mechanisms and build resilience in the face of anxiety-provoking situations.
