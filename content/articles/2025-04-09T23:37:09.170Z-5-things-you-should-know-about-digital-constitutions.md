## 1. What is a Digital Constitution?  Understanding the Fundamentals

A digital constitution isn't simply a paper document scanned and uploaded online. It's a far more sophisticated concept, representing a fundamental shift in how we codify and enforce laws and regulations.  It leverages blockchain technology, smart contracts, and other digital tools to create a dynamic, transparent, and secure framework for governance.  Think of it as a living, breathing document that adapts to changing circumstances, ensuring constant relevance and accessibility.
