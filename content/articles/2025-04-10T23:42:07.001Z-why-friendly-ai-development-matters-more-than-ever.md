## Defining "Friendly AI": More Than Just Safety

Friendly AI goes beyond simply avoiding harm. It encompasses a broader set of principles, including:

* 🔑 **Safety:** Minimizing the risk of unintended consequences and harmful behaviors.
* ⚡ **Alignment:** Ensuring the AI's goals are aligned with human values and intentions.
* ✅ **Transparency:** Making the AI's decision-making processes understandable and explainable.
* 🌍 **Fairness and Equity:** Preventing bias and ensuring equitable access and outcomes.
* 🤝 **Accountability:** Establishing clear lines of responsibility for the AI's actions.

##  The Pillars of Safe AI Development: Practical Guidelines
