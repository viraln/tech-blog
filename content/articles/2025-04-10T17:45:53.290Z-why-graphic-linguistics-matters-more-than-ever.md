## Understanding the Landscape of Graphic Linguistics

Graphic linguistics, also known as visual linguistics or language visualization, is the interdisciplinary field bridging linguistics and graphic design.  It focuses on the visual representation of language, exploring how we perceive and interpret linguistic information presented visually.
