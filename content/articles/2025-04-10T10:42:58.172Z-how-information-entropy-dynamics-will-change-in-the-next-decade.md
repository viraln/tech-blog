## What is Information Entropy? A Refresher

Before we look to the future, let's establish a firm understanding of the present.  Information entropy, a concept born from Claude Shannon's groundbreaking work in information theory, quantifies the uncertainty or randomness in a system.  In simpler terms, it measures how much information is needed to describe a system's state.  A highly ordered system has low entropy, while a chaotic one has high entropy. ![A visual representation of high vs. low entropy systems, perhaps using colored blocks.](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1704022472896-c7f2423b3ce7?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

> **Did you know?** The concept of entropy isn't limited to information theory. It's a fundamental concept in thermodynamics, describing the degree of disorder in a physical system.  This connection highlights the deep, underlying principles governing both physical and informational realms.
