## What is a Digital Twin?  A Deep Dive into the Technology

A digital twin is a virtual replica of a physical entity, mirroring its characteristics, behaviors, and performance in real-time.  It's not just a static model; it's a dynamic, evolving entity constantly updated with data from its physical counterpart through sensors, IoT devices, and other data sources. This continuous feedback loop allows for accurate simulations, predictive analysis, and proactive interventions. ![A graphic illustrating the connection between a physical object and its digital twin.](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1592084284963-884b5d66f7cc?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
