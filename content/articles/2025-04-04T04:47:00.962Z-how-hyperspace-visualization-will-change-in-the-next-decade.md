## The Current State of Hyperspace Visualization: A Glimpse into the 4th Dimension

Currently, visualizing hyperspace, especially four-dimensional (4D) space, relies heavily on projections and analogies. We often use 3D shadows of 4D objects, like the tesseract (a 4D hypercube), to give us a sense of their structure. These projections, while helpful, are inherently limited because they flatten the higher-dimensional object into a lower dimension, leading to distortions and a loss of information. Traditional methods also involve mathematical equations and abstract representations, making it challenging for non-experts to grasp the concepts.
