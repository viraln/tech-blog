## Demystifying the Tesseract: A Gateway to 4D Understanding

The tesseract, or hypercube, is often the first encounter people have with 4D geometry. It's the 4D analogue of the cube, and understanding its structure is crucial for grasping the basics of hyperspace.

Think of it this way:

**Step 1:** Start with a point (0D).
**Step 2:** Move the point a distance to create a line (1D).
**Step 3:** Move the line a distance perpendicular to itself to create a square (2D).
**Step 4:** Move the square a distance perpendicular to itself to create a cube (3D).
**Step 5:** Move the cube a distance perpendicular to our 3D space to create a tesseract (4D).
