## Common Challenges and Solutions in Medical Nanobot Development

Despite the immense potential, several challenges remain in developing and implementing medical nanobots.  These include ensuring biocompatibility, preventing toxicity, achieving effective targeting, and developing reliable control mechanisms.  Furthermore, the cost of developing and manufacturing nanobots, as well as the cost of nanobot therapy, remains a significant hurdle.  Addressing these challenges requires collaborative efforts between researchers, engineers, clinicians, and regulatory bodies.
