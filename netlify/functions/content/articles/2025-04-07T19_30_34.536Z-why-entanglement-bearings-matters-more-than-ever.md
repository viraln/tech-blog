## 1. Understanding Entanglement Bearings: Beyond Traditional Friction

Traditional bearings rely on mechanical components – balls, rollers, or fluids – to reduce friction between moving parts. Entanglement bearings, also known as quantum bearings, offer a fundamentally different approach. They leverage the principles of quantum entanglement to minimize or even eliminate friction at the atomic level.

Instead of relying on physical separation, entanglement bearings use entangled particles to create a quantum correlation between the stationary and rotating components.
