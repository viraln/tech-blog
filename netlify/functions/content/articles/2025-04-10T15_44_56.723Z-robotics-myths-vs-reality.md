## Collaborative Robots (Cobots): Working Side-by-Side with Humans

Collaborative robots, or cobots, represent a significant shift in the human-robot relationship. Unlike traditional industrial robots, which operate in isolated cages, cobots are designed to work safely alongside humans. This collaboration enhances productivity and allows for more flexible manufacturing processes. ![A human working safely alongside a collaborative robot](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1563968743333-044cef800494?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
