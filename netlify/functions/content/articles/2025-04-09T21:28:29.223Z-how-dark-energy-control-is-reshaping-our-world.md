## Understanding Dark Energy: A Deep Dive into the Unknown

Dark energy, comprising approximately 68% of the universe's total energy density, remains one of the biggest mysteries in modern cosmology.  Unlike ordinary matter and dark matter, which exert gravitational attraction, dark energy exhibits a repulsive gravitational effect, driving the accelerated expansion of the universe. ![Illustration of the universe's expansion driven by dark energy](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451847251646-8a6c0dd1510c?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

> **DID YOU KNOW?** The discovery of dark energy's accelerating effect on the universe's expansion earned the 2011 Nobel Prize in Physics.

Current research focuses on refining our understanding of dark energy's properties through precise measurements of the cosmic microwave background radiation, supernovae distances, and large-scale structure formation.  These observations help constrain theoretical models and narrow down the possibilities.
