## Metaphysics Quantum Reality: Exploring Different Interpretations

Several interpretations of quantum mechanics exist, each with its own metaphysical implications:

* **Copenhagen Interpretation:** This dominant interpretation suggests that a quantum system exists in a superposition of states until measured. The act of measurement collapses the wave function, forcing the system into a definite state.  This raises questions about the role of the observer in shaping reality.

* **Many-Worlds Interpretation:** This proposes that every quantum measurement causes the universe to split into multiple universes, each representing a different outcome.  This has profound implications for free will and the nature of reality itself.

* **Objective Collapse Theories:** These theories attempt to modify quantum mechanics to incorporate spontaneous wave function collapse, eliminating the need for an observer.
