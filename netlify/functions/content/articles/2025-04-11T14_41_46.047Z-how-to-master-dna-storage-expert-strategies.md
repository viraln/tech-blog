## Understanding the Fundamentals of DNA Data Storage

How does DNA storage work? It's surprisingly straightforward.  Digital data – your photos, videos, documents – is translated into a binary code (0s and 1s). This code is then used to synthesize a unique DNA sequence, where each nucleotide (A, T, C, G) represents a bit of information.  This synthesized DNA is then physically stored, offering an incredibly dense and stable archive. ![Microscopic image of DNA strands](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1579154392128-bf8c7ebee541?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
