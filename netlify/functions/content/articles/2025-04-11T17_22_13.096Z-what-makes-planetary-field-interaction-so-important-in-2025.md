## Effects of Solar Wind on Planets: Atmospheric Erosion and Habitability

The solar wind's impact extends far beyond auroras and geomagnetic storms.  For planets with weak or nonexistent magnetic fields, the solar wind can directly interact with their atmospheres, causing significant atmospheric erosion.  This process has profound implications for planetary habitability, particularly for planets in the habitable zone of their stars.  Mars, for instance, is believed to have lost a significant portion of its atmosphere due to solar wind stripping, resulting in its current arid and inhospitable environment.
