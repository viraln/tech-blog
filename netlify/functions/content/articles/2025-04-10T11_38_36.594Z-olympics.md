## A Journey Through Olympic History: From Olympia to the World Stage

The Olympic Games, steeped in ancient Greek tradition, trace their roots back to 776 BC in Olympia. ![Ancient Olympic Games depiction](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

However, the Games were eventually abolished in 393 AD by the Roman Emperor Theodosius I.

The modern Olympic Games, as we know them, were revived in 1896 by Baron Pierre de Coubertin.  The first modern Olympics, held in Athens, Greece, marked the beginning of a global sporting phenomenon that has since captivated billions.  The Games have witnessed incredible feats of athleticism, groundbreaking moments, and the emergence of legendary athletes.  Coubertin's vision of fostering international understanding and peace through sport has, despite its challenges, endured as a core principle.
