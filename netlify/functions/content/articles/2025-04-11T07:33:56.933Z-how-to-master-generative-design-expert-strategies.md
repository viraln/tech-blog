## What is Generative Design?  Understanding the Fundamentals

Generative design is an advanced computational process that leverages algorithms and artificial intelligence to explore a vast range of design possibilities based on specified parameters and constraints. Unlike traditional design, where a human designer iteratively refines a single concept, generative design automatically generates numerous design options, optimizing for factors like weight, strength, cost, and manufacturability. ## Generative Design vs. Parametric Design: Key Differences

While both generative and parametric design utilize algorithms, they differ significantly in their approach:

| Feature          | Generative Design                                  | Parametric Design                               |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| **Approach**     | Explores numerous design options automatically    | Modifies a single design based on parameters     |
| **Output**       | Multiple optimized design solutions                 | Single refined design                           |
| **Complexity**   | High computational complexity                       | Relatively lower complexity                       |
| **User Input**   | Defines goals, constraints, and parameters         | Defines parameters and relationships              |
