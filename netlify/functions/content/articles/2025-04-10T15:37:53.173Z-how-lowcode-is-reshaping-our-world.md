## LowCode vs. NoCode: Understanding the Differences

While often used interchangeably, LowCode and NoCode platforms differ in their level of coding requirement.

| Feature          | LowCode                               | NoCode                                   |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Coding Required  | Minimal hand-coding for customization  | Virtually no coding required             |
| Target User      | Citizen developers & professional devs | Primarily citizen developers, business users |
| Complexity       | Can handle complex applications        | Best suited for simpler applications       |
| Customization    | High degree of customization           | Limited customization options             |

![A simple infographic clearly illustrating the differences between LowCode and NoCode](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1484417894907-623942c8ee29?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

LowCode platforms offer a balance between ease of use and flexibility, allowing developers to customize and extend functionality as needed. NoCode platforms, on the other hand, prioritize ease of use and simplicity, ideal for less complex applications.
