## The Current State of ZIMS: A Foundation for the Future

The Zoological Information Management System (ZIMS) is currently the world's largest animal records database, serving as the cornerstone of animal data management for over 1,200 institutions across 102 countries. Managed by Species360, a non-profit organization, ZIMS provides a centralized platform for zoos, aquariums, and other wildlife organizations to record, manage, and share vital information about the animals in their care. This includes everything from basic demographic data (birth dates, parentage) to detailed medical records, behavioral observations, and husbandry practices.

Currently, ZIMS is a robust system offering a range of modules, including:

*   **ZIMS for Husbandry:** Managing daily care routines, feeding schedules, and environmental parameters.
*   **ZIMS for Medical:** Tracking animal health, diagnoses, treatments, and medications.
*   **ZIMS for Studbooks:** Maintaining accurate lineage records and facilitating breeding programs.
*   **ZIMS for Collection Planning:** Optimizing space utilization and resource allocation.

*   **Optimized Husbandry Practices:** Machine learning models could analyze husbandry data to identify the most effective feeding strategies, enrichment activities, and environmental conditions for different species.
