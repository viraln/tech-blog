## 1. Revolutionizing Construction with Adaptive Infrastructure

The construction industry, historically slow to adopt new technologies, is on the cusp of a significant transformation thanks to property-shifting materials. This property is being used to create earthquake-resistant structures that can absorb and dissipate energy during seismic events, minimizing damage and protecting lives. Imagine buildings that sway and return to their original position after an earthquake, thanks to SMA-reinforced support systems.

**Examples of Property-Shifting Materials in Construction:**

*   **Thermochromic Coatings:** Regulate building temperature by changing color based on sunlight.
*   **Piezoelectric Materials:** Harvest energy from vibrations and pressure, powering sensors.

These advancements not only improve the durability and safety of buildings but also contribute to more sustainable construction practices by reducing waste and energy consumption.
