## Key Design Elements of Dimension-Shifting Spaces

Several key elements contribute to the creation of a truly dimension-shifting space:

* **Perspective Manipulation:**  Using mirrors, strategically placed openings, and cleverly designed furniture can alter the perceived size and shape of a room.  Consider using forced perspective techniques, borrowed from filmmaking, to create stunning visual illusions.
* **Lighting and Shadows:** Lighting is paramount.
