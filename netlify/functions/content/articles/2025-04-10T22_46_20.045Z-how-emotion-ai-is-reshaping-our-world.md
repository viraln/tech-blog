* 🔑 **Facial expressions:**  Cameras analyze micro-expressions, subtle shifts in facial muscles, to identify emotions like happiness, sadness, anger, or surprise.
* ⚡ **Vocal tone and speech patterns:**  Audio analysis detects variations in pitch, intonation, and pace to identify emotional cues in speech.
