## What is Atomic Construction?

Atomic construction, in its broadest sense, refers to the precise and controlled assembly of structures at a microscopic or nanoscopic level.  While true "atom-by-atom" building is still science fiction, the term encompasses a range of emerging technologies that leverage advanced materials and manufacturing techniques to create structures with unprecedented precision, efficiency, and sustainability.
