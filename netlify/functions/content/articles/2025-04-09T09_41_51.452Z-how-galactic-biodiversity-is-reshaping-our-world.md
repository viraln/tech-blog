## The Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence (SETI) and its Implications

The Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence (SETI) represents a crucial aspect of exploring galactic biodiversity.  While the search for microbial life focuses on biosignatures – detectable signs of past or present life – SETI aims to detect technosignatures, evidence of advanced extraterrestrial civilizations. This includes radio signals, megastructures, or other technological artifacts that could indicate a technologically advanced civilization. ![Radio telescope array](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1682687981922-7b55dbb30892?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
