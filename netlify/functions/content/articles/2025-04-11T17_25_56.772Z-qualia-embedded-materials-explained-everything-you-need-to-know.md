## What are Qualia-Embedded Materials?

Qualia-embedded materials are engineered materials designed to elicit specific subjective experiences—qualia—in users.  Unlike traditional materials defined solely by their physical properties (strength, conductivity, etc.), these materials incorporate elements that directly influence our perception and emotional response.  This is achieved through a combination of material science, sensory design, and even cognitive psychology.  Think of a building material that subtly adjusts its temperature based on your body heat, or a textile that changes color depending on your mood.  These are examples of the potential of qualia-embedded materials.  The "qualia" refers to the subjective, qualitative character of experience—the redness of red, the bitterness of coffee, the feeling of coolness on your skin.  These materials aim to directly manipulate and enhance these experiences.
