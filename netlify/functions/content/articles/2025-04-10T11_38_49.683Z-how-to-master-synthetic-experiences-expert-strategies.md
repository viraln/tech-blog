## Common Challenges and Solutions in Synthetic Experience Development

* **Motion Sickness:**  Poorly designed experiences can induce nausea.  Solutions include gradual introduction of motion, clear visual cues, and optimized frame rates.
* **High Development Costs:**  Creating high-quality synthetic experiences can be expensive.  Outsourcing, utilizing pre-built assets, and focusing on MVPs (Minimum Viable Products) can help mitigate costs.
* **Technical Limitations:**  Current technology has limitations in rendering realism and processing power.  Continued technological advancements are addressing these issues.
* **Accessibility:**  Ensuring inclusivity for users with disabilities is crucial.  This requires careful consideration of design and functionality.
