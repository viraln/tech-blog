## What is Neuralink and How Does It Work?

Neuralink, founded in 2016, is developing a high-bandwidth brain-computer interface (BCI). This technology involves implanting tiny electrodes into the brain, allowing direct communication between the brain and external devices. The current Neuralink device, roughly the size of a coin, houses a sophisticated chip connected to thousands of microscopic threads. These threads are carefully implanted into specific regions of the brain responsible for motor control, sensory perception, and cognitive functions.

The implanted device records neural activity, translating brain signals into digital commands that can control computers, smartphones, or even prosthetic limbs. Conversely, the device can also send electrical signals back into the brain, potentially restoring lost functions or enhancing existing ones. Neuralink aims to create a minimally invasive and highly scalable BCI, paving the way for widespread adoption.

> **EXPERT TIP:** The key to Neuralink's potential lies in its high bandwidth – the amount of data that can be transferred between the brain and the device. Higher bandwidth allows for more precise control and richer sensory feedback.
