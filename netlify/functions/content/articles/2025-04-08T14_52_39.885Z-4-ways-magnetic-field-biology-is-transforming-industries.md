### Magnetoencephalography (MEG): Mapping Brain Activity

Magnetoencephalography (MEG) is a non-invasive neuroimaging technique that measures the magnetic fields produced by electrical activity in the brain. MEG offers excellent temporal resolution, allowing researchers to track brain activity in real-time. This makes it a valuable tool for studying cognitive processes, diagnosing neurological disorders, and monitoring the effects of brain injuries.

> **EXPERT TIP:** MEG provides complementary information to electroencephalography (EEG), which measures electrical activity on the scalp. MEG is more sensitive to activity in deeper brain structures and is less affected by artifacts from muscle activity.

### Magnetocardiography (MCG): Assessing Heart Function

Magnetocardiography (MCG) is a non-invasive technique that measures the magnetic fields produced by electrical activity in the heart. MCG can provide valuable information about heart rhythm, blood flow, and myocardial ischemia (reduced blood flow to the heart muscle). It is particularly useful for detecting subtle abnormalities that may not be apparent on an electrocardiogram (ECG).
