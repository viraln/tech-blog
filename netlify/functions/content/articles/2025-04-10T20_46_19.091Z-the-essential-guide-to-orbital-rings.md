## What are Orbital Rings and How Do They Work?

Orbital rings, often described as colossal, rotating structures in space, are proposed megastructures designed to provide a low-energy pathway for transporting cargo and potentially even people between the Earth's surface and orbit.  Unlike space elevators, which rely on a single tether anchored to the ground, orbital rings encircle the planet at a specific altitude, typically geosynchronous orbit (approximately 35,786 kilometers above the equator).  Their rotation generates centrifugal force, counteracting gravity and creating a stable structure.  ![A conceptual artist's rendering of an orbital ring around Earth](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1737072875530-cfc57792eb4b?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
