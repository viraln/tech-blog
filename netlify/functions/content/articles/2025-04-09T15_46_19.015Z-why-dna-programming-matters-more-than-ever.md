## Understanding the Fundamentals of DNA Programming

DNA programming, at its core, is the ability to design and synthesize DNA sequences with specific functionalities. This goes beyond traditional genetic engineering, encompassing the creation of entirely new biological systems and structures.  Think of it as programming a computer, but instead of silicon chips, we're using the building blocks of life.  This involves several key technologies:

* **DNA Synthesis:** The ability to chemically synthesize long, precise DNA strands, enabling the creation of custom genes, regulatory sequences, and entire genomes.  ![Microscopic image of DNA synthesis in progress](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1607799279861-4dd421887fb3?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
