## What is Biology? A Comprehensive Definition

Biology, at its core, is the scientific study of life and living organisms, including their physical structure, chemical processes, molecular interactions, physiological mechanisms, development, and evolution. ## Exploring the Human Body: Key Human Biology Topics

Human biology, a specialized branch of biology, delves into the intricacies of the human body, from the cellular level to the organ systems. Studying human biology provides a deep understanding of our physical and physiological processes. Key topics include:

* 🔑 **Genetics:** Inheritance, gene expression, and genetic disorders.
* ⚡ **Physiology:** How our body systems function, including the nervous, endocrine, and circulatory systems.
* ✅ **Anatomy:** The structure and organization of the human body.
* 🧠 **Immunology:** The body's defense mechanisms against disease.

The field of biology is constantly evolving.  In 2025, several key trends are shaping the future:
