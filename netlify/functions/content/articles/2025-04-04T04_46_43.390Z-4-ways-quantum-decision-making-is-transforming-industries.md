## 2. Quantum Probability: A New Language for Decision Analysis

At the heart of quantum decision making lies quantum probability. Unlike classical probability, which assumes events are independent and additive, quantum probability allows for interference effects. This means that the probability of an event can be influenced by other potential events, even if those events don't actually occur.

This "interference effect" is crucial for understanding phenomena like the "disjunction effect," where people choose one option in isolation and another when given both options together. Classical probability struggles to explain this, but quantum probability provides a natural explanation.

**Key Concepts in Quantum Probability:**

*   **Superposition:** Holding multiple possibilities in mind simultaneously. Think of Schrödinger's cat being both alive and dead until observed.
*   **Interference:** The interaction between different possibilities, influencing the final probability.
*   **Entanglement:** The correlation between two or more mental states, where the state of one influences the state of the others.
*   **Quantum Amplitudes:** Complex numbers that represent the probability of each possibility. The square of the amplitude gives the probability.

Quantum probability offers a more flexible and powerful framework for modeling human decision-making, capturing the complexities and nuances that classical models often miss.
