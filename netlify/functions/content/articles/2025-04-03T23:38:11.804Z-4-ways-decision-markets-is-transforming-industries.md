## What are Decision Markets and Why Should You Care?

Decision markets, also known as prediction markets or forecasting markets, are exchange-traded markets created for the purpose of making predictions. Participants buy and sell contracts that pay out based on the outcome of a future event. The price of a contract reflects the market's aggregated belief about the likelihood of that event occurring. Think of it as a real-time poll, constantly updated by the collective wisdom of its participants.

> **EXPERT TIP:** The key to a successful decision market lies in designing clear, unambiguous questions with well-defined outcomes.

But why are they important? Traditional forecasting methods, like expert opinions or statistical models, often suffer from biases and limitations. Decision markets, on the other hand, leverage the "wisdom of the crowd," aggregating diverse perspectives and knowledge to produce surprisingly accurate predictions. This accuracy can translate into significant advantages in strategic planning, risk management, and resource allocation.

*   **Reduced Bias:** By aggregating diverse perspectives, decision markets mitigate the impact of individual biases and cognitive distortions that can skew traditional forecasts.
