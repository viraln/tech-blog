## What is Living System Modeling?

Living system modeling encompasses a range of computational approaches used to simulate the behavior of complex, adaptive systems. Unlike traditional models that often assume linearity and equilibrium, living system models embrace complexity, incorporating feedback loops, non-linearity, and emergent properties.  Two prominent approaches are:

* **System Dynamics Modeling:** This focuses on the flows and feedback loops within a system, helping to understand how changes in one part affect the whole.  Think of it as mapping the circulatory system of a complex organism. ![Diagram illustrating a simple system dynamics model](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1467043153537-a4fba2cd39ef?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

* **Agent-Based Modeling (ABM) of Living Systems:** This approach simulates the interactions of individual agents (e.g., people, cells, animals) within a system, allowing researchers to observe emergent behavior from the bottom-up. Imagine simulating a flock of birds or the spread of an infectious disease. ![Visualization of agent-based model simulation](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1506377295352-e3154d43ea9e?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
