## Homemade Potato Chips Recipe: Control Your Ingredients!

Want to take control of your chip destiny? Making *homemade potato chips* is easier than you think! This recipe allows you to customize the flavors and control the ingredients, ensuring a healthier and tastier snack.

**Ingredients:**

*   2 large russet potatoes, thinly sliced (mandoline slicer recommended)
*   2 tablespoons olive oil (or avocado oil)
*   Salt and pepper to taste
*   Optional: Your favorite spices (e.g., paprika, garlic powder, onion powder)

**Instructions:**

**Step 1:** Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).

**Step 2:** Rinse the potato slices under cold water to remove excess starch. Pat them dry with paper towels. This will help them crisp up.

**Step 3:** In a large bowl, toss the potato slices with olive oil, salt, pepper, and any other desired spices.

**Step 4:** Spread the potato slices in a single layer on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.

**Step 5:** Bake for 15-20 minutes, or until the chips are golden brown and crispy. Flip halfway through baking.

**Step 6:** Let the chips cool completely on the baking sheet before serving.
