## 3.  Building Automation Systems: The Brain of the Operation

Self-evolving buildings rely on sophisticated building automation systems (BAS) to monitor their condition and respond to changes. These systems integrate various sensors, actuators, and control algorithms to manage energy consumption, environmental conditions, and structural integrity.  

* 🌡️ **Environmental control:**  BAS can adjust temperature, lighting, and ventilation based on occupancy, weather conditions, and energy prices, optimizing comfort and energy efficiency.
* 🚧 **Structural health monitoring:**  Sensors embedded within the building structure continuously monitor stress levels, vibrations, and other indicators of potential damage, alerting maintenance crews to potential issues before they become major problems.
* 🤖 **AI-driven optimization:**  Artificial intelligence algorithms analyze data from the BAS to predict maintenance needs, optimize energy consumption, and improve overall building performance.
