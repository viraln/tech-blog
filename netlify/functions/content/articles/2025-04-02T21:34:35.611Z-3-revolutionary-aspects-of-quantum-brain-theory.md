## Revolutionary Aspect #1: Quantum Coherence and Information Processing

One of the most revolutionary aspects of Quantum Brain Theory is the hypothesis that *quantum coherence* plays a crucial role in brain function. Quantum coherence refers to the ability of quantum systems to maintain a superposition of states, allowing them to explore multiple possibilities simultaneously. *   **Microtubules as Quantum Processors:** Pioneering work by Sir Roger Penrose and Stuart Hameroff suggests that microtubules, protein structures within neurons, could be the site of quantum computations. While controversial, Orch-OR has spurred significant research into the potential role of microtubules in quantum processing.

*   **Quantum Vibrations:** Recent research suggests that quantum vibrations within microtubules, and other brain structures, may be more significant than previously thought. *   **Quantum Tunneling:** This phenomenon, where particles pass through barriers they classically shouldn't be able to, could be occurring at synapses, potentially altering the efficiency and speed of neural transmission.
