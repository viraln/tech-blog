## What is Star Lifting? A Stellar Overview

Star lifting, at its core, is the process of extracting material from a star's surface. It's not about blowing up a star (that's more akin to a stellar engine, a different, even more ambitious concept). Instead, star lifting focuses on carefully and controllably siphoning off plasma, heavy elements, or even isotopes from the star's outer layers. This extracted material can then be used for a variety of purposes, from energy generation to manufacturing in space. Think of it as mining the ultimate natural resource: a star.
