## The Philosophical Underpinnings of Transhumanism

Transhumanist philosophy is diverse, but it generally revolves around several core tenets:

* **Belief in human potential:**  Transhumanists believe that humans are not limited by their current biological form and that we can and should strive to improve ourselves through technology.
* **Emphasis on reason and evidence:**  Decisions about human enhancement should be based on scientific evidence and rational assessment, not on dogma or superstition.
* **Concern for ethical considerations:**  The ethical implications of human enhancement technologies must be carefully considered and addressed to ensure responsible development and implementation.  This includes equitable access and avoidance of exacerbating existing social inequalities.
* **Focus on individual autonomy:**  Individuals should have the right to choose how they enhance their capabilities, within ethical and legal boundaries.
