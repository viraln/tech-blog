## Types of Vertical Farming Systems and Technologies

Several distinct vertical farming systems exist, each with its own advantages and disadvantages.  Choosing the right system depends on factors like budget, scale, crop type, and available space.

* **Hydroponics:** Plants are grown in nutrient-rich water solutions without soil.  This method conserves water and allows for precise nutrient delivery. 🔑
* **Aeroponics:** Plant roots are suspended in the air and periodically sprayed with a nutrient solution.  This maximizes oxygenation and promotes rapid growth. ⚡
* **Aquaponics:**  Combines hydroponics with aquaculture (raising fish).  Fish waste provides nutrients for the plants, creating a sustainable and closed-loop system.  ✅
* **Soil-based vertical farming:** While less common, some vertical farms utilize soil in stacked layers, offering a more traditional approach within a controlled environment.
