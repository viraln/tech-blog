## Stellar Engineering Projects:  Ambitious Visions Taking Shape

While fully realized stellar engineering projects remain largely in the future, several ambitious concepts are under active research and discussion.  These include:

* **Dyson Spheres and Megastructures:**  These hypothetical megastructures would encompass a star to capture its energy output almost entirely.  While a complete Dyson sphere remains a distant prospect, smaller, more manageable versions are theoretically possible. ![Diagram illustrating different types of Dyson structures](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1581092580497-e0d23cbdf1dc?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

* **Stellar Lifts:**  These colossal structures would extend from a planet's surface to a point beyond its gravitational pull, enabling efficient transportation of materials and energy between a planet and its star.
* **Starlifting:**  This involves extracting matter directly from a star, potentially providing an almost limitless supply of resources for future civilizations.
* **Planetary Engineering and Terraforming:**  While not strictly "stellar" engineering, modifying the atmospheres and environments of exoplanets to make them habitable for humans is intrinsically linked to the broader concept.
