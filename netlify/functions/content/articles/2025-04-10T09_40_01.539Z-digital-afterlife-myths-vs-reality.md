##  Mythbusting: Common Misconceptions about Digital Legacy

Many misconceptions surround the digital afterlife. Let's address some of the most prevalent ones:

* **Myth 1:  My accounts will automatically disappear.**  False.  Most platforms have policies regarding inactive accounts, but these usually involve suspension, not immediate deletion.  
* **Myth 2: My family will automatically inherit my digital assets.**  False.  Access to your accounts often requires legal documentation and may be difficult to obtain without proper planning.
* **Myth 3:  It's too complicated to plan for my digital afterlife.** False. While it requires planning, there are tools and services available to simplify the process.

##  Digital Legacy Planning: A Practical Guide

Planning for your digital afterlife is crucial.  It's not about avoiding death, but about ensuring your digital legacy reflects your wishes and protects your loved ones. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. **Inventory your digital assets:**  Create a comprehensive list of all your online accounts, including passwords (consider a secure password manager). ![A screenshot of a digital asset inventory spreadsheet](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512295767273-ac109ac3acfa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
