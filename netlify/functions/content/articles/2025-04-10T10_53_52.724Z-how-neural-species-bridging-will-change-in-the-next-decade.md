##  Neural Interface Species Bridging: The Technology Behind the Breakthrough

The core technology driving neural species bridging relies on increasingly sophisticated neural interfaces.  These interfaces, implanted or non-invasively attached, detect and record neural activity in animals.  Miniaturization and biocompatibility are crucial advancements, moving away from bulky, invasive implants towards smaller, less intrusive devices.

* 🔑 **Electroencephalography (EEG):**  Non-invasive method using scalp electrodes to detect brainwave patterns.  Limited spatial resolution but increasingly sophisticated algorithms are improving data interpretation.
* ⚡ **Electrocorticography (ECoG):**  Invasive method placing electrodes on the brain's surface. Offers better spatial resolution than EEG.
* ✅ **Utah Arrays:**  Microelectrode arrays implanted directly into the brain, providing high-resolution recordings of neural activity.  Ethical considerations regarding invasiveness are paramount.
