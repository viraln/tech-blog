## What is Entangled Cognition? Moving Beyond Shared and Distributed Cognition

Entangled cognition goes beyond the commonly understood concepts of "shared cognition" and "distributed cognition," although it builds upon them. While shared cognition implies a common understanding or mental model among individuals, and distributed cognition emphasizes the distribution of cognitive tasks across individuals and artifacts, entangled cognition focuses on the *dynamic, reciprocal, and often unconscious* ways in which our minds influence and are influenced by each other.

Think of it like this:

*   **Shared Cognition:** Everyone on the team understands the project goals.
*   **Distributed Cognition:** One person manages the budget, another handles the marketing, and a third oversees the development.
*   **Entangled Cognition:** During a brainstorming session, one person's seemingly random idea sparks a chain reaction, leading to a breakthrough solution that no one could have conceived of alone. The ideas *entangle*, building on each other in unpredictable and powerful ways.

## The Neuroscience of Entanglement: How Our Brains Connect

While the concept of entangled cognition might sound abstract, it has roots in neuroscience. Research suggests that our brains are wired for social interaction and cognitive interdependence.

*   **Mirror Neurons:** These neurons fire both when we perform an action and when we observe someone else performing the same action, allowing us to understand and empathize with others.
*   **Neural Synchrony:** Studies have shown that the brain activity of individuals engaged in collaborative tasks can become synchronized, suggesting a deeper level of cognitive connection.
*   **Emotional Contagion:** Emotions are contagious, and this emotional sharing can influence our cognitive processes, affecting our judgment and decision-making.

Understanding the neurological basis of entangled cognition can help us design environments and practices that foster deeper cognitive connections and enhance team performance.

## Strategies for Cultivating Entangled Cognition: A Practical Guide

Now, let's dive into the actionable strategies you can use to cultivate entangled cognition:

**1. Foster Psychological Safety:**

*   Create a culture where individuals feel safe to express their ideas, even if they seem unconventional or half-baked.
*   Encourage constructive feedback and avoid personal attacks.
*   Promote a growth mindset, where mistakes are seen as opportunities for learning and improvement.

**2. Embrace Diversity of Thought:**

*   Assemble teams with diverse backgrounds, perspectives, and skill sets.
*   Actively seek out dissenting opinions and challenge assumptions.
*   Use techniques like "devil's advocate" to encourage critical thinking and prevent groupthink.

**3. Facilitate Meaningful Dialogue:**

*   Design meetings that encourage active participation and open communication.
*   Use structured brainstorming techniques like "brainwriting" or "round robin" to ensure that everyone has a chance to contribute.
*   Encourage active listening and paraphrasing to ensure that everyone understands each other's perspectives.

**4. Leverage Visual Thinking:**

*   Use visual aids like whiteboards, diagrams, and sticky notes to externalize thoughts and ideas.
*   Encourage team members to sketch out their ideas and share them visually.
*   Use visual metaphors and analogies to help the team grasp complex concepts.

**5. Promote Embodied Cognition:**

*   Encourage physical movement and interaction during collaborative activities.
*   Use physical props or artifacts to represent ideas and concepts.
*   Create a comfortable and stimulating physical environment that promotes creativity and collaboration.
