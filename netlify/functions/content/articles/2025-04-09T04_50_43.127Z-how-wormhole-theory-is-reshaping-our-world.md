##  Understanding the Basics: What are Wormholes?

Wormhole theory, also known as the Einstein-Rosen bridge theory, proposes the existence of hypothetical tunnels through spacetime.  Imagine spacetime as a fabric, and a wormhole as a shortcut connecting two distant points, potentially even different universes.
