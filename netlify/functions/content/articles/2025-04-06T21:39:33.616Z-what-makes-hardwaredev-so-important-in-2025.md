As the number of connected devices increases, so does the potential for security vulnerabilities. Hardware security is no longer an afterthought; it's a fundamental requirement. In 2025, hardware-based security measures are becoming increasingly common, including secure enclaves, trusted platform modules (TPMs), and hardware-rooted identity.

⚡ **Critical Focus:** HardwareDev professionals need to understand the principles of secure hardware design, including protection against side-channel attacks, fault injection attacks, and reverse engineering.
