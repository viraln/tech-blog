*   **Dream Within a Dream:** This is a common motif in literature and film, where a character awakens from a dream only to discover they are still dreaming. This creates a sense of disorientation and raises questions about the nature of reality and perception.

*   **Hierarchical Realities:** This model suggests that realities exist in a hierarchy, with each level building upon the previous one. For example, our physical reality might be based on a more fundamental level of information or consciousness.
