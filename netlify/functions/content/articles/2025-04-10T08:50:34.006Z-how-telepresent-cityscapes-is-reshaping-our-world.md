##  Telepresence Technology for Cities: Applications Beyond Tourism

While virtual tourism is a major application, telepresent cityscapes offer far broader potential.  Consider these key applications:

* **Urban Planning & Development:** Architects and urban planners can use immersive simulations to visualize and test different design proposals, engaging stakeholders and the public in the process.  This collaborative approach leads to more informed and sustainable city development.
* **Real Estate:** Potential buyers can virtually tour properties, saving time and travel costs.  Developers can showcase projects to a global audience, boosting sales and attracting investment.
* **Education & Training:** Students can virtually explore historical sites, learn about different cultures, and experience urban environments firsthand, enhancing their learning experience.
* **Remote Work & Collaboration:** Businesses can use telepresence to conduct virtual meetings, site inspections, and training sessions, bridging geographical divides and fostering collaboration.
* **Accessibility & Inclusivity:** Telepresent cityscapes provide an accessible way for individuals with disabilities to explore cities they might otherwise be unable to visit.
