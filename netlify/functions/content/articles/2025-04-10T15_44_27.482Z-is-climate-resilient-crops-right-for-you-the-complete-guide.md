## Implementing Resilient Farming Techniques: Beyond the Seed

Choosing climate-resilient seeds is only half the battle. Implementing sustainable farming practices is crucial for maximizing their effectiveness.  These include:

1. **Conservation agriculture:**  Minimizing soil disturbance to retain moisture.
2. **Water harvesting:**  Collecting rainwater for irrigation.
3. **Agroforestry:**  Integrating trees into farming systems to improve soil health and water retention.
4. **Crop diversification:**  Planting a variety of crops to reduce risk and improve soil health.
5. **Precision agriculture:** Using technology to optimize resource use.
