##  Creating an Emergency Mental Health Plan Template

An emergency mental health plan is crucial for navigating acute crises. This plan should outline specific steps to take when you're experiencing a mental health emergency.

**Step 1:** Identify your warning signs.  What are the early indicators that your mental health is deteriorating?
**Step 2:**  List your immediate support network – who can you contact in a crisis?
**Step 3:**  Develop a list of coping strategies to use in the moment, including relaxation techniques and distraction methods.
**Step 4:**  Identify local emergency resources, such as crisis hotlines and mental health facilities.
**Step 5:**  Keep this plan readily accessible, perhaps on your phone or in a readily accessible location.  ![An example of a simple Emergency Mental Health Plan template](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1493836512294-502baa1986e2?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
