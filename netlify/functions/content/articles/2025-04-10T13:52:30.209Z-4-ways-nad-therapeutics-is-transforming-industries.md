## What is NAD+ Therapy?

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+) is a coenzyme found in every cell of your body.  It plays a crucial role in hundreds of metabolic processes, including energy production, DNA repair, and cellular signaling.  As we age, our NAD+ levels naturally decline, contributing to age-related decline in various bodily functions.  NAD+ therapy aims to replenish these dwindling levels, potentially mitigating the effects of aging and improving overall health.  This is achieved through various methods, including intravenous (IV) infusions, intramuscular (IM) injections, and oral NAD+ precursor supplements.  ![Microscopic image of NAD+ molecule](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1736034595749-b258a635ef71?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
