## What Are Living Processors?  A Deep Dive into Biological Computing

Living processors, also known as biological computers or living computers, utilize biological systems – cells, tissues, or even entire organisms – to perform computational tasks. Unlike traditional silicon-based processors, these systems operate on principles of biological information processing, offering unique advantages in terms of adaptability, self-repair, and energy efficiency.  The most prominent examples currently under development include DNA computing, where DNA strands are used to encode and manipulate data, and cellular computing, which leverages the inherent computational capabilities of living cells. ![Microscopic image of cells](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1524758631624-e2822e304c36?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
