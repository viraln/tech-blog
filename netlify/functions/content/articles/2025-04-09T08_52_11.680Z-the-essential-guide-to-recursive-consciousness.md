## What is Recursive Consciousness?

Recursive consciousness refers to the mind's ability to process information about itself, creating layers of self-awareness.  It's not simply being aware of your thoughts; it's being aware of your awareness of your thoughts, and so on, in a potentially infinite regress. This capacity allows for metacognition – thinking about thinking – enabling us to analyze our own thought processes, beliefs, and motivations.
