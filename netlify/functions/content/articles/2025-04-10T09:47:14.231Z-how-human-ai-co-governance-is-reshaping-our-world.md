## The Human-in-the-Loop Approach: Empowering Human Oversight

"Human-in-the-Loop" (HITL) AI emphasizes continuous human involvement throughout the AI lifecycle. This isn't just about human review at the end; it's about integrating human judgment at every stage, from data collection and algorithm design to decision-making and feedback loops.  This approach is particularly crucial in high-stakes applications like healthcare and autonomous vehicles.
