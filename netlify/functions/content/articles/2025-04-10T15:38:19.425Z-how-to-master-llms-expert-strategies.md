## What are LLMs and How Do LLMs Work?

LLMs are sophisticated algorithms trained on massive datasets of text and code.  They learn to predict the probability of the next word in a sequence, allowing them to generate human-quality text, translate languages, write different kinds of creative content, and answer your questions in an informative way.  Essentially, they learn patterns and relationships within the data, enabling them to perform complex linguistic tasks. ## LLM vs. Chatbot: Understanding the Nuances

While often used interchangeably, LLMs and chatbots are distinct.  Chatbots are applications built *using* LLMs (or other technologies) to interact with users.  LLMs provide the underlying language processing power, while chatbots provide the user interface and conversational flow.  Think of LLMs as the engine and chatbots as the car.
