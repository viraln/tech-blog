A fundamental question in alien emotion mapping is whether emotions are universal. Are the underlying neurological and physiological processes that give rise to emotions the same across different species, regardless of their origin?

This is where the concept of "emotional primitives" comes in. These are hypothesized to be the most basic building blocks of emotions, such as valence (positive or negative feeling) and arousal (level of activation).
