## The Rise of the Digital Afterlife: Why PDX Matters

This digital presence creates a unique challenge: what happens to all of this data after we're gone? ## Navigating Social Media After Death: Memorialization vs. Deletion

Social media presents unique challenges in the context of PDX. Platforms typically offer two main options for deceased accounts: memorialization and deletion.

*   **Memorialization:** This option preserves the account as a tribute to the deceased. The profile remains visible, but certain features, such as posting new content, are typically disabled. Memorialized accounts often include a special tag or banner indicating that the user has passed away.

*   **Deletion:** This option permanently removes the account and all associated data from the platform. This can be a preferred option for individuals who value privacy or who do not want their online presence to continue after death.

## Digital Estate Planning: Securing Your Digital Legacy

Digital estate planning is the process of making arrangements for the management and distribution of your digital assets after death. This involves creating a comprehensive plan that outlines your wishes and designates a trusted individual to carry out your instructions.

**Step 1:** Create a digital asset inventory. This list should include all your online accounts, passwords, and digital assets. Store this information securely, using a password manager or other secure method.
**Step 2:** Review the terms of service for each platform you use. Understand their policies regarding account management after death.
**Step 3:** Designate a digital executor. This person will be responsible for managing your digital assets after you die. Choose someone you trust and who is comfortable with technology.
**Step 4:** Create a digital will or include provisions for your digital assets in your traditional will. This document should outline your wishes regarding your digital legacy.
**Step 5:** Communicate your plan to your loved ones. Make sure they understand your wishes and know who your digital executor is.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Regularly update your digital asset inventory and review your digital estate plan to ensure it remains accurate and up-to-date.
