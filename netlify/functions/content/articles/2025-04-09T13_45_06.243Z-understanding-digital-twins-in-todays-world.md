## What is a Digital Twin?

At its core, a digital twin is a virtual representation of a real-world object, process, or system.  It's a dynamic, data-driven model that continuously updates itself based on real-time data from its physical counterpart.  This constant feedback loop allows for accurate simulations, predictive analysis, and informed decision-making.  Think of it as a digital mirror reflecting the state and behavior of its physical twin.  ![A graphic showing a physical object and its digital twin mirroring each other](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1592084284963-884b5d66f7cc?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
