## 5.  Cognitive Commons vs. Creative Commons: Key Differences

While both share a common goal of promoting open access, the Cognitive Commons and Creative Commons differ significantly in their scope and focus.

| Feature          | Cognitive Commons                               | Creative Commons                                   |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| **Focus**        | Knowledge, data, algorithms, methodologies      | Creative works (text, images, music, software)     |
| **Primary Goal** | Accelerate innovation, foster collaboration     | Protect creators' rights while promoting sharing   |
| **Licensing**    | Still evolving, often project-specific         | Established framework with various license types     |
| **Nature of Use**| Primarily collaborative, building upon existing work | Primarily consumption and redistribution            |
