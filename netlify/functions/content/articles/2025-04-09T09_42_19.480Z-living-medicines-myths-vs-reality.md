## Living Medicines Side Effects: Understanding Potential Risks

While generally safe, living medicines can have side effects, albeit usually milder than pharmaceuticals.  These can include:

* Allergic reactions (especially to herbal remedies)
* Digestive upset (with probiotics, for example)

Always start with a low dose and monitor your body's response.  Discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional if you experience adverse effects.
