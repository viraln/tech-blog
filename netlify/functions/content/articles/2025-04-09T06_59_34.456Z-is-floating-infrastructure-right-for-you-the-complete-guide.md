## Common Challenges and Solutions

Despite its potential, floating infrastructure faces several challenges:

* **Extreme Weather Conditions:**  Hurricanes, storms, and strong currents pose significant risks.  **Solution:** Robust design, advanced mooring systems, and real-time monitoring.
* **Environmental Impacts:**  Potential impacts on marine ecosystems need careful consideration.  **Solution:**  Environmental impact assessments, mitigation strategies, and sustainable design practices.
* **High Initial Costs:**  The upfront investment can be substantial.  **Solution:**  Government subsidies, innovative financing models, and collaborative partnerships.
* **Maintenance and Repair:**  Access for maintenance and repairs can be challenging.  **Solution:**  Remotely operated vehicles (ROVs), autonomous maintenance systems, and modular designs.
