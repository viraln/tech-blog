## Sustainable Symbiotic Buildings: Materials and Construction Techniques

The materials used in symbiotic building construction will undergo a significant change.  The focus will shift towards bio-based and recycled materials with minimal environmental impact.  Expect to see a surge in the use of:

* 🌱 **Mycelium-based composites:** These sustainable alternatives to traditional building materials offer excellent insulation and strength.
* ♻️ **Recycled timber and steel:**  Circular economy principles will be central, maximizing the reuse of existing materials.
* 🌿 **Hempcrete and other bio-based concretes:** These offer lower embodied carbon and superior thermal performance.
* 🪨 **Locally sourced materials:** Reducing transportation costs and emissions will be a priority.
