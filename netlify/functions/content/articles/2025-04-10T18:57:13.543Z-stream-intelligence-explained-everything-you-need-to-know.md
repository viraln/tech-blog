## What is Stream Intelligence?

Stream intelligence is the process of analyzing and interpreting data *as it's generated*, providing immediate insights and enabling real-time decision-making.  Unlike traditional batch processing which analyzes data in large chunks after it's collected, stream intelligence focuses on continuous, high-velocity data streams.  Think of it as moving from a rear-view mirror perspective to having a crystal ball – anticipating trends and reacting proactively.
