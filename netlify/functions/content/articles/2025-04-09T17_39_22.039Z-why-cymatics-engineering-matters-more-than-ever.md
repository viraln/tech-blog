## What is Cymatics Engineering?

Cymatics, at its core, is the study of visible sound.  Cymatics engineering takes this further, applying the principles of sound vibration to design, manufacturing, and various other applications. It involves creating and manipulating visual representations of sound waves – those mesmerizing patterns you've likely seen in videos – and using these patterns to understand and influence physical processes.
