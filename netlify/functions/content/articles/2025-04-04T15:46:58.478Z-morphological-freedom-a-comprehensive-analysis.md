## Body Autonomy Rights: A Foundation for Morphological Freedom

The concept of *body autonomy rights* is fundamental to the principle of morphological freedom. Body autonomy asserts that individuals have the right to control their own bodies and make their own decisions about their health and well-being, free from coercion or interference.

This right is grounded in fundamental human rights principles, such as:

*   **The right to privacy:** The right to make personal decisions about one's body without government intrusion.
*   **The right to self-determination:** The right to make one's own choices about one's life and future.
*   **The right to bodily integrity:** The right to be free from unwanted physical contact or interference.

However, body autonomy is not absolute. There are limits to this right, particularly when it comes to actions that may harm others. The challenge lies in finding the right balance between individual freedom and the protection of public health and safety.
