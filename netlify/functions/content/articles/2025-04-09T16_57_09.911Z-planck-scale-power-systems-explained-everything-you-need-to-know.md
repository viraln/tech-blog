## Understanding the Planck Scale and its Energy Potential

The Planck scale, named after Max Planck, represents the smallest possible length, time, and energy scales that have physical meaning.  These are not arbitrary units; they arise from fundamental constants of nature: the speed of light (c), the gravitational constant (G), and the reduced Planck constant (ħ).  The Planck energy (approximately 1.22 × 10<sup>19</sup> GeV) represents an incredibly immense amount of energy – the energy released in a collision between two particles at near-light speeds.
