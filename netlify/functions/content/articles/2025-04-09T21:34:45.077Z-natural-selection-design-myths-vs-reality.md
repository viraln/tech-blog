##  Nature-Inspired Design Patterns:  Unlocking Efficiency and Resilience

Nature has perfected numerous design patterns over millions of years.  Understanding these patterns can provide valuable insights for designers:

* **Hierarchical Structures:**  From tree branches to the human circulatory system, hierarchical structures optimize strength and efficiency while minimizing material use.
* **Self-Assembly:**  Many natural structures self-assemble, reducing manufacturing complexity and energy consumption.
* **Redundancy and Fail-Safe Mechanisms:**  Nature incorporates redundancy and fail-safe mechanisms to ensure survival even in adverse conditions.  This principle can be applied to designing more robust and resilient systems.
