Conscious perception is the process by which we actively select, organize, and interpret sensory information to create our subjective experience of reality. > **DID YOU KNOW?**  Studies using fMRI technology have shown that our brains actively filter out a vast amount of sensory information, focusing only on what's deemed relevant at any given moment. This selective attention is a key component of conscious perception.

Our perceptions are not merely reflections of objective reality; they are interpretations shaped by our individual mental frameworks.
