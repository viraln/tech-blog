## Brain-Computer Interface Applications: Peeking into the Future

Brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) represent a particularly fascinating and potentially transformative area of human-machine symbiosis. These interfaces allow us to communicate directly with computers using our thoughts, bypassing the need for traditional input devices.

Current BCI applications are largely focused on medical applications, such as:

*   **Restoring Movement:** Helping paralyzed individuals control prosthetic limbs or assistive devices with their thoughts.
*   **Communication:** Enabling individuals with locked-in syndrome to communicate through text or speech.
*   **Treating Neurological Disorders:** Exploring the potential of BCIs to treat conditions like epilepsy, Parkinson's disease, and depression.
