## Defining Thought Transmission: More Than Just Mind Reading

While often conflated with "mind reading," thought transmission is a more nuanced concept. Mind reading implies deciphering specific thoughts already formed in someone's mind. Thought transmission, on the other hand, suggests the direct transfer of thoughts, feelings, or images from one person (the sender) to another (the receiver), without the use of conventional sensory channels. This process, if proven, would bypass the need for verbal or non-verbal communication.

Telepathic communication can manifest in various forms, including:

*   **Emotional telepathy:** Experiencing the emotions of another person.
*   **Sensory telepathy:** Receiving sensory impressions (images, sounds, smells) from another person.
*   **Mental telepathy:** Receiving thoughts, ideas, or concepts from another person.

It's important to differentiate between genuine telepathy and other phenomena, such as:

*   **Empathy:** Understanding and sharing the feelings of another person, but not necessarily receiving direct thought transmissions.
*   **Intuition:** Gaining insights or knowledge without conscious reasoning.
*   **Cold reading:** Using general statements and observations to create the illusion of psychic abilities.
