## Common Challenges and Solutions

Despite its advantages, AI tutoring faces certain challenges:

* **Data privacy and security:** Protecting student data is crucial.  Choose reputable platforms with robust security measures.
* **Lack of human interaction:**  The absence of human interaction can limit social and emotional development.  Supplement AI tutoring with other learning activities.
* **Technical issues:**  Software glitches and internet connectivity problems can disrupt the learning process.  Ensure reliable internet access and have backup plans.
* **Algorithmic bias:**  AI algorithms can reflect existing biases, leading to unfair or inaccurate assessments.  Choose platforms that address algorithmic bias.
