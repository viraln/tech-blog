## Understanding the Effects of Moon Phases on Plants

The moon's gravitational pull influences tides, and many believe it similarly affects the flow of water within plants.  This impact is most noticeable in the sap flow, influencing growth and nutrient absorption.  Different moon phases are associated with varying levels of sap flow:

* **New Moon:** Minimal sap flow, ideal for sowing seeds and transplanting root crops. ## Lunar Gardening Techniques: Beyond the Calendar

Lunar agriculture extends beyond simply following a calendar.  Here are some key techniques to enhance your success:

* **Soil Preparation:**  Pay meticulous attention to soil health.  Rich, well-draining soil is crucial regardless of the moon phase.
* **Seed Selection:** Choose high-quality seeds from reputable sources.
