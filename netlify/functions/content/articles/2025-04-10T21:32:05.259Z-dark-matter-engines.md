## Understanding Dark Matter: The Invisible Fuel

Before we delve into the mechanics of dark matter engines, it's crucial to understand what dark matter actually is.  Scientists believe that approximately 85% of the matter in the universe is dark matter – a mysterious substance that doesn't interact with light or ordinary matter through electromagnetic forces.  ![Artistic depiction of a galaxy showing the distribution of dark matter](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1475070929565-c985b496cb9f?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
