## Understanding Neural Species Bridging: A Deep Dive

Neural species bridging, also known as species bridging technology, focuses on creating direct neural interfaces between different species. This isn't simply about observing animal behavior; it's about establishing a direct communication channel—a bridge—between their brains and ours (or other species' brains).
