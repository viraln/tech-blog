## 4.  The Role of Technology in Protecting Your Privacy

Technology plays a dual role in privacy: it facilitates data collection, but it also offers tools for protection.  Beyond VPNs, several technologies are emerging to enhance privacy:

* **Differential Privacy:** This technique allows data analysis without revealing individual identities.
* **Federated Learning:** This approach enables machine learning models to be trained on decentralized data, reducing the need to centralize sensitive information.
* **Homomorphic Encryption:** This allows computations to be performed on encrypted data without decryption, maintaining confidentiality.

These advancements are promising, but they are still evolving and not always readily accessible to the average user.
