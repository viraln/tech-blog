## What is Cellular Superposition?

Cellular superposition, a fascinating application of quantum mechanics, refers to the ability of individual cells – or even subcellular components – to exist in multiple states simultaneously. Unlike classical physics where a cell is either alive or dead, in a state of cellular superposition, a cell can exist in a probabilistic superposition of both states until measured.
