Cognitive biases are systematic errors in thinking that can lead to poor decision-making. These biases are often unconscious and can affect our judgments in a variety of situations, from personal relationships to business negotiations. The next decade will see a significant increase in the use of cognitive bias detection tools to help individuals and organizations identify and mitigate these biases.
