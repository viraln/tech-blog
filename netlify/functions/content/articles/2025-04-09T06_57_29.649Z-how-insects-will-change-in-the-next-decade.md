##  The Future of Insect Control: Beyond Pesticides

The overreliance on chemical pesticides has demonstrably negative consequences for both insect populations and human health. The next decade will see a growing shift towards more sustainable and integrated pest management (IPM) strategies. This includes biopesticides, biological control methods (e.g., introducing natural predators), and habitat manipulation to reduce pest populations.  Research into pheromone-based traps and other non-toxic control methods is also gaining momentum.

> **DID YOU KNOW?** Some fungi are being developed as effective biopesticides, targeting specific insect pests without harming beneficial insects or the environment.
