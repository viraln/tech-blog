## What is Fusion Management? Unpacking the Core Concept

Fusion management, at its core, is a strategic approach to integrating and optimizing all aspects of a business. It goes beyond simple business process management (BPM) and enterprise resource planning (ERP) by creating a truly unified business platform. Think of it as orchestrating all your business functions – from sales and marketing to operations and finance – into a cohesive, high-performing whole. It aims to break down silos, eliminate redundancies, and foster seamless collaboration across departments. It's about creating a synergistic environment where the whole is demonstrably greater than the sum of its parts.
