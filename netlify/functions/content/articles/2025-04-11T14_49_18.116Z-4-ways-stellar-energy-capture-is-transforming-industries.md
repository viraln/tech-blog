Several companies are actively developing SBSP technologies.  These systems involve deploying large arrays of solar panels in geostationary orbit, converting sunlight into electricity, and then beaming this energy wirelessly to Earth using microwave or laser technology.  While the upfront costs are substantial, the long-term benefits – including a 24/7 energy supply and reduced environmental impact – make SBSP a compelling investment for a sustainable future.
