## Stellar Engine Computing: Tapping the Star's Power Directly

*   **Orbital Data Centers:** Clusters of interconnected computing nodes orbiting a star, utilizing solar energy for power and the vacuum of space for cooling.

## The Challenges of Computing in Space: Radiation, Temperature, and Logistics

Despite its immense potential, stellar energy computing faces significant challenges. The harsh environment of space poses a number of hurdles:

*   **Radiation Exposure:** Space is filled with high-energy particles that can damage electronic components. Protecting computing systems from radiation requires specialized shielding and radiation-hardened hardware.
*   **Temperature Fluctuations:** The extreme temperature variations in space can cause components to expand and contract, leading to mechanical stress and potential failures. Thermal management is critical.
*   **Logistical Challenges:** Transporting equipment and personnel to space is expensive and complex. Developing efficient and reliable launch systems is essential for the widespread adoption of stellar energy computing.
*   **Maintenance and Repair:** Repairing or replacing components in space is a significant challenge. Designing systems for redundancy and remote maintenance is crucial.
