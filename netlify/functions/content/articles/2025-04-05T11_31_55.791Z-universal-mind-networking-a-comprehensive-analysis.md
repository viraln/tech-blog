## The Theoretical Foundations: Collective Consciousness and Group Consciousness

While often used interchangeably, Collective Consciousness and Group Consciousness have subtle distinctions.

*   **Collective Consciousness:** This term, popularized by sociologist Émile Durkheim, refers to the shared beliefs, ideas, and moral attitudes which operate as a unifying force within a society. It's a broader, more societal-level phenomenon.
*   **Group Consciousness:** This term typically refers to a more focused and intentional form of shared awareness within a specific group or community. It involves a deliberate effort to align thoughts, intentions, and actions towards a common goal.

Both concepts are fundamental to understanding Universal Mind Networking. They suggest that human minds are not merely individual entities but are capable of forming larger, more complex systems of awareness.
