## Understanding the Science Behind the Art

To truly appreciate genetic expression art, it's important to grasp the underlying science. DNA sequencing technologies have become increasingly accessible and affordable. This allows artists to obtain a person's genetic data, which is then processed and translated into visual elements. The specific genes chosen for representation, the color palettes used, and the overall artistic style are all carefully considered to create a meaningful and aesthetically pleasing artwork.

*   **DNA Sequencing:** The process of determining the precise order of nucleotides (A, T, C, and G) within a DNA molecule.
*   **Data Visualization:** Transforming raw genetic data into visual representations, such as graphs, charts, and abstract patterns.
*   **Artistic Interpretation:** The creative process of translating visual data into a final artwork, using various artistic mediums and techniques.
