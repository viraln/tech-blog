## Maximizing Small Space Design with Smart Storage Solutions

Storage is the cornerstone of successful folded space design.  In 2025, integrated storage solutions are more sophisticated than ever.  Consider these options:

1. **Vertical Storage:** Utilize wall-mounted shelves, tall bookcases, and vertical organizers to maximize vertical space.
2. **Under-Bed Storage:** Employ under-bed storage containers, drawers, or lift-up beds to utilize often-neglected space.
3. **Hidden Storage:** Integrate storage into furniture pieces, such as ottomans with hidden compartments or coffee tables with lift-up tops.
4. **Multi-functional Furniture:** Choose pieces that serve multiple purposes, such as a sofa bed or a storage bench.
