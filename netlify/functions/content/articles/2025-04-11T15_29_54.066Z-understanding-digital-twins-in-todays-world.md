## What is a Digital Twin?

A digital twin is a virtual replica of a physical entity, mirroring its properties, behaviors, and functionalities. It's not simply a 3D model; it's a dynamic, data-driven representation that evolves in real-time, reflecting changes in its physical counterpart.  Think of it as a constantly updating mirror, reflecting the state of a machine, a building, or even an entire city.  Data feeds from sensors, simulations, and other sources constantly update the digital twin, providing a comprehensive and accurate picture. ![A graphic showing a physical object and its digital twin mirroring each other](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1592084284963-884b5d66f7cc?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
