Self-organizing networks are a paradigm shift in network management. ## How Do Self-Organizing Networks Work? A Deep Dive

The magic of SONs lies in their sophisticated algorithms and protocols.  These algorithms enable nodes to:

1. **Self-configure:** Automatically discover and establish connections with other nodes.
2. **Self-optimize:** Adjust parameters (e.g., power levels, routing protocols) to maximize performance and resource utilization.
3. **Self-heal:** Detect and recover from failures without external intervention.
4. **Self-protect:** Implement security measures and defend against attacks.

This process typically involves the use of distributed consensus mechanisms, machine learning algorithms, and sophisticated communication protocols.  ![Diagram illustrating the self-configuration process in a SON](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1502139214982-d0ad755818d8?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
