## How Does Quantum Positioning Work?

Unlike traditional GPS, which relies on signals from satellites vulnerable to atmospheric interference and jamming, quantum positioning leverages the principles of quantum mechanics for superior accuracy and robustness.  Several approaches are being explored, including:

* **Quantum entanglement:**  This method uses entangled particles to establish a precise reference frame, enabling extremely accurate distance measurements even over vast distances.
