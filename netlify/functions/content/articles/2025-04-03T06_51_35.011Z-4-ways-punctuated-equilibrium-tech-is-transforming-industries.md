## Understanding Punctuated Equilibrium: Beyond Gradualism

Punctuated equilibrium, originally a concept from evolutionary biology, describes a pattern of long periods of stasis interrupted by short bursts of significant change. In the context of technology, this translates to extended periods where existing technologies improve incrementally, followed by sudden disruptions caused by entirely new innovations. These disruptions can render older technologies obsolete, creating new markets and reshaping existing ones. The traditional model of gradual technological evolution simply doesn't capture the speed and intensity of these transformations.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Understanding punctuated equilibrium is crucial for strategic planning. Assuming continuous, gradual improvement can leave your organization vulnerable to sudden disruptive forces.
