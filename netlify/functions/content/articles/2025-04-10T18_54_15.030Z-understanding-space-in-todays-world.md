##  Peering into the Cosmos: How Big is the Universe?

The sheer scale of the universe is almost incomprehensible.  Light years, the distance light travels in a year (approximately 5.88 trillion miles), are used to measure interstellar distances.  Even with this vast unit, the observable universe, the portion we can see with our current technology, stretches for billions of light-years.  The actual size of the universe, however, may be infinitely larger, a concept that continues to challenge our understanding of space and time.  Scientists are constantly refining their estimates of the universe's size and age, using advanced telescopes and sophisticated models.
