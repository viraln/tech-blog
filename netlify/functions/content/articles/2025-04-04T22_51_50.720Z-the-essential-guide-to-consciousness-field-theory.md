*   **Neuroscience:** Studies on the default mode network (DMN) in the brain, which is active during rest and self-referential thought, suggest that the brain may be more of a filter or receiver of information than a generator.
*   **Parapsychology:** Research into psi phenomena, such as telepathy, clairvoyance, and precognition, provides evidence for non-local consciousness and information transfer. Dean Radin's work, for example, shows statistically significant results in experiments testing precognition.
*   **Near-Death Experiences (NDEs):** NDEs often involve out-of-body experiences, vivid memories, and encounters with deceased loved ones, suggesting that consciousness can exist independently of the brain.
*   **Placebo Effect:** The powerful placebo effect demonstrates the mind's ability to influence physical reality, hinting at a deeper connection between consciousness and the physical world.

It's important to note that much of this evidence is controversial and requires further rigorous investigation. However, these findings offer intriguing clues that challenge conventional materialistic views and lend support to the plausibility of CFT.
