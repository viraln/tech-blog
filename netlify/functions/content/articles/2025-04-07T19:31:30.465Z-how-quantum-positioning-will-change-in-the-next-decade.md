## Unveiling the Quantum Positioning System: A New Paradigm

The Global Positioning System (GPS), while revolutionary, has inherent limitations. It relies on radio signals from satellites, making it vulnerable to jamming, spoofing, and signal degradation in urban canyons or indoor environments. Quantum positioning systems (QPS), on the other hand, leverage the principles of quantum mechanics to achieve unparalleled accuracy, security, and resilience. But how does quantum positioning work?

At its core, quantum positioning relies on phenomena like quantum entanglement and atomic clocks. Instead of relying on satellites, it can use ground-based quantum sensors or even leverage the properties of entangled particles to determine position with extreme precision. This eliminates the reliance on external signals, making it immune to jamming and spoofing.
