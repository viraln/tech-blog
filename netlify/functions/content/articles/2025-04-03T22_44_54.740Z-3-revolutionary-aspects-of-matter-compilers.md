3.  **Security Testing:** Assessing the device's security vulnerabilities and ensuring that it meets the required security standards.
4.  **Performance Testing:** Evaluating the device's performance under various conditions.

Matter compilers help developers navigate this complex process by providing tools and resources that simplify testing, identify potential issues, and ensure compliance with the Matter standards.
