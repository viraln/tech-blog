##  Buy Lunar Land? The Legal Landscape of Lunar Property

While various organizations claim to sell plots of lunar land, there's no legal framework currently in place that recognizes private ownership of celestial bodies.  The Outer Space Treaty of 1967 prohibits national appropriation of outer space, including the moon.  Therefore, any "purchase" of lunar land is largely symbolic and holds no legal weight.
