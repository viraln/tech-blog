## Living Systems Theory Applications in LIS Architecture

The architecture of a LIS is fundamentally different from traditional systems.  It's based on principles of modularity, decentralization, and feedback loops. This allows for greater flexibility and scalability.

* 🔑 **Modularity:**  The system is composed of independent, interacting modules, allowing for easy modification and expansion without disrupting the entire system.
* ⚡ **Decentralization:**  Data and processing are distributed across multiple nodes, enhancing resilience and preventing single points of failure.
* ✅ **Feedback Loops:**  Continuous monitoring and feedback mechanisms allow the system to self-regulate and adapt to changing conditions.  ![A visual representation of a modular, decentralized LIS architecture](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1506377295352-e3154d43ea9e?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
