## The False Promise and Damning Reality of Conversion Therapy

Conversion therapy, also known as reparative therapy or sexual orientation change efforts (SOCE), operates on the false premise that homosexuality and gender dysphoria are mental illnesses that can be "cured." This idea is vehemently rejected by every major medical and mental health organization, including the American Psychological Association, the American Psychiatric Association, and the World Professional Association for Transgender Health. Despite this overwhelming consensus, conversion therapy persists, often shrouded in secrecy and fueled by prejudice.

## Conversion Therapy: An Overview of Unethical Practices

Conversion therapy is rife with unethical practices that violate basic human rights and professional standards. These practices include:

*   **Misrepresentation of Effectiveness:** Practitioners often make false claims about the effectiveness of conversion therapy, despite the lack of scientific evidence to support these claims.
*   **Coercion and Manipulation:** Individuals are often pressured or manipulated into undergoing conversion therapy, either by family members, religious leaders, or therapists.
*   **Lack of Informed Consent:** Survivors are often not fully informed about the risks and potential harms of conversion therapy before undergoing the treatment.
*   **Exploitation of Vulnerability:** Conversion therapy often targets vulnerable individuals who are struggling with their identity or facing discrimination.
*   **Violation of Professional Ethics:** Conversion therapy violates the ethical codes of conduct of most major mental health organizations.

The lack of regulation and oversight in the conversion therapy industry allows these unethical practices to flourish, putting vulnerable individuals at risk.
