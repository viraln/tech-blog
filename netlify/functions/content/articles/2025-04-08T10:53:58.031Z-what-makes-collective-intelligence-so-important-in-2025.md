## Understanding the Core Principles of Collective Intelligence

At its heart, Collective Intelligence refers to the shared or group intelligence that emerges from the collaboration, collective effort, and competition of many individuals. It's more than just the sum of individual intelligence; it’s about creating a system where the whole is demonstrably greater than the parts. This phenomenon, often described as the "wisdom of the crowd," relies on several key principles:

*   **Diversity of Opinion:** A variety of perspectives helps to overcome individual biases and blind spots.
*   **Independence:** Individuals should form their opinions independently to avoid groupthink.
*   **Decentralization:** Distributing decision-making power across the group.
*   **Aggregation:** Mechanisms for combining individual judgments into a collective decision.

These principles are not new; they have been observed in nature for centuries, from ant colonies optimizing foraging routes to flocks of birds navigating complex environments. However, in 2025, technology enables us to scale these principles to unprecedented levels, unlocking the true potential of CI.
