## What is Tesseract Transit? A Conceptual Overview

Tesseract Transit, at its core, is a theoretical method of faster-than-light (FTL) travel that leverages the mathematical concept of a tesseract – a four-dimensional analogue of a cube. Imagine folding a piece of paper and piercing it with a pen. The pen creates two holes on the paper, even though the distance between the holes is insignificant in the folded dimension. Tesseract Transit attempts to replicate this principle on a cosmic scale, utilizing the higher dimensions to "fold" spacetime, creating a shortcut between two distant points.
