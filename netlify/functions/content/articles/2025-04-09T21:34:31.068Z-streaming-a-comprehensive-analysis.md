## How to Cancel Streaming Subscriptions: A Step-by-Step Guide

Unwanted subscriptions can quickly drain your budget.  Here's a simple guide on how to cancel most streaming services:

1. **Step 1:** Log in to your account on the streaming service's website (avoiding the app).
2. **Step 2:** Navigate to your "Account," "Settings," or "Profile" section.
3. **Step 3:** Find the "Subscription" or "Membership" tab.
4. **Step 4:** Locate the "Cancel Subscription" or "Cancel Membership" button.
5. **Step 5:** Follow the prompts to confirm your cancellation.  Note the cancellation date.

> **DID YOU KNOW?**  Many services offer a grace period after cancellation, allowing you to continue watching for a few days.
