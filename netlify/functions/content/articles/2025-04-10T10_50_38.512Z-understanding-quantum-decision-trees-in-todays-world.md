## What are Quantum Decision Trees?

Classical decision trees, a staple of machine learning, use a tree-like model of decisions and their possible consequences to classify data or predict outcomes.  Quantum decision trees leverage the principles of quantum mechanics, specifically superposition and entanglement, to enhance the decision-making process. Instead of binary decisions (yes/no), quantum decision trees can explore multiple possibilities simultaneously, leading to potentially faster and more accurate results.  This is achieved through the use of quantum bits (qubits), which can represent 0, 1, or a superposition of both. ![A simple illustration comparing a classical and quantum decision tree](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1729556227126-452c221f1ef4?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
