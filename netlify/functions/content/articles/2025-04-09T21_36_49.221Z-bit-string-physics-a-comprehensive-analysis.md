## What is Bit-String Physics?

Bit-string physics proposes that the universe can be described, and potentially simulated, using a finite set of bits. Instead of relying on continuous variables in traditional physics, this approach utilizes binary strings (sequences of 0s and 1s) to represent fundamental particles, forces, and spacetime itself.
