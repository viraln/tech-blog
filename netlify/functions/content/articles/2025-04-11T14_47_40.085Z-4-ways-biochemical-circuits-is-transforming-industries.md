## What are Biochemical Circuits?

Biochemical circuits, also known as metabolic pathways, are intricate networks of interconnected biochemical reactions within cells. These circuits process information and execute specific functions, much like electrical circuits in electronics.  They are composed of enzymes, proteins, DNA, RNA, and small molecules that interact in a highly regulated manner to achieve a particular biological outcome.  Understanding and manipulating these circuits is the cornerstone of metabolic engineering and synthetic biology.
