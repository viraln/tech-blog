## How Does Fractal Energy Work? Beyond the Hype

The operational principle of a fractal energy network relies on intelligent energy management systems (EMS) and advanced control algorithms. These systems constantly monitor energy demand and supply at different points in the network, dynamically adjusting energy flow to optimize efficiency and stability.

Think of it as a smart, self-organizing energy ecosystem. When one part of the network experiences a surge in demand, the EMS can automatically redirect energy from other sources or activate energy storage systems to meet the need.
