## What is Branching Intelligence?

Branching Intelligence explained simply, is a paradigm shift in AI, moving away from rigid, pre-programmed algorithms towards dynamic, adaptive systems. Unlike traditional AI, which relies on fixed decision trees and statistical models, Branching Intelligence mimics the human brain's ability to explore multiple possibilities and adapt to changing circumstances. It’s a system that can generate and evaluate different scenarios, essentially "branching out" to find the optimal solution. Think of it as AI that can think outside the box – or rather, build its own box as needed.
