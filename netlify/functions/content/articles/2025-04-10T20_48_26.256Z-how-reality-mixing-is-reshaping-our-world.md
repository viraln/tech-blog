## What is Reality Mixing?

Reality mixing goes beyond the capabilities of AR by allowing for real-time interaction between the physical and digital worlds. While AR overlays digital information onto the real world, reality mixing allows digital objects to interact with, and be influenced by, the physical environment. Think of it as a more sophisticated, dynamic form of AR where digital objects can react to real-world physics, like shadows, reflections, and even collisions with physical objects. ![A visual representation of reality mixing showing a digital object interacting with a physical object, casting a realistic shadow.](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1618413712329-45c9ebbe8e53?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## Reality Mixing vs. Augmented Reality: Key Differences

While often confused, reality mixing and augmented reality are distinct technologies.  Here's a clear comparison:

| Feature          | Reality Mixing                               | Augmented Reality                             |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Interaction      | Bidirectional: Physical and digital interact | Primarily unidirectional: Digital overlays     |
| Physics          | Digital objects obey real-world physics      | Digital objects often ignore physics         |
| Complexity       | Higher                                      | Lower                                       |
| Processing Power | More demanding                             | Less demanding                               |
