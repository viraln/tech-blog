## Biohacking Supplements: A Deeper Dive

Biohacking supplements can play a supporting role in your journey, but they're not a magic bullet.  Always consult a healthcare professional before incorporating supplements into your routine.  Popular choices include:

* **Nootropics:**  These cognitive enhancers aim to improve focus, memory, and mental clarity.  Examples include Lion's Mane mushroom, Bacopa Monnieri, and L-theanine.
* **Adaptogens:**  These help the body adapt to stress and improve resilience.  Examples include Ashwagandha, Rhodiola Rosea, and ginseng.
* **Omega-3 Fatty Acids:**  Essential for brain health and overall well-being.
* **Magnesium:**  Crucial for muscle function, sleep, and stress management.
