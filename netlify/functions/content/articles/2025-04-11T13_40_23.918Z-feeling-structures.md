## How to Identify Your Feeling Structures: A Practical Guide

Identifying your feeling structures requires self-reflection and a willingness to explore your inner world.  Here's a step-by-step approach:

**Step 1:**  **Identify a Trigger:** Begin by recalling a recent situation that evoked a strong emotional response.

**Step 2:** **Name the Primary Emotion:** What was the dominant emotion you felt? (e.g., anger, sadness, fear)

**Step 3:**  **Explore Associated Feelings:**  What other feelings accompanied the primary emotion?  (e.g., if the primary emotion was anger, you might have also felt frustration, resentment, or helplessness).

**Step 4:** **Uncover Underlying Thoughts:** What thoughts went through your mind during and after the event? These thoughts often reinforce or shape your emotional experience.

**Step 5:** **Notice Bodily Sensations:** Pay attention to any physical sensations associated with your emotions.  This might include muscle tension, rapid heartbeat, or changes in breathing.

**Step 6:** **Connect to Memories:**  Are there any past experiences that resonate with this current emotional response? This step helps uncover the deeper roots of your feeling structures.
