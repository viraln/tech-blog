The core components include:

* 🔑 **Text-to-Speech (TTS) Engine:** Converts written text into phonetic representations.
* ⚡ **Acoustic Model:** Generates waveforms based on the phonetic information, mimicking human speech patterns.
* ✅ **Voice Synthesis:**  Combines the acoustic model output with voice characteristics to produce audible speech.
