## 1. Revolutionizing Materials Science: Designing the Impossible

Traditional materials science relies heavily on experimentation and complex simulations. Cellular Automata offer a complementary, and often faster, approach to understanding and designing new materials. By modeling the interaction of atoms or molecules as cells within a CA, researchers can simulate the growth, crystallization, and behavior of materials under various conditions.
