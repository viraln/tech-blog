## Space-Based Solar Power: A Clean Energy Revolution

Space-based solar power (SBSP) offers the potential to revolutionize our energy production.  Satellites equipped with solar panels in geostationary orbit could collect significantly more solar energy than ground-based systems, transmitting it wirelessly to Earth.  While still in its developmental phase, SBSP holds immense promise for a sustainable future.  Several companies are actively researching and developing different SBSP technologies.

Did you know?  The amount of solar energy that reaches the Earth's atmosphere is far greater than our current energy needs.  SBSP harnesses this untapped potential.
