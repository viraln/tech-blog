## What are Higher-Order Connections?

Higher-order connections represent the cognitive process of linking seemingly unrelated ideas, concepts, or information to create new understanding, solve complex problems, and foster innovation.  It's about moving beyond surface-level comprehension to a deeper, more nuanced understanding.  Instead of simply memorizing facts, higher-order thinking involves analyzing, evaluating, creating, and applying knowledge in novel ways.  This differs significantly from lower-order thinking, which focuses primarily on recall and comprehension.

## Higher-Order Thinking vs. Higher-Order Connections: A Crucial Distinction

While often used interchangeably, higher-order thinking and higher-order connections are distinct yet related concepts. Higher-order thinking encompasses a broader range of cognitive skills, including analysis, evaluation, synthesis, and application. Higher-order *connections* are a specific *component* of higher-order thinking, focusing on the process of establishing links between different pieces of information.  It's the "connecting the dots" aspect of higher-order thinking.
