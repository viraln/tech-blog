## What is a Fitness Landscape?  A Deep Dive into the Theory

The term "fitness landscape," also known as an adaptive landscape, is a conceptual model visualizing the relationship between a genotype (or phenotype) and its fitness.  Imagine a three-dimensional graph: one axis represents the genotype (the genetic makeup or a set of traits), another represents the phenotype (the observable characteristics), and the third represents the fitness (the reproductive success or performance).  The peaks on this landscape represent optimal genotypes or phenotypes, while valleys represent less successful ones. ![A 3D visualization of a simple fitness landscape with peaks and valleys](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1598601065215-751bf8798a2c?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
