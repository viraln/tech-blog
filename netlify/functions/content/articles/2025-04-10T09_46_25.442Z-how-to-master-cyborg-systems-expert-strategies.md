## How Do Cyborg Systems Work?  A Look Under the Hood

The functioning of cyborg systems depends heavily on the specific application. However, several core principles apply:

**Step 1:** **Signal Acquisition:**  Sensors capture data from the body (neural signals, muscle activity, etc.).
**Step 2:** **Signal Processing:**  This data is processed and interpreted by a control unit, often a computer.
**Step 3:** **Actuator Control:**  The processed information is used to control actuators (motors, artificial muscles, etc.) in the prosthetic or device.
**Step 4:** **Feedback Control:**  Sensors provide feedback to the control unit, allowing for continuous adjustment and refinement of the system's actions.  ![A diagram illustrating the signal flow within a typical cyborg system](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1536826340696-adb3811c02c1?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
