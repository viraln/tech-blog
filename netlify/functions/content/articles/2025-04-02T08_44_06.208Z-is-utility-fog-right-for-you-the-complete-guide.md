## Understanding Utility Fog: Programmable Matter Defined

Utility fog, a term coined by Dr. J. Storrs Hall, is a hypothetical collection of incredibly small robots – often called "foglets" or "nanobots" – that can collectively form macroscopic objects or shapes. These foglets would be able to communicate with each other and move relative to one another, effectively acting as programmable matter. The key to utility fog lies in its ability to rearrange itself into virtually any shape or structure, on demand.

Each foglet, theoretically, would be equipped with:

*   **Processors:** For computation and communication.
*   **Actuators:** To move and connect to neighboring foglets.
