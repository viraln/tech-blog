## Unraveling the Mysteries of Bioluminescence: How it Works

Bioluminescence, the production and emission of light by living organisms, is the cornerstone of biological light networks.  This phenomenon isn't some magical trick; it's a complex biochemical process involving an enzyme called luciferase, which catalyzes a reaction with a luciferin substrate, producing light.  ![Microscopic image of bioluminescent bacteria](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1564325724739-bae0bd08762c?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
