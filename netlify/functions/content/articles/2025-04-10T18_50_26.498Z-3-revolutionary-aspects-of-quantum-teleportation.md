## 2. How Does Quantum Teleportation Work?  A Step-by-Step Guide

Quantum teleportation doesn't beam matter from point A to point B like in Star Trek. Instead, it transfers the quantum state of one particle to another, effectively creating a perfect copy at a distant location.  This process involves several steps:

1. **Entanglement:** Two particles (B and C) are entangled and separated.
2. **Measurement:** The particle whose state we want to teleport (particle A) is measured along with one of the entangled particles (B).
3. **Classical Communication:** The results of this measurement are sent classically (e.g., via fiber optic cable or radio waves) to the location of particle C.
4. **Reconstruction:** Based on the received classical information, a quantum gate operation is performed on particle C, reconstructing the original quantum state of particle A.

This process destroys the original quantum state of particle A.  What arrives at the destination is a perfect replica, but the original is gone. This is a crucial distinction.

⚡ **Important Note:**  The speed of teleportation is limited by the speed of classical communication, not by the speed of light itself.
