## How Does Sense Expansion Work?  The Mechanisms Behind Sensory Augmentation

* **Sensory Substitution:**  This involves converting information from one sensory modality into another.  For example, a device might translate visual information into tactile sensations, allowing a visually impaired individual to "see" through touch.
