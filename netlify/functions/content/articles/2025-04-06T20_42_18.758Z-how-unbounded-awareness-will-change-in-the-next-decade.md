## Unbounded Awareness Meditation: A Deeper Dive

Unbounded awareness meditation is a specific type of meditation that aims to directly cultivate the experience of non-duality. It often involves practices that encourage you to question the boundaries of self and other, to recognize the underlying unity of all things.

Here's a simple unbounded awareness meditation practice you can try:

1.  Find a quiet and comfortable place to sit or lie down.
2.  Close your eyes and take a few deep breaths, relaxing your body.
3.  Notice the sensations of your body – the feeling of your breath, the contact of your body with the chair or ground.
4.  Now, expand your awareness to include the sounds around you – the rustling of leaves, the hum of the refrigerator.
5.  Continue to expand your awareness to include all the sensations, thoughts, and emotions that arise within you.
6.  Notice that all these experiences are arising within the field of awareness.
7.  Ask yourself: "Who is aware of these experiences?"
8.  Rest in the awareness itself, without trying to grasp or control it.
9.  Notice that awareness is boundless, infinite, and unconditioned.
10. Simply rest in this unbounded awareness, allowing yourself to dissolve into the vastness of being. ![Unbounded Awareness visualization](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1674574124976-a56d9052c2f8?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
