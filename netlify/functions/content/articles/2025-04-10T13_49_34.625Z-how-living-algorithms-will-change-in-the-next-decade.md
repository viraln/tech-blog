## What Are Living Algorithms?

Living algorithms are computational systems that mimic the adaptive and evolutionary processes found in nature. Unlike traditional algorithms, which follow pre-programmed instructions, living algorithms are dynamic, evolving, and capable of self-optimization. They leverage biological principles like natural selection, genetic algorithms, and swarm intelligence to solve complex problems.  Think of them as "programmable ecosystems" where the "code" is constantly adapting and improving.
