## The Casimir Effect: Evidence of Quantum Vacuum Forces

The Casimir effect provides compelling experimental evidence for the reality of the quantum vacuum. It demonstrates an attractive force between two uncharged, perfectly conducting parallel plates placed in a vacuum. This force arises because the plates alter the zero-point energy density between them. Only certain wavelengths of virtual photons can exist in the space between the plates, leading to a lower energy density compared to the space outside the plates. This pressure difference results in the plates being pushed together.

✅ **Key Takeaway:** The Casimir effect isn't just a theoretical curiosity; it's a measurable force that confirms the existence of quantum vacuum fluctuations.
