## What is Panpsychism, Really?

At its core, panpsychism proposes that consciousness is not a late-stage emergent property of complex biological systems (like brains), but rather a fundamental and ubiquitous feature of reality. In other words, *everything* possesses some degree of consciousness, however rudimentary it may be. This doesn't mean your coffee mug is having existential thoughts, but it does suggest that it possesses a basic level of awareness or proto-consciousness.

Panpsychism offers an elegant solution to the "hard problem of consciousness," which is the difficulty of explaining how subjective experience arises from purely physical processes. If consciousness is fundamental, like mass or charge, then it doesn't need to be "created" – it's already there, inherent in the fabric of the universe.

Think of it like this: We know that matter has intrinsic properties like mass and charge. Panpsychism simply adds consciousness to the list of fundamental properties.
