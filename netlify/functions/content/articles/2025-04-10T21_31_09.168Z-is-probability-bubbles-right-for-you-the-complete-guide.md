## What Are Probability Bubbles?  Decoding the Phenomenon

Probability bubbles, unlike traditional asset bubbles driven solely by speculation, are characterized by a disproportionate increase in asset prices fueled by a confluence of factors.  These factors include genuine positive news, investor sentiment, and often, a degree of self-fulfilling prophecy.  The key differentiator is the perceived probability of a positive outcome; this probability becomes inflated beyond rational justification, leading to unsustainable price appreciation.
