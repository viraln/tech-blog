## The Technology Behind the Telepathic Revolution: BCIs and Beyond

The core technology driving this revolution is the rapidly advancing field of brain-computer interfaces (BCIs).  BCIs use various methods to decode neural signals, ranging from non-invasive techniques like EEG (electroencephalography) to more invasive approaches involving implanted electrodes.  Current research focuses on improving signal accuracy, reducing latency, and enhancing the overall user experience.  The goal is to create a truly intuitive and seamless interaction, allowing for complex commands and nuanced information exchange.

> **EXPERT TIP:** While invasive BCIs offer higher resolution data, non-invasive methods are gaining traction due to their ease of use and reduced risk.  The future likely lies in a combination of both approaches.
