## Large-Scale Neural Network Training: Challenges and Solutions

Training galaxy-scale neural networks presents unique challenges.  The sheer volume of data and the distributed nature of the computation necessitate innovative approaches.

* **Data Management:**  Handling and distributing petabytes, or even exabytes, of data efficiently is a major hurdle.  Techniques like distributed file systems and data sharding are essential.
* **Communication Overhead:**  Communication between nodes can become a bottleneck.  Optimized communication protocols and specialized hardware are crucial to minimize latency.
* **Synchronization:**  Ensuring consistency and synchronization across numerous nodes during training is vital.  Advanced synchronization algorithms are needed to maintain accuracy and prevent divergence.
* **Fault Tolerance:**  The system must be resilient to node failures.  Redundancy and error-correction mechanisms are essential for continuous operation.
