## What is Zero-Energy Bond Formation?

Zero-energy bond formation refers to a chemical reaction where the overall change in enthalpy (ΔH) is essentially zero.  This means the energy absorbed in breaking existing bonds is precisely compensated by the energy released when new bonds are formed.  Unlike exothermic reactions (which release energy) or endothermic reactions (which absorb energy), zero-energy bond formation represents a delicate energetic balance.
