## 1. The Fluid Shift Phenomenon:  More Than Just a Swollen Face

One of the most immediate and dramatic effects of zero gravity is the redistribution of bodily fluids.  In the presence of Earth's gravity, fluids pool in the lower extremities.  In microgravity, this pooling ceases, causing fluids to shift upwards towards the head and torso. ![Illustration showing fluid shift in zero gravity](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1505243542579-da5adfe8338f?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

But the fluid shift is far more complex than a cosmetic issue.  It affects cardiovascular function, intracranial pressure, and even visual acuity.  The increased pressure in the head can lead to Space Adaptation Syndrome (SAS), characterized by nausea, vomiting, and headaches.  Research in 2025 is focusing on countermeasures like specialized suits and lower body negative pressure (LBNP) devices to mitigate these effects.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  Understanding the fluid shift is crucial for developing effective pre-flight training protocols and in-flight countermeasures to minimize the risks associated with SAS.
