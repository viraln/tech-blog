## Understanding the Collective Unconscious

The collective unconscious, as defined by Carl Jung, is a universal layer of the human psyche containing inherited predispositions to respond to the world in certain ways. It's distinct from the personal unconscious, which holds repressed memories and experiences unique to an individual. The collective unconscious is populated by archetypes – universal, symbolic patterns that manifest in myths, dreams, and cultural expressions across the globe. Think of the Hero, the Mother, the Shadow – these are archetypal figures that resonate deeply within us.
