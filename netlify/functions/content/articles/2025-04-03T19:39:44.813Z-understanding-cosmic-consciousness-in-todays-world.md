## The Science Behind the Spiritual: Exploring the Neurological Correlates

While cosmic consciousness is often discussed in spiritual terms, there is growing interest in understanding its neurological correlates. Research suggests that certain brainwave patterns, particularly alpha and theta waves, are associated with meditative states and altered states of consciousness.
