## What is Edge Intelligence?

Edge intelligence represents the convergence of edge computing and artificial intelligence (AI).  Instead of sending all data to a central cloud server for processing, edge intelligence performs computations and analyses closer to the data source—at the "edge" of the network. This "edge" can be anything from an IoT device to a local server within a factory.  Think of it as giving intelligence to your devices, allowing them to make decisions autonomously and react instantly to changing conditions. ![Illustration of data processing at the edge vs. the cloud](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1665170284743-ba507dd955bd?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## Edge Intelligence vs. Cloud Computing: A Clear Comparison

While cloud computing remains a cornerstone of modern technology, edge intelligence complements it by addressing its limitations.  Let’s compare the two:

| Feature          | Edge Intelligence                               | Cloud Computing                                  |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| **Data Processing** | Local, near the data source                     | Centralized, in the cloud                        |
| **Latency**       | Extremely low                                   | Can be high, depending on network conditions     |
| **Bandwidth**     | Reduced bandwidth requirements                  | High bandwidth requirements                      |
| **Cost**          | Potentially lower infrastructure costs initially | Potentially higher infrastructure and bandwidth costs |
| **Security**      | Enhanced data security, reduced risk of breaches | Increased risk of data breaches in transit/storage |
| **Scalability**   | Scalability can be more complex to manage initially| Generally easier to scale                      |
