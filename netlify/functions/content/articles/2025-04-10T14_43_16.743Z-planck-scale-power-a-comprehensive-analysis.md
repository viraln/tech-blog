## Understanding Planck Units and Planck Scale Physics

Before diving into Planck scale power, we need to establish a foundation in Planck units.  These units, developed by Max Planck, represent the smallest possible meaningful measurements in physics. They are derived from fundamental physical constants: the speed of light (c), the gravitational constant (G), the reduced Planck constant (ħ), and the Boltzmann constant (k<sub>B</sub>). > **DID YOU KNOW?** The Planck time, approximately 5.39 × 10<sup>−44</sup> seconds, is the time it would take light to travel the Planck length.  It represents the smallest unit of time that has physical meaning according to current theories.

Planck energy, expressed as E<sub>P</sub> = √(ħc<sup>5</sup>/G), represents the energy equivalent of a Planck mass.
