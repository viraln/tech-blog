## What is Augmented Evolution? (Augmented Evolution Definition)

Augmented evolution, in its simplest form, is the process of accelerating and directing the evolutionary trajectory of organisms using technological interventions.  Unlike natural selection, which relies on random mutations and environmental pressures, augmented evolution employs human-guided techniques to enhance desirable traits and eliminate undesirable ones. This involves manipulating the genetic code, selectively breeding organisms, and even utilizing advanced technologies like CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing to achieve specific outcomes.  ![A microscopic image of CRISPR technology in action](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1624620178246-011ab2e9f0f3?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
