## What is Adaptive Intelligence?

Adaptive intelligence (AI) is the ability of a system to modify its behavior based on real-time data and feedback. Unlike traditional artificial intelligence, which relies on pre-set algorithms and fixed parameters, adaptive AI systems can learn from experience, adjust their strategies, and optimize their performance dynamically. Think of it as AI that doesn't just *execute* instructions, but *understands* its environment and *improves* its approach accordingly.

## Adaptive Learning in AI: The Engine of Evolution

Adaptive learning is the core mechanism that enables adaptive intelligence. It involves using machine learning algorithms to continuously learn from data and improve performance over time. There are several key techniques used in adaptive learning:

1.  **Reinforcement Learning:** The system learns by trial and error, receiving rewards for correct actions and penalties for incorrect ones. This is particularly useful for optimizing complex processes where the optimal strategy is not known in advance.
2.  **Online Learning:** The system learns continuously as new data becomes available, allowing it to adapt to changing conditions in real-time.
3.  **Transfer Learning:** Knowledge gained from solving one problem is applied to another, related problem. This can significantly speed up the learning process and improve performance in new situations.
4.  **Evolutionary Algorithms:** These algorithms mimic the process of natural selection, using genetic operators such as mutation and crossover to evolve solutions over time.

> **DID YOU KNOW?** Some adaptive AI systems use a combination of these techniques to achieve even greater levels of adaptability and performance.
