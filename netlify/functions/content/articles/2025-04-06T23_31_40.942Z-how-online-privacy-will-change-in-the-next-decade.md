## The Rise of Privacy-Enhancing Technologies (PETs)

Privacy-Enhancing Technologies (PETs) are a set of tools and techniques designed to protect online privacy by minimizing the amount of personal data that is collected, processed, or disclosed. These technologies are becoming increasingly sophisticated and will play a crucial role in safeguarding online privacy in the next decade.

Examples of PETs include:

*   **Encryption:** Encrypting your data makes it unreadable to unauthorized parties.
*   **Anonymization:** Removing identifying information from data to protect individual privacy.
*   **Differential Privacy:** Adding noise to data to protect the privacy of individuals while still allowing for statistical analysis.
*   **Homomorphic Encryption:** Performing computations on encrypted data without decrypting it.

## Best Online Privacy Tools & How to Improve Online Privacy (Practical Guide)

Protecting your online privacy doesn't have to be complicated. Here's a practical guide to help you improve your online privacy:

**Step 1:** **Use a strong password manager:** Generate and store complex passwords for all your online accounts.

**Step 2:** **Enable two-factor authentication:** Add an extra layer of security to your accounts by requiring a second form of verification, such as a code sent to your phone.

**Step 3:** **Use a Virtual Private Network (VPN):** Encrypt your internet traffic and mask your IP address.

**Step 4:** **Use a privacy-focused browser:** Consider browsers like Brave or Firefox with privacy-enhancing extensions.

**Step 5:** **Review and adjust your privacy settings:** Regularly check your privacy settings on social media platforms and other online services.

**Step 6:** **Use a secure email provider:** Opt for email providers like ProtonMail or Tutanota that offer end-to-end encryption.

**Step 7:** **Be mindful of what you share online:** Think before you post and avoid sharing sensitive information publicly.
