## What is Quantum Foam?

Quantum foam, a concept born from quantum field theory, describes the spacetime at its smallest scales.  Instead of being a smooth, continuous entity as depicted in classical physics, it’s theorized to be a frothy, turbulent sea of virtual particles constantly fluctuating into and out of existence.  These particles, governed by the Heisenberg uncertainty principle, briefly violate energy conservation for incredibly short periods. ![Artistic representation of quantum foam, showing a chaotic, bubbling texture](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1488590528505-98d2b5aba04b?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
