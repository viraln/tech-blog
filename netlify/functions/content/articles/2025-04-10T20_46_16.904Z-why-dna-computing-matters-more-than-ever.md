## What is DNA Computing?

DNA computing harnesses the immense data storage capacity and parallel processing capabilities of deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA). Unlike silicon-based computers that process information sequentially, DNA computers perform billions of calculations simultaneously, making them ideally suited for complex problems like drug discovery, materials science, and cryptography.  ![A microscopic image of DNA strands](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1579154392128-bf8c7ebee541?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

The process involves encoding data into DNA sequences, performing computations through biochemical reactions, and then decoding the results.
