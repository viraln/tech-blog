## What Exactly Are Physical Voxels?

At its core, a physical voxel is a three-dimensional representation of a digital voxel. Think of it as a 3D pixel. In digital modeling, voxels are the fundamental units that make up a 3D object. Physical voxels take this concept into the real world, manifesting as tangible blocks that can be manipulated, arranged, and interacted with. They can be made from a variety of materials, including plastic, wood, metal, or even more advanced materials like shape-memory alloys. The key is that each voxel represents a specific location in 3D space.
