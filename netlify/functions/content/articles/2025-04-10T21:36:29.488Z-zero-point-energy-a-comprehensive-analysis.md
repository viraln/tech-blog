## Zero-Point Energy Explained: Quantum Fluctuations and the Casimir Effect

The Casimir effect provides compelling experimental evidence for ZPE. When two uncharged, perfectly conducting plates are placed extremely close together in a vacuum, a measurable attractive force is observed. This force is attributed to the difference in zero-point energy fluctuations between the space inside and outside the plates.  ![Diagram illustrating the Casimir effect](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1473341304170-971dccb5ac1e?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
