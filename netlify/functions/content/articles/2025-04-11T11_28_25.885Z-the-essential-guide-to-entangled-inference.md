## Understanding the Fundamentals of Entangled Inference

Entangled inference harnesses the unique properties of quantum entanglement – a phenomenon where two or more particles become linked, sharing the same fate regardless of the distance separating them.  This interconnectedness allows for the creation of powerful computational models capable of processing information in ways impossible for classical computers.  Unlike classical inference, which relies on probabilistic reasoning based on independent variables, entangled inference leverages the correlated states of entangled particles to perform computations with significantly enhanced efficiency and accuracy.  ![Illustration of entangled particles](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1622321789745-991d19be0e90?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
