## Observer Consciousness Explained: Different Perspectives

Several interpretations attempt to explain the observer effect and its implications for consciousness.  These include:

* **The Copenhagen Interpretation:** This dominant interpretation suggests that the act of measurement "collapses" the wave function, forcing the quantum system into a definite state.  However, it doesn't fully explain *how* consciousness plays a role.
* **Many-Worlds Interpretation:** This proposes that every quantum measurement causes the universe to split into multiple branches, each representing a different outcome.  Consciousness experiences only one branch, but all possibilities exist.

* **Consciousness-Causes-Collapse Theories:** These theories directly link consciousness to the collapse of the wave function, suggesting that consciousness is a fundamental aspect of reality.
