## Understanding Cognitive Meta-Processes: The "Thinking About Thinking" Revolution

Cognitive meta-processes, often simply called *metacognition*, represent our ability to think about our thinking. It's the executive control system of the brain, overseeing processes like planning, monitoring, evaluating, and self-regulating our thoughts, learning, and problem-solving strategies.
