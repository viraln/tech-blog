## What is Claytronics?

Claytronics is a nascent field of research focused on creating programmable matter –  collections of tiny, independent robotic components, called claytronic modules or "claytronic atoms," that can self-assemble and reconfigure themselves into virtually any three-dimensional shape.  These modules communicate wirelessly, allowing for complex coordinated movements and transformations.  Think of it as a microscopic swarm of robots working together to create macroscopic objects on demand.  ![Microscopic image of claytronic modules](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

Unlike traditional robots with fixed structures, claytronics offers unprecedented flexibility.  A claytronics device could be a phone one moment, a small robot the next, and then morph into a complex tool or even a piece of art. This dynamic adaptability opens doors to numerous applications across various sectors.
