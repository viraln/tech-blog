## Preserving Your Digital Identity: Creating a Digital Will

A digital will, often called a digital legacy plan, is a crucial document outlining your wishes for your online presence after your death. This isn't a legal will, but a separate document specifying who should access and manage your various accounts, what should be done with your data, and how your online memorial should be handled.  This should be stored securely and shared with your designated executor.

**Step 1:**  List all your online accounts and assets.
**Step 2:**  Designate an executor – someone trustworthy and tech-savvy.
**Step 3:**  Specify your wishes for each account (deletion, access, archiving, etc.).
**Step 4:**  Outline instructions for your online memorial (if desired).
**Step 5:**  Store the document securely (consider a digital vault or a physical copy with your legal will).
