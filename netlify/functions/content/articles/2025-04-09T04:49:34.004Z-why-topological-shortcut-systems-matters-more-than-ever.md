## What are Topological Shortcuts?

Topological shortcut systems leverage the principles of topology—the study of shapes and spaces—to identify and utilize "shortcuts" within complex datasets or networks.  Instead of traversing every node or edge in a network, these systems cleverly identify optimal paths by analyzing the underlying topological structure.  This allows for significantly faster analysis and more efficient navigation, particularly in datasets with high dimensionality or intricate interconnections.
