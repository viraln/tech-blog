## What is Immersive Learning?  A Deep Dive

Immersive learning leverages cutting-edge technologies like virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) to create engaging and interactive learning experiences. Unlike traditional methods, immersive learning transcends the limitations of textbooks and lectures, placing learners directly within the subject matter.
