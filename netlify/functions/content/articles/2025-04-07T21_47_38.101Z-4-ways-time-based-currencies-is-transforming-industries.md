## 1. Redefining Value: From Monetary to Human Capital

At its core, a time-based currency, also known as time banking or a local exchange trading system (LETS), assigns value to time itself. One hour of service provided equals one time credit, regardless of the skill or profession. This simple yet profound concept disrupts traditional economic hierarchies and democratizes access to goods and services. It acknowledges that everyone has valuable skills and contributions to offer, fostering a sense of equality and mutual respect.
