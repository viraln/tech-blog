## How to Perform Quantum Fluctuation Tapping: A Step-by-Step Guide

While seeking guidance from a certified practitioner is recommended, here's a basic guide to get you started with Quantum Fluctuation Tapping:

**Step 1:** **Identify the Issue:** Clearly define the issue, emotion, or belief you want to address. Be specific and honest with yourself.

**Step 2:** **Rate the Intensity:** On a scale of 0 to 10 (where 10 is the most intense), rate the current intensity of the issue.

**Step 3:** **The Setup Statement:** While tapping on the Karate Chop point (the fleshy part of the side of your hand), repeat the following statement three times: "Even though I have this [issue], I deeply and completely accept myself." You can modify this statement to suit your specific needs.

**Step 4:** **The Tapping Sequence:** Tap on each of the following meridian points approximately 5-7 times while focusing on the issue. Breathe deeply and consciously throughout the process.

   *   **Eyebrow (EB):** The beginning of the eyebrow, closest to the nose.
   *   **Side of the Eye (SE):** On the bony area at the side of the eye.
   *   **Under the Eye (UE):** On the bony area under the eye.
   *   **Under the Nose (UN):** The area between the bottom of the nose and the upper lip.
   *   **Chin Point (CP):** The crease in the chin.
   *   **Collarbone Point (CB):** The point where the collarbone meets the sternum.
   *   **Under the Arm (UA):** About four inches below the armpit.
   *   **Top of the Head (TH):** The crown of the head.

**Step 5:** **The Reminder Phrase:** While tapping on each point, repeat a reminder phrase that encapsulates the essence of the issue. For example, "This anxiety," or "This limiting belief."

**Step 6:** **Re-evaluate:** After completing the tapping sequence, take a deep breath and re-evaluate the intensity of the issue on a scale of 0 to 10.

**Step 7:** **Repeat as Needed:** If the intensity is still high, repeat the tapping sequence until you experience a noticeable shift. You can also adjust your setup statement and reminder phrase to address different aspects of the issue.

**Step 8:** **Quantum Integration (Optional):** Visualize yourself surrounded by a field of positive energy. Affirm: "I am now aligning with the highest potential of my being. I am open to receiving healing and transformation."
