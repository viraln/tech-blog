## What are Decision Markets?

Decision markets are prediction markets specifically designed to forecast the likelihood of specific outcomes related to internal business decisions. Unlike traditional prediction markets that focus on external events (e.g., election results, product launches), decision markets harness the knowledge and insights within an organization to improve internal planning and resource allocation.  They work by allowing employees to "invest" (often with virtual currency) in the outcome they believe is most likely.  The market price reflects the collective wisdom of participants, offering a dynamic and continuously updated probability assessment. ![A graphic illustrating the flow of information and investment in a decision market](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1642543348781-ed9c6d67ed20?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## How Do Decision Markets Work?

The mechanics are surprisingly straightforward:

1. **Define the Question:**  Clearly articulate the decision requiring prediction.  This needs to be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).  Example: "Will Project X launch on time by December 31st, 2025?"

2. **Establish the Market:** Create a platform (either using dedicated software or a simpler system) where participants can trade shares representing different potential outcomes.

3. **Participant Engagement:** Encourage employees with relevant expertise to participate, providing incentives if needed.

4. **Trading Begins:** Participants buy and sell shares based on their belief in the likelihood of each outcome.  Prices fluctuate reflecting the collective assessment.

5. **Outcome Resolution:** Once the event occurs, the market settles, and participants receive payouts based on the actual outcome and their investments.

> **EXPERT TIP:** The success of a decision market hinges on the quality of the question posed and the engagement of knowledgeable participants.  A poorly defined question will yield unreliable results.

## Prediction Markets vs. Decision Markets: Key Differences

While both prediction markets and decision markets use similar mechanisms, there are critical distinctions:

| Feature          | Prediction Market                               | Decision Market                                      |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| **Focus**        | External events (e.g., elections, weather)      | Internal business decisions                           |
| **Participants** | Public or a defined group                        | Primarily internal employees                          |
| **Objective**    | Forecasting external events                    | Improving internal decision-making and resource allocation |
| **Incentives**   | Financial gains or losses                        | Often virtual currency, recognition, or other incentives |
