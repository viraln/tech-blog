Augmented evolution relies on several key technologies and techniques, each with its own potential and ethical considerations. Understanding these pillars is crucial to grasping the scope of this transformative field.

*   **Nanotechnology:** The manipulation of matter at the atomic and molecular level opens up possibilities for creating new materials, devices, and therapies. Nanobots could be used to repair damaged tissues, deliver drugs directly to cells, or even enhance cognitive function.
