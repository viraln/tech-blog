## What are Wormholes, and How Might They Enable Faster-Than-Light Communication?

Wormholes, also known as Einstein-Rosen bridges, are hypothetical tunnels through spacetime predicted by Einstein's theory of general relativity. ![Artistic depiction of a wormhole connecting two distant points in space](https://images.unsplash.com/uploads/1413222992504f1b734a6/1928e537?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

The concept hinges on the warping of spacetime itself, creating a bridge that bypasses the usual limitations imposed by the speed of light.  While purely theoretical at present, their existence remains a captivating area of research.

> **DID YOU KNOW?** The term "wormhole" was coined by the physicist John Wheeler in the 1950s, inspired by the visual analogy of a worm burrowing through an apple, connecting two points on its surface.
