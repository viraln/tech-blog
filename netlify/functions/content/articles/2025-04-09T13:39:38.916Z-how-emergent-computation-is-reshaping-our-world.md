## What is Emergent Computation?

Emergent computation is a computational approach where complex, unpredictable behavior arises from the interaction of simpler components. Unlike traditional computation, which follows pre-programmed instructions, emergent systems self-organize and adapt based on interactions within the system and with their environment.  Think of a flock of birds: each bird follows simple rules, yet the flock as a whole exhibits complex, coordinated behavior. This collective intelligence is a hallmark of emergent computation.  It's about letting the system discover solutions, rather than explicitly programming them. ![Illustration of a flock of birds demonstrating emergent behavior](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1518133835878-5a93cc3f89e5?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
