## 1. Revolutionizing Healthcare with Emotional AI

Healthcare is at the forefront of affective computing adoption. The ability to detect and interpret a patient's emotional state opens doors to personalized and proactive care.     *   **Example:** Apps are being developed that use a smartphone's camera to analyze facial expressions during video calls, providing therapists with valuable insights into a patient's emotional state.
