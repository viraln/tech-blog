## What is Programmable Matter?

Programmable matter refers to materials whose physical properties, such as shape, stiffness, and conductivity, can be altered dynamically and autonomously through external control.  This control can be achieved through various mechanisms, including magnetic fields, electric fields, light, or even chemical signals.  Unlike traditional materials, programmable matter possesses a level of adaptability and responsiveness that opens up unprecedented possibilities across diverse sectors.
