## Expert Tips and Recommendations for Reducing Food Waste at Home

1. **Plan your meals:** Create a weekly meal plan to avoid impulse purchases and reduce food spoilage. ✅
2. **Shop smart:** Buy only what you need, focusing on seasonal produce and avoiding excessive bulk purchases. 🥕
3. **Store food properly:** Utilize **best food storage containers** to maintain freshness and prevent spoilage. 🧊
4. **First In, First Out (FIFO):**  Use older items before newer ones to prevent spoilage. 🔄
5. **Learn to preserve food:** Explore methods like freezing, canning, and pickling to extend the shelf life of perishable items. 🍓
6. **Compost food scraps:**  Start a home compost bin or utilize municipal composting programs. 🌎
7. **Use leftovers creatively:** Get creative with leftover ingredients to avoid food waste. 🍽️
