## What is Spatial Computing?  Understanding the Fundamentals

Spatial computing transcends the limitations of traditional computing by understanding and interacting with the physical world in 3D. It's not just about displaying information on a screen; it's about integrating digital content seamlessly into our real-world environments. This involves capturing, processing, and understanding spatial data – information about the location, orientation, and relationships between objects in space. Key technologies powering spatial computing include:

* 🔑 **Augmented Reality (AR):** Overlays digital information onto the real world, enhancing our perception.
* ⚡ **Virtual Reality (VR):** Creates completely immersive, simulated environments.
* ✅ **Mixed Reality (MR):** Blends the real and virtual worlds, allowing interaction with both simultaneously.
* 🌐 **Computer Vision:** Enables computers to "see" and interpret the world through cameras and sensors.
* 🗺️ **3D Mapping and Modeling:** Creates accurate digital representations of physical spaces.
