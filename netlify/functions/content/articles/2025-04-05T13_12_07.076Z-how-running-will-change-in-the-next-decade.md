## The Rise of Personalized Running: Data-Driven Performance

The days of generic training plans are fading. In the next decade, personalized running will become the norm, fueled by advancements in wearable technology and artificial intelligence. We're already seeing sophisticated smartwatches and fitness trackers that monitor heart rate variability (HRV), sleep patterns, and even sweat composition. This data is then used to create highly customized training schedules that adapt to your individual physiology and lifestyle.
