**2. Focused Attention Techniques (Pomodoro Technique):**

*   **Step 1:** Choose a task you want to focus on.
*   **Step 2:** Set a timer for 25 minutes.
*   **Step 3:** Work on the task with unwavering focus until the timer rings.
*   **Step 4:** Take a 5-minute break.
*   **Step 5:** Repeat steps 2-4 four times, then take a longer break of 20-30 minutes.
*   **Benefits:** Breaks down overwhelming tasks into manageable chunks, enhances focus, and improves productivity.

**3. Sensory Deprivation (Floatation Therapy):**

*   **Description:** Spending time in a sensory deprivation tank filled with Epsom salt water, eliminating external stimuli.

## Time Dilation Effects in Extreme Environments

While we've focused on manipulating *perceived* time, it's important to acknowledge the real-world phenomenon of time dilation. This occurs when time passes at different rates for observers in different frames of reference, particularly at high speeds or in strong gravitational fields, as predicted by Einstein's theory of relativity.

This is particularly relevant in 2025 as space exploration becomes more accessible. Astronauts traveling at high speeds experience time dilation, albeit a very small amount. However, as we venture further into space and potentially encounter stronger gravitational fields, these effects will become more pronounced.

> **DID YOU KNOW?** Astronaut Scott Kelly, who spent nearly a year on the International Space Station, aged slightly less than his twin brother Mark, who remained on Earth, due to time dilation effects.
