## Understanding Architectural Adaptation: More Than Just a Renovation

Architectural adaptation, also known as adaptive reuse architecture, encompasses the conversion of existing buildings or structures into new functions while retaining their original character. It's far more than a simple renovation; it's a holistic approach that considers historical context, structural integrity, sustainability, and the needs of modern users.  This process can involve repurposing industrial spaces into lofts, transforming old schools into boutique hotels, or converting abandoned factories into modern office complexes. ![A striking before-and-after image showcasing a successful adaptive reuse project](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
