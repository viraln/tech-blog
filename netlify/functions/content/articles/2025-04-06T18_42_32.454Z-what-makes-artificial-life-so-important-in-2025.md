## Evolutionary Robotics: Building Robots That Learn and Adapt

Evolutionary robotics is a fascinating subfield of Alife that combines robotics with evolutionary algorithms. The goal is to design robots that can learn and adapt to their environment without explicit programming. This is achieved by using evolutionary algorithms to automatically optimize the robot's morphology (shape) and control system.

Here’s how it works:

1.  **Generate a Population of Robots:** Create a diverse set of robot designs, each with slightly different physical characteristics and control parameters.
2.  **Evaluate Robot Performance:** Test each robot in a simulated or real-world environment and measure its performance on a specific task.
3.  **Select the Best Robots:** Choose the robots that performed best and use them as "parents" for the next generation.
4.  **Create New Robots:** Generate new robot designs by combining and mutating the "genes" of the parent robots.
5.  **Repeat:** Repeat steps 2-4 for many generations, allowing the robot population to evolve and improve over time.
