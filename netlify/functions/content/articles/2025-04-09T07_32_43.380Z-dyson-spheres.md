## What is a Dyson Sphere?

A Dyson sphere, first conceptualized by physicist Freeman Dyson in his 1960 paper, isn't a solid shell. Instead, it represents a hypothetical megastructure designed to encompass a star and capture nearly all of its energy output.  This energy could then be harnessed to power a civilization far beyond our current capabilities.  The most common conceptualization is a **Dyson swarm**, a vast collection of independent solar power satellites orbiting the star in a complex, potentially dynamic arrangement. ![Dyson Spheres visualization](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1664670013330-1389e470f44a?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
