## Understanding Gravity: A Foundation for Manipulation

Before we explore the possibilities of manipulating gravity, we must first understand what it is.  Gravity, as described by Einstein's general theory of relativity, is not a force but a curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy.  The more massive an object, the greater its effect on the surrounding spacetime, resulting in a stronger gravitational pull.  ![Illustration of spacetime curvature caused by a massive object](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1503002415073-d8dae15b3cca?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
