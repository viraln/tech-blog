## What is Climate Intelligence?

Climate intelligence (CI) is the synthesis of climate science, data analytics, and business strategy.  It's about turning raw climate data into actionable insights that inform decision-making across all sectors.  Unlike simply understanding climate change, CI focuses on translating scientific projections into practical applications for businesses, governments, and individuals.  This includes identifying climate-related risks, opportunities, and the best strategies for mitigation and adaptation.  Think of it as a sophisticated early warning system, providing a clearer picture of future climate scenarios and their potential impact.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Don't confuse climate intelligence with climate science. While climate science provides the foundational data, CI transforms that data into actionable intelligence for specific applications.

## Climate Risk Assessment: Identifying and Mitigating Threats

Climate risk assessment is a core function of CI. It involves systematically identifying, analyzing, and evaluating the potential negative impacts of climate change on an organization or community. This includes:

* **Physical Risks:** Direct impacts like extreme weather events (floods, droughts, wildfires), sea-level rise, and changes in temperature and precipitation patterns.
* **Transition Risks:**  Risks associated with the shift towards a low-carbon economy, including policy changes, technological advancements, and shifts in consumer preferences.
* **Liability Risks:** Potential legal and financial consequences arising from climate-related damages or failures to adequately address climate risks.
