## What Are Reality Filters?  A Deep Dive

Reality filters, in their simplest form, are digital overlays applied to the real world, typically through a camera lens or augmented reality (AR) device.  They alter our perception of reality by adding digital elements – images, text, animations, 3D models – to the live camera feed.
