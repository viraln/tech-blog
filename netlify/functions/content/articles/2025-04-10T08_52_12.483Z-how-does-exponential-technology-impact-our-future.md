## What is Exponential Technology?

Exponential technology isn't just about incremental improvements.  It's characterized by growth that doubles, triples, or even increases by orders of magnitude over relatively short periods.  Think of Moore's Law, which predicted the doubling of transistors on a microchip roughly every two years. This kind of growth creates a cascading effect, leading to breakthroughs in seemingly unrelated fields. ![A graph illustrating exponential growth compared to linear growth](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1488590528505-98d2b5aba04b?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
