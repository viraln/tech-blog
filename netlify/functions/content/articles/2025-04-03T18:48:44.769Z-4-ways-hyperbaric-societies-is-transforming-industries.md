## 1. Setting Standards and Guidelines for Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT)

One of the most critical functions of hyperbaric societies is establishing and maintaining rigorous standards for hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT). These standards ensure patient safety and efficacy across diverse applications. Without these guidelines, the inconsistent application of HBOT could lead to compromised treatment outcomes and increased risks.

Organizations like the Undersea and Hyperbaric Medical Society (UHMS) and the European Underwater and Baromedical Society (EUBS) play a pivotal role in this area. The UHMS, for example, publishes a comprehensive list of approved indications for HBOT, based on thorough reviews of scientific literature. This list helps physicians determine when HBOT is an appropriate and effective treatment option.
