## Mastering the Strokes: A Guide to Freestyle and Beyond

Freestyle, or crawl, is the most popular competitive stroke, but mastering other strokes like backstroke, breaststroke, and butterfly offers a varied and challenging workout.  Proper technique is paramount for efficiency and injury prevention.

### How to Swim Freestyle: A Beginner's Guide

1.  **Body Position:** Maintain a streamlined, horizontal position in the water.
2.  **Arm Stroke:**  Alternate arm movements, extending your arm forward and pulling it back through the water.
3.  **Leg Kick:**  Use a flutter kick, keeping your legs relatively straight and kicking from your hips.
4.  **Breathing:**  Coordinate your breathing with your arm strokes, exhaling underwater and inhaling as you rotate your head to the side.
