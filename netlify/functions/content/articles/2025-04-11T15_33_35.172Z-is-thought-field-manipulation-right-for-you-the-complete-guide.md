## What is Thought Field Therapy (TFT)?

Thought Field Therapy, also known as Thought Field Manipulation, is a psychological acupressure technique developed by Roger Callahan in the 1980s.  It's based on the premise that negative emotions are linked to disruptions in the body's energy system.  TFT practitioners use a series of gentle tapping sequences on specific meridian points (similar to those used in acupuncture) to restore energy flow and alleviate emotional distress.  ![A diagram showing the meridian points used in TFT](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1498408040764-ab6eb772a145?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

Unlike traditional talk therapy, TFT focuses on the physical manifestation of emotional issues. The process involves identifying the specific problem, rating the intensity of the emotion, and then applying the tailored tapping sequence.  It aims to quickly reduce the intensity of negative emotions, allowing individuals to process and resolve underlying issues more effectively.

> **EXPERT TIP:** TFT is not a replacement for professional mental health care. It's best used as a complementary therapy alongside traditional approaches.
