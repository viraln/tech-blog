## Understanding Neuralink's Core Technology: A Primer

At its heart, Neuralink is developing a brain-computer interface (BCI) – a device that allows direct communication between the brain and external devices like computers. The current Neuralink device, often referred to as the "Link," is a small, coin-sized implant packed with thousands of tiny electrodes. These electrodes are surgically implanted into the brain and are designed to record and stimulate neural activity.

Unlike earlier BCI technologies, Neuralink aims for a minimally invasive approach, using a robotic system to precisely insert the electrodes while minimizing damage to brain tissue. This precision is crucial for long-term stability and functionality. The data collected by the electrodes is then transmitted wirelessly to an external device, allowing for real-time analysis and control.
