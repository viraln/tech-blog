## Molecular Dynamics Simulation: Unveiling Molecular Behavior

Molecular dynamics (MD) simulation uses computational methods to model the movements and interactions of molecules over time.  This powerful technique allows researchers to study complex systems at the atomic level, gaining valuable insights into processes like protein folding, drug-receptor interactions, and material properties.  Advancements in computing power have made MD simulation increasingly accessible and powerful.
