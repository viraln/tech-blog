## How ITNs Work: The Physics of Low-Energy Trajectories

The core principle behind ITNs lies in exploiting "weak stability boundaries" – regions in space where gravitational forces from multiple celestial bodies nearly cancel each other out.  By carefully timing a spacecraft's launch and trajectory, mission planners can use these boundaries to "slingshot" the craft from one planet to another, using minimal propellant.  This is achieved through a series of gravitational assists, effectively harnessing the planets' orbital momentum.  These weak stability boundaries form intricate pathways, creating the "network" aspect of ITNs. ![Diagram illustrating a spacecraft utilizing a gravitational assist](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1521350837072-770f027a7877?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
