## Axiology and Ethics:  A Necessary Distinction

While closely related, axiology and ethics are distinct concepts. Ethics primarily focuses on moral principles and right conduct, while axiology encompasses a broader range of values, including aesthetic, epistemological, and economic values.  Understanding this difference is crucial for creating a holistic and robust framework.  For instance, an ethical framework might address issues of fairness and honesty, while an axiological framework might consider the value of innovation, creativity, and customer satisfaction.
