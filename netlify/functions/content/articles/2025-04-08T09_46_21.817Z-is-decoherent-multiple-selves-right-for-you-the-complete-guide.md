## The Quantum Physics Behind Multiple Realities

Understanding DMS requires a grasp of basic quantum mechanics. Here are some core principles:

*   **Superposition:** Quantum particles can exist in multiple states simultaneously until measured. Imagine a coin spinning in the air; it's neither heads nor tails until it lands.
*   **Quantum Measurement:** The act of observing or measuring a quantum particle forces it to "choose" a single state. The spinning coin lands on either heads or tails.
*   **Many-Worlds Interpretation (MWI):** Instead of collapsing into a single state, the universe splits, with each possible outcome realized in a separate universe. The coin lands on heads in one universe and tails in another.
*   **Decoherence:** Interaction with the environment causes quantum systems to lose their coherence, preventing interference between different states. This is why we don't see quantum effects in our everyday macroscopic world.

The link to DMS is that our brains, at a fundamental level, operate according to the laws of quantum mechanics. Therefore, quantum events within our brains could potentially trigger branching realities, leading to the creation of multiple selves.
