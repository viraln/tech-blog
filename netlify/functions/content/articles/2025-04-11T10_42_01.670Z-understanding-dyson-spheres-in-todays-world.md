## What is a Dyson Sphere? A Comprehensive Explanation

A Dyson sphere, in its purest form, is a hypothetical megastructure encompassing a star to capture its energy output.  Dyson himself envisioned a swarm of independent solar collectors, orbiting the star, rather than a single solid shell.
