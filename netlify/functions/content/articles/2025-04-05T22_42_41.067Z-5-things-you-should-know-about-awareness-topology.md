## 1. Awareness Topology Definition: Seeing the Bigger Picture

Awareness Topology, at its core, is a method for visualizing and understanding the relationships between different elements within a system or environment. Think of it as a map, but instead of just showing physical locations, it illustrates the connections, dependencies, and interactions between various entities. The Awareness Topology Definition extends beyond simple asset inventories; it's about understanding the context and relationships between those assets. This includes data flows, communication channels, dependencies, and potential vulnerabilities.
