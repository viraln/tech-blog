Traditional geolocation methods, primarily relying on GPS, have inherent limitations. Wave-function geolocation, however, bypasses these limitations by utilizing quantum sensors and the principles of quantum mechanics. These sensors exploit quantum phenomena like superposition and entanglement to achieve unparalleled precision.

## 2. Expanding Geolocation Capabilities: Navigating the Unnavigable

One of the most significant limitations of GPS is its reliance on satellite signals. This makes it unusable in environments where satellite signals are blocked, such as indoors, underground, or underwater. Wave-function geolocation offers a solution to this problem by operating independently of satellite infrastructure.

This independence is crucial for a variety of applications:

*   **Underground Mining:** Providing accurate location data for miners and equipment in environments where GPS is unavailable.
*   **Subterranean Infrastructure:** Mapping and monitoring underground pipes, cables, and tunnels with pinpoint accuracy.
*   **Underwater Navigation:** Guiding underwater vehicles and divers with precision in the absence of GPS signals.
