## What is Spacetime Coordinate Sensing?

Spacetime coordinate sensing, often referred to as 4D sensing, is a technique that captures information about an object or event in three spatial dimensions (x, y, z) *and* time (t). Unlike traditional sensors that primarily focus on spatial data, spacetime sensors provide a complete picture of how things evolve, move, and interact within a given timeframe. This added temporal dimension unlocks a wealth of new insights and possibilities.
