## Unveiling the Mystery: What is Cosmic Entanglement?

At its core, entanglement is a quantum mechanical phenomenon where two or more particles become linked together in such a way that they share the same fate, no matter how far apart they are. When you measure a property of one particle, you instantly know the corresponding property of the other, even if they are light-years away. This correlation is instantaneous and seemingly violates the classical understanding of locality, which states that an object is only directly influenced by its immediate surroundings.

Cosmic entanglement takes this concept and applies it to the largest scales of the universe. The idea is that particles created during the Big Bang, or through other high-energy cosmic events, could have become entangled and remain so across vast stretches of space and time. This means that the properties of galaxies, black holes, or even the very fabric of spacetime could be interconnected through entanglement.

Entanglement theory in cosmology draws upon the principles of quantum information theory and applies them to cosmological scenarios. Several key concepts and models are used to describe and understand cosmic entanglement:

*   **Entanglement Entropy:** Entanglement entropy is a measure of the amount of entanglement between two subsystems. It is used to quantify the degree of entanglement in cosmological systems.
*   **AdS/CFT Correspondence:** This conjecture relates quantum gravity in anti-de Sitter space (AdS) to a conformal field theory (CFT) on the boundary of AdS space. It provides a powerful tool for studying entanglement in quantum gravity.
