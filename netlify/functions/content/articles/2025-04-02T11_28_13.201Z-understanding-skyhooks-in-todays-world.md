## The Evolution of Skyhook Technology: From Simple Rigging to Advanced Systems

The concept of lifting loads from above has existed for centuries, with early forms of rigging and pulley systems serving as precursors to modern skyhooks. These early systems relied heavily on manual labor and were limited in their lifting capacity and precision.
