## The Hype vs. The Hard Facts: Debunking Common Myths

Robotic exoskeletons have captivated the public imagination, often leading to unrealistic expectations. Let's tackle some prevalent myths:

* **Myth 1: Exoskeletons grant users superhuman strength effortlessly.**  Reality: While exoskeletons significantly augment strength and endurance, they don't magically transform wearers into superheroes. They work by providing assistance and support, requiring the user to still exert effort.  The level of augmentation varies greatly depending on the exoskeleton's design and application.

* **Myth 2: All exoskeletons are the same.** Reality:  The world of robotic exoskeletons is incredibly diverse.  They vary drastically in size, weight, power source, intended application (industrial, medical, military), and level of assistance provided.  A device designed for rehabilitation will be vastly different from one used in a construction site.

* **Myth 3: Exoskeletons are readily available and affordable.** Reality:  The cost of a robotic exoskeleton can range from tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands of dollars, depending on the complexity and features.
