The advantages of microconsciousness interfaces are numerous and far-reaching. Here are some key benefits that make them so important in 2025:

*   **Reduced Invasiveness:** 🔑 Smaller size and less invasive implantation techniques significantly reduce the risk of infection, tissue damage, and other complications associated with traditional BCIs.
*   **Enhanced Biocompatibility:** ✅ Micro-BCIs are often made from biocompatible materials that minimize the body's immune response, leading to better long-term integration and performance.
*   **Improved Signal Resolution:** ⚡ Their proximity to individual neurons or neural circuits allows for the capture of more precise and detailed brain signals, resulting in more accurate and reliable control.
*   **Increased Accessibility:** The minimally invasive nature of micro-BCIs makes them a more attractive option for a wider range of patients and applications.
*   **Cognitive Enhancement:** Micro-BCIs open the door to enhancing cognitive functions such as memory, attention, and learning.
