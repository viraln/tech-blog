## What is Mutualistic Engineering?

Mutualistic engineering, simply put, is the design and implementation of engineered systems that foster mutually beneficial relationships between human-made structures and natural ecosystems.  It moves beyond simply minimizing negative environmental impact to actively designing for symbiosis.  Instead of viewing nature as a constraint, mutualistic engineering sees it as a partner, leveraging natural processes and organisms to enhance the functionality, resilience, and sustainability of our built environment.
