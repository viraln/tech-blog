## What are Self-Organizing Structures?

Self-organizing structures are systems that spontaneously develop order and complexity without central control or explicit programming.  Think of a flock of birds effortlessly changing direction as one, or the intricate patterns in a termite colony.  These systems operate on local interactions, decentralized decision-making, and emergent properties, leading to global patterns and behaviors.  In the context of human systems, this translates to teams and organizations that adapt and evolve organically, driven by the collective intelligence and autonomy of their members.  They are distinct from hierarchical structures, where top-down control dictates operations.  Instead, they embrace distributed leadership and shared responsibility.  Understanding self-organizing systems is crucial to grasping their potential to revolutionize how we work and live.
