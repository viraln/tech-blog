## Cognitive Commons vs. Creative Commons: Key Differences

While both aim to promote open sharing, the Cognitive Commons and Creative Commons address distinct domains. The Creative Commons focuses primarily on creative works like text, images, and music, providing licenses to govern their usage. ![A comparison table highlighting the key differences between Creative Commons and Cognitive Commons](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1522624159390-c31243607917?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
