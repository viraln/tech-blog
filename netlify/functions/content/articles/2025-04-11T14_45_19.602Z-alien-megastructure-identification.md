## Current Methods for Alien Megastructure Detection

Current efforts to identify alien megastructures rely on several key techniques:

1. **Transit photometry:**  Observing dips in a star's light curve as a potential megastructure transits across it.  The Kepler and TESS missions have significantly advanced this technique.
2. **Infrared astronomy:**  Searching for the infrared radiation signature of energy-harvesting megastructures like Dyson spheres.  The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) is a powerful tool in this regard.
3. **Radio astronomy:**  Listening for unusual radio signals that might originate from advanced civilizations. The SETI program continues to actively pursue this avenue.
4. **Data mining and machine learning:**  Utilizing advanced algorithms to sift through vast datasets from telescopes and identify subtle anomalies that might indicate the presence of megastructures.
