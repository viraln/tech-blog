## How Does Neuralink Work? Unveiling the Technology

At its core, Neuralink's technology involves implanting a device, often referred to as the "Link," into the brain. This Link is a small, coin-sized device containing thousands of tiny electrodes that can record neural activity and, in some cases, stimulate specific brain regions.

Here’s a breakdown of the key components:

*   **Electrodes:** Ultra-thin, flexible threads that are surgically implanted into the brain to detect and transmit neural signals. These threads are designed to be less invasive than traditional electrodes, minimizing damage to brain tissue.
*   **N1 Chip (The Link):** The processing unit that sits on the surface of the skull and connects to the electrodes. It amplifies and processes the neural signals, transmitting them wirelessly to an external device.
*   **Surgical Robot:** A custom-built robot designed to precisely insert the electrodes into the brain with minimal damage. This robot is crucial for ensuring the accuracy and safety of the implantation procedure.
