## Planetary Systems Architecture: The Building Blocks of Global Connectivity

Building a planetary-scale system requires a sophisticated architecture that addresses its unique demands.  This often involves:

* **Microservices:** Breaking down the system into smaller, independent services that can be scaled and updated individually.
* **Distributed Databases:** Utilizing databases spread across multiple locations for high availability and scalability.  Examples include Cassandra, MongoDB, and Google Cloud Spanner.
* **Cloud Computing:** Leveraging the vast resources and infrastructure of cloud providers like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.
