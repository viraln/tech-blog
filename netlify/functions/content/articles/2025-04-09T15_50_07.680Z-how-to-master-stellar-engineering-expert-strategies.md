## What is Stellar Engineering? A Comprehensive Definition

Stellar engineering, in its simplest form, is the hypothetical application of engineering principles to manipulate stars or other celestial bodies for human benefit.  This isn't just science fiction; it's a rapidly developing field exploring concepts like:

*   **Starlifting:** Extracting energy and matter from stars.
*   **Stellar nurseries:**  Constructing artificial environments to facilitate star formation.
*   **Megastructures:** Designing and building enormous structures around stars, such as Dyson spheres, for energy harvesting.
*   **Interstellar propulsion:** Developing advanced propulsion systems for interstellar travel.

While many concepts remain theoretical, advancements in materials science, astrophysics, and computational modeling are steadily bringing them closer to reality.
