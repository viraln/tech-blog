## What is Distributed Awareness?

Distributed awareness, in its simplest form, is the ability to understand the complete state of a distributed system in real-time. This goes beyond simply knowing individual component status; it involves understanding the interactions, dependencies, and overall health of the entire system.  Imagine a vast network of interconnected sensors, devices, and applications – distributed awareness provides a holistic, unified view, allowing for proactive management and rapid response to issues. It's about achieving a single source of truth, even when dealing with geographically dispersed components and diverse data sources.
