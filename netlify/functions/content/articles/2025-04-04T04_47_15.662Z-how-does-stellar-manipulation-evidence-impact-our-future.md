Stellar manipulation, at its core, refers to the theoretical engineering feats by advanced civilizations to control or utilize the energy output of stars. ## Star Dimming Anomalies: The Tabby's Star Enigma and Beyond

One of the most intriguing pieces of potential stellar manipulation evidence came from KIC 8462852, also known as Tabby's Star. This star exhibited unusual and dramatic dimming events, far exceeding what would be expected from orbiting planets. While numerous natural explanations have been proposed (comet swarms, circumstellar dust), none fully account for the peculiar dimming pattern.

Other stars have shown similar, albeit less dramatic, dimming anomalies. These include:

*   **Boyajian's Star Candidates:** Stars exhibiting similar, though less pronounced, dimming events as Tabby's Star.
*   **Eclipsing Binary Systems with Unusual Light Curves:** Systems where the light curve deviates significantly from the expected pattern.
*   **Stars with Unexplained Infrared Excess:** Excess infrared radiation could indicate waste heat from large-scale engineering projects.

The data from projects like the Kepler Space Telescope and the Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) continue to provide a wealth of data to analyze for these anomalies.

## Dyson Sphere Evidence: Searching for the Tell-Tale Signs

While a complete Dyson sphere would be easily detectable due to its characteristic infrared signature, the search for partial Dyson spheres or Dyson swarms is more challenging. The key indicators include:

*   **Infrared Excess:** The most common signature, indicating waste heat from energy conversion.
*   **Unusual Spectral Absorption Lines:** Specific absorption lines in a star's spectrum could indicate the presence of artificial materials.
*   **Periodic Occlusion Events:** Regular dimming events caused by orbiting collectors passing in front of the star.
