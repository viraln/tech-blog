## Digital Painting Tutorial: Techniques and Styles

Digital painting allows for unparalleled control over color, texture, and light.  Several techniques can enhance your digital paintings:

* **Layering:** Build up your painting gradually, using layers to isolate elements and adjust them independently.
* **Blending Modes:** Experiment with different blending modes to create unique effects and interactions between colors.
* **Value Studies:**  Focus on light and shadow to create depth and realism.
* **Brush Control:** Master different brush types and settings to achieve varied textures and strokes.
