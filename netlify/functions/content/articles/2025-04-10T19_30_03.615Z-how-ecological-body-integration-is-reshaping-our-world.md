## The Core Principles of Ecological Body Integration

Several key principles underpin the practice of EBI:

* 🔑 **Embodied Awareness:** Cultivating a heightened awareness of bodily sensations, emotions, and their connection to the environment.
* ⚡ **Interconnectedness:** Recognizing the intrinsic link between our physical, emotional, and spiritual well-being and the health of the ecosystems around us.
* 🌳 **Nature as a Healer:** Utilizing nature's restorative power to promote healing and self-regulation.
* 🌊 **Flow and Rhythm:** Aligning with the natural rhythms of the body and the environment to reduce stress and promote balance.
* 🫂 **Relationality:** Understanding the impact of our relationships – both human and non-human – on our well-being.
