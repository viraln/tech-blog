Dream manipulation devices typically use a combination of sensory stimulation, brainwave entrainment, and biofeedback techniques to influence brain activity during sleep. Some examples include:

*   **Targeted Memory Reactivation (TMR):** This technique involves presenting sensory cues (sounds or smells) during sleep to reactivate specific memories and influence dream content.
*   **Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS):** TMS uses magnetic pulses to stimulate or inhibit specific brain regions, potentially altering dream experiences.
*   **Auditory and Visual Stimulation:** Presenting specific sounds or images during sleep can influence the themes and content of dreams.

Dream manipulation holds incredible potential for therapeutic applications, such as treating nightmares, overcoming phobias, and enhancing learning. However, it also raises significant ethical concerns about privacy, autonomy, and the potential for misuse.
