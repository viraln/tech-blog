## 3. Quantum Cognition vs. Classical Cognition: A Comparative Analysis

The core difference lies in how information is represented and processed. Classical models use Boolean logic, representing information as either true or false. Quantum models, however, leverage superposition, allowing information to exist in a probabilistic combination of states until measured.  This difference is crucial in explaining inconsistencies observed in human reasoning that classical models struggle to address.
