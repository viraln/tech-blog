## What is Fractal Learning?

Fractal learning is a pedagogical approach based on the concept of fractals – self-similar patterns that repeat at different scales.  Instead of a linear progression, fractal learning involves exploring a topic at multiple levels of detail, connecting seemingly disparate concepts to reveal underlying unity. Imagine zooming in on a fern: you see the same intricate pattern repeated at every magnification.  Fractal learning does the same with knowledge, building a robust understanding through interconnected layers of information.  It's about seeing the "big picture" while simultaneously mastering the intricate details. ![Illustration of a fractal pattern, like a Mandelbrot set, overlaid with learning concepts](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1546930722-a1292ed9dee8?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
