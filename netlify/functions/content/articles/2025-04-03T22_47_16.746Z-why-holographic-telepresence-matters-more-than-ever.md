## What is Holographic Telepresence? A Deep Dive

Holographic telepresence, often referred to as 3D telepresence or holographic communication, is a technology that allows individuals to interact with each other in a virtual, three-dimensional space, regardless of their physical location. It goes beyond traditional video conferencing by creating a realistic, immersive experience that mimics face-to-face interactions. This is achieved through a combination of advanced technologies, including:

*   **3D Capture:** High-resolution cameras and sensors capture the subject's image and movements from multiple angles.
*   **Data Processing:** Sophisticated algorithms process the captured data to create a 3D model of the subject.
*   **Transmission:** The 3D model is transmitted in real-time to the remote location using high-bandwidth networks.
*   **Holographic Projection:** Specialized projectors or holographic displays recreate the 3D model as a hologram, allowing participants to see and interact with the remote individual as if they were physically present.
