## What are Quantum Decision Trees?

Classical decision trees are a cornerstone of machine learning, used for classification and regression tasks.  They work by recursively partitioning data based on feature values, creating a tree-like structure that leads to a final prediction.  Quantum decision trees leverage the principles of quantum mechanics, specifically superposition and entanglement, to potentially surpass the capabilities of their classical counterparts.  Instead of binary decisions at each node, quantum decision trees explore multiple possibilities simultaneously, leading to faster and potentially more accurate predictions, especially for high-dimensional data.  ![A visual representation of a classical decision tree compared to a quantum decision tree.](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1729556227126-452c221f1ef4?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

> **DID YOU KNOW?**  The concept of superposition allows a quantum bit (qubit) to exist in multiple states (0 and 1) simultaneously, unlike a classical bit which can only be 0 or 1. This inherent parallelism is what gives quantum algorithms their potential speed advantage.
