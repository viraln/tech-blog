## Understanding Digital Darwinism: Explained

Digital Darwinism, coined by Charles Darwin's great-grandson, describes the process by which businesses that fail to adapt to the rapidly changing digital environment become obsolete.  It's a brutal but accurate reflection of the competitive pressures in today's market.  The fittest survive, not just by offering a better product or service, but by mastering the digital tools and strategies that define modern customer interaction and operational efficiency.
