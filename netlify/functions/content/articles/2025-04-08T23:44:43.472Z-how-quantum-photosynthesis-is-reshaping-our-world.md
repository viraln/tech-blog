At its core, photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy. ## Quantum Mechanics in Photosynthesis: The Foundation

*Quantum mechanics in photosynthesis* provides the theoretical framework for understanding how quantum effects can influence biological processes. Quantum mechanics describes the behavior of matter at the atomic and subatomic level, where classical physics breaks down.

Key concepts from quantum mechanics that are relevant to photosynthesis include:

*   **Superposition:** The ability of a quantum system to exist in multiple states simultaneously.
*   **Coherence:** The preservation of phase relationships between quantum states.
*   **Entanglement:** The correlation of quantum states between two or more systems, even when they are separated by large distances.
*   **Tunneling:** The ability of a particle to pass through a potential barrier that would be insurmountable in classical physics.

These quantum mechanical principles provide a powerful toolkit for understanding and manipulating the light-harvesting process in photosynthesis.
