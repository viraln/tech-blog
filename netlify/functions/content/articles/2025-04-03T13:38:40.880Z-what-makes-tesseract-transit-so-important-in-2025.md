## Understanding the Fundamentals: Tesseract Transit Explained

At its core, Tesseract Transit leverages the theoretical concept of wormholes, sometimes called Einstein-Rosen bridges. These are hypothetical topological shortcuts through spacetime, connecting two distant points as if folding space. The "tesseract" aspect refers to the idea of navigating higher dimensions to traverse these wormholes more efficiently.
