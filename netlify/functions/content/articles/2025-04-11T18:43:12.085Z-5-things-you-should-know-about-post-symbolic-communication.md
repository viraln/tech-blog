## What is Post-Symbolic Communication? A Definition

Post-symbolic communication transcends the limitations of traditional linguistic models.  It's not about *what* is said, but *how* it's said, and the overall experience it creates. Think less about the explicit message and more about the implicit feeling, the atmosphere, the visceral impact.  It's about tapping into the subconscious, bypassing the rational mind, and connecting on a deeper, more emotional level. This form of communication relies heavily on nonverbal cues, sensory details, and emotional triggers to convey meaning.  ![A brain with highlighted emotional centers](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1455849318743-b2233052fcff?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## Post-Symbolic Communication Theory: The Underlying Principles

Several theoretical frameworks inform our understanding of post-symbolic communication.  These include:

* **Neuro-linguistic programming (NLP):**  This focuses on the interplay between language, neurological processes, and learned behavior in shaping communication.
* **Affective communication:** This emphasizes the role of emotions in conveying meaning and building relationships.
* **Semiotics:** This explores how signs and symbols create meaning, offering insights into the visual and sensory elements of post-symbolic communication.
