## What is an Environmental OS?

An Environmental OS, or green OS, is a software system designed with sustainability at its core.  It's not just about reducing energy consumption; it encompasses a holistic approach, considering the entire lifecycle of the system, from manufacturing to disposal.  This includes:

* 🔑 **Reduced Energy Consumption:**  Optimized code, intelligent power management, and efficient resource allocation contribute to lower energy usage.
* ⚡ **Minimized Hardware Requirements:**  Lightweight designs reduce the need for powerful (and energy-hungry) hardware.
* ♻️ **Improved E-waste Management:**  Designed for longer lifespans and easier component recycling.
* 🌱 **Sustainable Development Practices:**  Using recycled materials in manufacturing and promoting responsible sourcing of components.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  Look for OS certifications that verify their environmental claims.  Independent audits can provide trustworthy information about a system's true environmental impact.
