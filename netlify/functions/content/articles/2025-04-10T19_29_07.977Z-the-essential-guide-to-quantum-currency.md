## What is Quantum Currency?

Quantum currency represents a theoretical leap forward in digital finance, utilizing the principles of quantum mechanics – specifically, superposition and entanglement – to create fundamentally different digital assets compared to existing cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin.  Unlike traditional cryptocurrencies reliant on cryptographic hash functions, quantum currency aims to leverage quantum phenomena to achieve unprecedented levels of security and anonymity.
