## Understanding Wetware: A Definition and its Distinction from Hardware

Wetware, in its simplest definition, refers to the biological components of a living organism, specifically the nervous system and brain, responsible for processing information and controlling behavior.  Think of it as the "software" running on the "hardware" of your body.  This contrasts sharply with the term "hardware," which denotes the physical, non-biological components of a computer system.  ![A stylized comparison of a brain and a computer chip](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
