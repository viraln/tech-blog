## Understanding Personality Upload Technology: A Deep Dive

Personality upload, also known as mind uploading, digital consciousness, or consciousness transfer, aims to replicate a person's complete mental state – including memories, personality, skills, and consciousness – into a digital substrate.  This isn't simply copying data; it's about achieving a functional equivalence, creating a digital entity that thinks, feels, and experiences the world as the original person did.
