While individualism has been a dominant cultural value in many Western societies, there are signs that this may be shifting towards a greater emphasis on collectivism and community.

*   **The Pandemic's Impact:** The COVID-19 pandemic highlighted the interconnectedness of society and the importance of collective action. This experience may lead to a greater appreciation for community and a willingness to prioritize the needs of others.
*   **Growing Concerns about Climate Change:** The climate crisis is a global challenge that requires collective action. This will likely lead to a greater emphasis on sustainability and a willingness to make sacrifices for the benefit of future generations.
*   **The Rise of Mutual Aid Networks:** Mutual aid networks, where people help each other directly without relying on government or charitable organizations, have become increasingly popular in recent years. This reflects a growing desire for community-based solutions to social problems.
