## What is Substrate Shifting?

At its core, **substrate shifting** refers to the strategic transition of resources, processes, and attention from one platform, technology, or medium (the "substrate") to another, typically newer and more efficient, substrate. It's about recognizing that the foundation upon which your business operates is constantly changing, and proactively adapting to leverage new opportunities and mitigate risks.

Think of it like this: a plant growing in soil (the substrate). If the soil becomes infertile, the plant needs to be transplanted to a richer, more nourishing environment to thrive. Similarly, businesses must be willing to "transplant" their operations to more fertile grounds – be it a new social media platform, an emerging technology like AI, or a different business model altogether.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Substrate shifting isn't just about adopting new technology; it's about rethinking your entire business model in light of evolving customer behaviors and market dynamics.
