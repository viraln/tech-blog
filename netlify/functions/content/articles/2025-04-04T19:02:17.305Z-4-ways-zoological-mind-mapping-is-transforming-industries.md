Teachers can use mind maps to introduce students to the basic concepts of zoology, such as animal classification, anatomy, and behavior. Students can also use mind maps to take notes, organize their research, and prepare for exams.

**Step 1:** Start with a central topic – for example, "Mammals."
**Step 2:** Branch out to different orders of mammals (e.g., Primates, Carnivora, Cetacea).
**Step 3:** For each order, add branches detailing specific families, genera, and species.
**Step 4:** Include information on their key characteristics, such as diet, habitat, and behavior.
