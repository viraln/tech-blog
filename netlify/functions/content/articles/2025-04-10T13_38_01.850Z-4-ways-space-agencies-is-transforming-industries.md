* **Advanced composites:**  Carbon fiber and other composite materials developed for spacecraft structures are now used in everything from wind turbine blades to high-performance bicycles.  Their strength-to-weight ratio is unparalleled.

* **Advanced imaging techniques:**  Techniques developed for monitoring astronaut health, such as advanced MRI and ultrasound technologies, are now used to improve diagnostic capabilities on Earth.

* **Disaster management:**  Satellite imagery is used to monitor natural disasters like hurricanes, floods, and wildfires, enabling faster response and improved disaster preparedness.
