## Understanding the Science Behind the Art

At its core, genetic expression art relies on understanding the basic principles of molecular biology. DNA, the double helix molecule containing our genetic code, is transcribed into RNA, which is then translated into proteins. These proteins are responsible for a vast array of functions within our bodies, influencing everything from our physical characteristics to our susceptibility to certain diseases.

⚡ **Key Concepts:**

*   **DNA Sequencing:** Determining the precise order of nucleotides (A, T, C, G) in a DNA molecule.
*   **Gene Expression:** The process by which information encoded in a gene is used to synthesize a functional gene product (protein or RNA).
*   **Bioinformatics:** The application of computer science and statistics to analyze biological data, including genetic sequences.

Artists collaborate with scientists and bioinformaticians to translate complex genetic data into visually appealing and meaningful artworks. Sophisticated algorithms and visualization techniques are employed to represent genetic information in a variety of forms, from abstract patterns to realistic portraits.
