## The Evolution of Celebrity: From Stage to Screen to Social Media

The definition of "celebrity" has undergone a dramatic transformation over the years. In the past, fame was typically reserved for individuals who achieved significant accomplishments in fields like acting, music, or sports. However, the rise of reality television and social media has democratized the concept of celebrity, allowing anyone with a compelling personality or a knack for creating viral content to gain widespread recognition.

Here's a brief timeline illustrating this evolution:

1.  **The Golden Age of Hollywood:** Celebrities were carefully crafted and controlled by studio systems. Their images were meticulously managed, and their personal lives were largely kept private.
2.  **The Rise of Television:** Television brought celebrities into our homes, creating a more intimate connection with audiences. Talk shows and entertainment news programs provided a platform for celebrities to share their stories and personalities.
3.  **The Internet Era:** The internet revolutionized the way we consume celebrity news and interact with famous individuals. Websites, blogs, and online forums provided a platform for unfiltered gossip and speculation.
4.  **The Social Media Revolution:** Social media platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and X (formerly Twitter) have given celebrities unprecedented control over their own narratives. They can directly engage with fans, share personal updates, and shape their public image in real-time.
