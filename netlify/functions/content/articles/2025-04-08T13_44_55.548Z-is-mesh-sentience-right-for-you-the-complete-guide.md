## What is Mesh Sentience? Unpacking the Concept

Mesh sentience, at its core, refers to the hypothetical emergence of consciousness or intelligence within a mesh network. A mesh network, unlike a traditional client-server model, is a decentralized network topology where each node connects to many other nodes. Think of it as a spiderweb of interconnected devices, rather than a tree with a single root.
