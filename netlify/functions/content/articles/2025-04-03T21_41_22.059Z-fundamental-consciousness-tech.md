*   **Integrated Information Theory (IIT):** IIT proposes that consciousness is directly related to the amount of integrated information a system possesses. Researchers are developing methods to measure integrated information in the brain and other complex systems, potentially providing a quantitative measure of consciousness.
*   **Global Workspace Theory (GWT):** GWT suggests that consciousness arises from a global workspace in the brain, where information is broadcast to various cognitive modules. Researchers are using neuroimaging to identify the neural structures that constitute this global workspace and how information is integrated within it.
*   **Predictive Processing:** This framework posits that the brain is constantly generating predictions about the world and updating these predictions based on sensory input. Researchers are exploring how predictive processing contributes to conscious experience and how disruptions in predictive processing may underlie certain mental disorders.
