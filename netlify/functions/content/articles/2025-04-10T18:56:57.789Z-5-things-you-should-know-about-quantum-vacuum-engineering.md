> **DID YOU KNOW?**  These virtual particles aren't just theoretical; their effects are measurable, most famously through the Casimir effect.

The Casimir effect demonstrates the real physical consequences of these fluctuations.  Two closely spaced, uncharged conductive plates experience an attractive force due to the difference in virtual particle density between the plates and the surrounding space. This seemingly small effect holds immense implications for quantum vacuum engineering.
