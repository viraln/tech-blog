## Latest Trends and Developments

The field of galaxy-scale neural networks is rapidly evolving. Here are some of the latest trends and developments:

*   **Self-Supervised Learning:** Training models on unlabeled data, reducing the need for expensive labeled datasets.

## Common Challenges and Solutions

Despite their potential, galaxy-scale neural networks face several challenges:

*   **Data Scarcity:** Access to high-quality, labeled data can be limited in some domains. **Solution:** Use data augmentation techniques to increase the size of the training dataset. Explore self-supervised learning methods.
*   **Computational Cost:** Training and running these networks can be computationally expensive. **Solution:** Use distributed training techniques to train models on multiple GPUs or TPUs. Explore model compression techniques to reduce the size of the models.
*   **Overfitting:** The models can overfit to the training data, resulting in poor performance on new data. **Solution:** Use regularization techniques to prevent overfitting. Use cross-validation to evaluate the performance of the models.
*   **Bias:** The models can be biased if the training data is biased. **Solution:** Carefully curate the training data to ensure that it is representative of the population of interest. Use bias detection and mitigation techniques.
