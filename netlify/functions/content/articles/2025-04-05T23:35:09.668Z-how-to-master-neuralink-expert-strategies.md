The Neuralink device is a marvel of engineering, combining cutting-edge neuroscience with advanced microelectronics. Here's a breakdown of its key components:

2.  **N1 Chip:** The N1 chip is the central processing unit of the Neuralink device. It amplifies and processes the neural signals, transmitting them wirelessly to an external device.
3.  **Battery:** A small, rechargeable battery powers the Neuralink device. The battery is designed to last for several years and can be recharged wirelessly.
4.  **Wireless Transmitter:** The wireless transmitter allows the Neuralink device to communicate with external devices, such as computers, smartphones, and prosthetic limbs.
5.  **Surgical Robot:** Neuralink has developed a specialized surgical robot to precisely implant the electrodes into the brain. This robot minimizes damage to brain tissue and ensures accurate placement of the electrodes.
