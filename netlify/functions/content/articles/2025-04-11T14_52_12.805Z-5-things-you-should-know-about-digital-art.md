**Step 2: Sketching:**  Many artists begin with a digital sketch, refining their ideas before moving to color and detail.  This stage is crucial for establishing the foundation of your artwork.

**Step 3: Line Art (if applicable):** For comic art, illustration, or any piece requiring clean lines, this step involves refining your sketch into precise lines.

## 3.  The Business of Art: Selling Digital Art Online

The digital art market is booming, presenting exciting opportunities for artists to monetize their creations.

*   **Print-on-Demand Services:** Platforms like Printful and Society6 allow you to upload your artwork and sell it on various products (t-shirts, mugs, phone cases, etc.) without managing inventory.
*   **Online Marketplaces:**  Sites like Etsy, Creative Market, and ArtStation provide dedicated spaces for artists to showcase and sell their digital artwork.
*   **Personal Website/Portfolio:**  Building your own website allows for greater control and branding opportunities.
*   **Digital Art Commissions:**  Offering custom commissions allows you to directly collaborate with clients and create personalized artwork.
*   **NFT Digital Art:**  Non-fungible tokens (NFTs) have revolutionized the art world, enabling artists to sell unique digital assets as verifiable ownership certificates on blockchain platforms like OpenSea. ![Example of NFT digital art](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1637416067365-2b5e7e8fe8fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## 4.  The Ever-Evolving Landscape: Latest Trends in Digital Art

The digital art world is constantly evolving.  Here are some current trends:

*   **AI Art Generators:** Tools like Midjourney, Dall-E 2, and Stable Diffusion are changing the game, enabling artists to create unique visuals through text prompts.  The ethical implications of AI art are still being debated, but its integration into creative workflows is undeniable.
*   **Metaverse Art:**  With the growing popularity of virtual worlds, digital artists are creating immersive experiences and virtual art installations within metaverse platforms.
*   **Generative Art:** Algorithms and code are used to create unique and ever-changing artwork, pushing the boundaries of artistic expression.
*   **Digital Art Prints:** High-quality digital art prints are increasingly popular, bridging the gap between the digital and physical worlds. Selling digital art prints is a lucrative option for artists.
