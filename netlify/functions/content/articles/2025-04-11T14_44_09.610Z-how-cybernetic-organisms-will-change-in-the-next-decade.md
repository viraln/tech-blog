## Defining Cybernetics: Beyond the Bionic Limb

Before diving into the future, let's clarify what cybernetics entails.  **Cybernetics definition:**  It's the interdisciplinary study of the structure and function of regulatory systems in both living organisms and machines, and the application of this knowledge to the design of artificial systems.  This isn't just about replacing lost limbs; it's about enhancing existing capabilities and integrating technology seamlessly with the human body.  ![A graphic illustrating the interconnectedness of cybernetics, biology, and engineering](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1579781403299-11c35baa47e2?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
