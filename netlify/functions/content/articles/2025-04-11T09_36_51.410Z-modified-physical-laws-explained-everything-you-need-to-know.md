## Modified Gravity Theories: Beyond Einstein

Einstein's theory of general relativity revolutionized our understanding of gravity, describing it as a curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy. However, observations like dark matter and dark energy suggest that general relativity might be incomplete.  This has led to the development of modified gravity theories, attempting to explain these cosmic mysteries without resorting to exotic dark matter or energy.

Examples include:

* **f(R) gravity:** Modifies the Einstein-Hilbert action by adding a function of the Ricci scalar.
* **Scalar-tensor theories:** Introduce scalar fields that interact with gravity.
* **TeVeS (Tensor-Vector-Scalar) gravity:**  A more complex theory attempting to explain galactic rotation curves without dark matter.
