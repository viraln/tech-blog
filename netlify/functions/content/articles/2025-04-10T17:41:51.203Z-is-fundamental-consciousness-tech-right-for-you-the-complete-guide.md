## How Does Consciousness Tech Work?  The Science Behind the Hype

The underlying science of FCT is complex and multifaceted, drawing upon neuroscience, psychology, computer science, and engineering.  Different technologies employ various methods, including:

* **EEG (Electroencephalography):**  Measures brainwave activity through electrodes placed on the scalp.
* **fNIRS (Functional Near-Infrared Spectroscopy):**  Measures changes in blood flow in the brain, providing insights into neural activity.
* **MEG (Magnetoencephalography):**  Measures magnetic fields produced by electrical activity in the brain.
* **TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation):**  Uses magnetic pulses to stimulate specific brain regions.
* **tDCS (Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation):**  Applies a weak electrical current to the scalp to modulate brain activity.
