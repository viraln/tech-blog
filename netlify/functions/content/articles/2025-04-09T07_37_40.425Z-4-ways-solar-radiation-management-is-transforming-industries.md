## 1.  Understanding Solar Radiation Management (SRM): A Deep Dive

Solar Radiation Management (SRM) encompasses a range of technologies designed to reflect a small percentage of incoming solar radiation back into space, thereby cooling the planet.  This isn't about stopping climate change entirely; it's about mitigating its most devastating effects while we work to reduce emissions.  The most discussed SRM technique is **Stratospheric Aerosol Injection (SAI)**, mimicking the cooling effect of large volcanic eruptions.  Other approaches include marine cloud brightening and cirrus cloud thinning, each with its own set of complexities and potential impacts. ![A graphic illustrating different SRM techniques](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1559302504-64aae6ca6b6d?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
