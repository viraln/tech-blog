## Understanding the Basics of Wave Interference

##  Constructive and Destructive Interference: A Deeper Dive

Let's explore the two main types of wave interference in more detail:

**Constructive Interference:** This occurs when two waves with the same frequency and phase meet, resulting in a wave with a larger amplitude. Think of it like adding two waves together; the peaks reinforce each other, creating a stronger signal.

**Destructive Interference:**  This happens when two waves with the same frequency but opposite phases meet. The peak of one wave cancels out the trough of the other, resulting in a smaller amplitude or even complete cancellation.  This is like subtracting waves.
