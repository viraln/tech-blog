## Understanding the Fundamentals of Entanglement Bearing Technology

At its core, an entanglement bearing seeks to manipulate and exploit the quantum phenomenon of entanglement to reduce friction and enhance bearing performance. Traditional bearings rely on physical components like balls or rollers to minimize contact and facilitate rotation. Entanglement bearings, however, aim to minimize or even eliminate physical contact by leveraging quantum effects.

The basic idea involves entangling particles within the bearing structure. While the precise mechanisms are still under development and vary depending on the specific design, the goal is generally to create a system where the quantum state of one particle is intrinsically linked to the state of another, even when separated by a distance. This entanglement can be used to influence the behavior of the bearing components, ideally leading to reduced friction and improved stability.
