##  Addressing the Challenges: Sustainability and Space Debris

The increasing activity in space brings with it significant challenges.  Space debris poses a growing threat to operational satellites and spacecraft, creating a potential cascading effect of collisions.  Developing effective methods for removing space debris is crucial for the long-term sustainability of space activities.

The environmental impact of space exploration must also be carefully considered.  Minimizing the amount of waste left in orbit and on celestial bodies is paramount.  Sustainable practices, including the development of reusable launch vehicles and in-situ resource utilization (ISRU), will be essential for ensuring the responsible exploration and utilization of space.
