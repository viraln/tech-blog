## The Science Behind Quantum Money: Quantum Cryptography

The foundation of quantum currency lies in quantum cryptography, a field of physics that uses the principles of quantum mechanics to encrypt and transmit data securely. Here's a breakdown of the key concepts:

*   **Quantum Key Distribution (QKD):** This is the primary method for secure key exchange. QKD protocols, like BB84, use single photons of light to transmit encryption keys. The quantum state of these photons is used to encode the key.

*   **Superposition:** This principle allows a quantum bit (qubit) to exist in multiple states simultaneously (e.g., both 0 and 1). This is unlike a classical bit, which can only be either 0 or 1.

*   **Entanglement:** Two or more qubits can be linked together in such a way that they share the same fate, no matter how far apart they are. Measuring the state of one entangled qubit instantly determines the state of the other.

*   **Quantum Measurement:** The act of measuring a quantum state inevitably disturbs it. This means that any attempt to intercept or eavesdrop on a QKD transmission will leave detectable traces.

**Did you know:** The BB84 protocol, one of the earliest and most well-known QKD protocols, was developed in 1984 by Charles Bennett and Gilles Brassard.
