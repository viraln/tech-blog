UCS software typically encompasses several key functionalities:

*   **Mission Planning & Scheduling:** Creating detailed mission plans, including trajectory calculations, resource allocation, and task scheduling. This often involves advanced simulation and modeling capabilities to optimize mission parameters and identify potential risks.
*   **Telemetry Monitoring & Analysis:** Real-time monitoring of spacecraft health, performance, and environmental conditions. The system analyzes telemetry data to detect anomalies, predict potential failures, and provide operators with actionable insights.
*   **Command Generation & Execution:** Generating and transmitting commands to spacecraft, satellites, and ground stations. This includes managing command sequences, validating command integrity, and ensuring secure communication.
*   **Anomaly Detection & Resolution:** Identifying and responding to anomalies in spacecraft behavior or environmental conditions. The system may automatically trigger alerts, initiate corrective actions, or provide operators with decision support tools.
*   **Data Management & Archiving:** Storing and managing vast amounts of mission data, including telemetry, command logs, and operational reports. This data is crucial for post-mission analysis, performance evaluation, and future mission planning.
*   **Situational Awareness & Visualization:** Providing operators with a clear and intuitive view of the mission environment, including spacecraft position, attitude, and environmental conditions. This may involve 3D visualizations, interactive maps, and other graphical displays.
