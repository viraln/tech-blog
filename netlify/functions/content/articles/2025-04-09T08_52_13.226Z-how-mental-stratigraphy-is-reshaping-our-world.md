## The Layers of the Mind: Unpacking the Mental Strata

Mental stratigraphy identifies several key layers, although the specific number and terminology vary among practitioners.  A common model includes:

* 🔑 **Surface Level:** Conscious thoughts, feelings, and immediate reactions.
* ⚡ **Belief Layer:** Deep-seated beliefs about ourselves, the world, and others.  These often operate unconsciously.
* 🪨 **Experiential Layer:** Memories, both conscious and repressed, from significant life events.
* 🌳 **Developmental Layer:**  Foundational beliefs and patterns formed during childhood and adolescence.
* 🫙 **Archetypal Layer:**  Universal patterns and themes that shape our subconscious.
