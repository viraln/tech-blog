The Animal Language Internet isn't just about translation; it's about creating a connected ecosystem where animals can interact with their environment and with us in more meaningful ways. IoA refers to the network of smart pet devices, sensors, and communication platforms that are designed to improve animal welfare and facilitate human-animal interaction.
