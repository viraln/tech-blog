## Unveiling Biomolecular Processing Techniques

Biomolecular processing employs a diverse array of techniques, each designed for specific purposes. Here's an overview of some key methods:

1.  **Protein Purification Methods:**
    *   **Affinity Chromatography:** Separating proteins based on their specific binding affinity to a ligand.
    *   **Size Exclusion Chromatography:** Separating proteins based on their size.
    *   **Ion Exchange Chromatography:** Separating proteins based on their charge.
2.  **DNA and RNA Manipulation:**
    *   **PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction):** Amplifying specific DNA sequences.
    *   **DNA Sequencing Services:** Determining the nucleotide sequence of DNA.
    *   **RNA Sequencing (RNA-Seq):** Analyzing gene expression patterns.
3.  **Cell Culture and Engineering:**
    *   **Mammalian Cell Culture:** Growing mammalian cells in a controlled environment for research and production.
    *   **Microbial Fermentation:** Utilizing microorganisms to produce specific biomolecules.
    *   **Genetic Engineering:** Modifying the genetic makeup of cells to alter their behavior or produce desired products.
4.  **Biomolecular Interaction Analysis:**
    *   **Surface Plasmon Resonance (SPR):** Studying real-time interactions between biomolecules.
    *   **Isothermal Titration Calorimetry (ITC):** Measuring the thermodynamic parameters of biomolecular interactions.
    *   **ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay):** Detecting and quantifying specific biomolecules.
