**Did you know?** Binaural beats work by presenting slightly different frequencies to each ear. The brain then perceives a third frequency, the difference between the two, which can entrain brainwave activity.

*   **Binaural Beats:** These auditory illusions can promote relaxation, focus, creativity, and even altered states of consciousness by influencing brainwave frequencies. They are often used to support meditation, sleep, and cognitive enhancement.
*   **Isochronic Tones:** These tones are regular pulses of sound that can quickly entrain brainwave activity. They are often used to induce deep relaxation, reduce stress, and improve focus.
*   **Digital Drug Experiences:** These are carefully crafted soundscapes that combine binaural beats, isochronic tones, and other auditory elements to create a specific mental state. They can be used to simulate the effects of various substances, promote relaxation, or enhance creativity.

**Case Study:** A study published in the *Journal of Alternative and Complementary Medicine* found that listening to binaural beats for 30 minutes a day reduced anxiety and improved mood in participants.
