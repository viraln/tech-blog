## What is Cybernetics and How Does it Relate to Cybernetic Organisms?

Cybernetics, at its core, is the study of control and communication in both animals and machines.  It explores how systems, whether biological or artificial, process information and respond to their environment.  A cybernetic organism, therefore, is a being whose biological functions are augmented or enhanced through the integration of technological components. This integration can range from relatively simple devices like pacemakers to complex systems involving neural interfaces and prosthetic limbs.  The defining characteristic is the synergistic interaction between the biological and the technological, creating a hybrid system. ![A graphic depicting the integration of technology with a human body](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1579781403299-11c35baa47e2?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
