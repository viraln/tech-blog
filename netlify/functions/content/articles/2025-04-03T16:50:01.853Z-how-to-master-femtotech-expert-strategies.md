## 1. Understanding the Fundamentals: What is Femtotech?

Femtotech, at its core, is the manipulation of matter using femtosecond (10^-15 seconds) lasers. These incredibly short pulses of light allow us to observe and control atomic and molecular processes in real-time. This contrasts with traditional methods that often rely on bulk processes and statistical averages. The ability to interact with matter on this timescale opens doors to unprecedented levels of control and precision.

Essentially, femtotech allows us to "see" and "manipulate" the very building blocks of reality as they interact.

## 3. Femtosecond Lasers: The Heart of Femtotech

The femtosecond laser is the enabling technology behind femtotech. These lasers generate pulses of light so short that they can capture snapshots of molecular motion. The shorter the pulse, the more precise the control.

The key characteristics of a femtosecond laser are:

*   **Pulse Duration:** Measured in femtoseconds (10^-15 seconds). Shorter pulses offer greater temporal resolution.
*   **Wavelength:** Determines the type of material the laser can interact with.
*   **Power:** Influences the intensity of the laser-matter interaction.
*   **Repetition Rate:** Dictates how frequently the pulses are generated.

Different types of femtosecond lasers exist, each with its own advantages and disadvantages. Ti:sapphire lasers are commonly used due to their broad bandwidth and high power output. Fiber lasers are gaining popularity due to their compact size and high stability.
