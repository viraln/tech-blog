## Core Principles of Existential Optimization

Several key principles underpin effective existential optimization:

* ⚡ **Purpose Discovery:** Identifying your core values and passions helps you define a life purpose that feels authentic and meaningful. This might involve exploring different interests, volunteering, or engaging in creative pursuits.

* 🎯 **Goal Setting:** Setting SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) goals aligned with your values provides direction and motivation.
