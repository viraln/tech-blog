**Understanding Mood Swings:**  The ability to accurately track and analyze mood fluctuations is crucial. **Treating Low Mood:**  The development of targeted therapies, including personalized medication regimens and innovative approaches like neurofeedback, is transforming the treatment of mood disorders like depression and anxiety.
