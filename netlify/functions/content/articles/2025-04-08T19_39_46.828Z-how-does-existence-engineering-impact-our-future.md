## Understanding Existential Risks: The Threats We Face

What are the specific threats that Existence Engineering seeks to address? These are often categorized as follows:

*   **Natural Risks:** Asteroid impacts, supervolcanic eruptions, global pandemics (like COVID-19, but potentially far more lethal).
*   **Anthropogenic Risks:** Climate change, nuclear war, biotechnology risks (engineered pandemics), artificial intelligence risks, nanotechnology risks, societal collapse.
*   **Exogenous Risks:** Risks originating from beyond Earth, such as advanced extraterrestrial threats (though these are considered less likely).

While natural risks are often viewed as inevitable, the focus of Existence Engineering is primarily on mitigating anthropogenic risks – those created by human actions. This is where we have the most agency and the greatest potential for prevention.

✅ **Key Point:** Many existential risks are interconnected. For example, climate change can exacerbate resource scarcity, leading to political instability and increasing the likelihood of conflict.
