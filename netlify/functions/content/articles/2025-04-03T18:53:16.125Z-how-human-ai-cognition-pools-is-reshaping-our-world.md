## Navigating the Challenges of Human-AI Collaboration

While the potential of Human-AI Cognition Pools is immense, there are also challenges that need to be addressed:

*   **Data Privacy and Security:** Ensuring the responsible and ethical use of data is crucial, especially when dealing with sensitive information.
*   **Algorithmic Bias:** AI algorithms can perpetuate and amplify existing biases in the data they are trained on, leading to unfair or discriminatory outcomes.
