## Arctic Climate Change: A Looming Threat

The Arctic is warming at a rate two to three times faster than the global average – a phenomenon known as Arctic amplification. This rapid warming is causing significant changes, including:

* 🌡️ **Melting sea ice:** Reducing habitat for many species and impacting ocean currents.
* 🌊 **Rising sea levels:** Threatening coastal communities and ecosystems.
* permafrost thaw: Releasing vast amounts of greenhouse gases, further accelerating warming.
