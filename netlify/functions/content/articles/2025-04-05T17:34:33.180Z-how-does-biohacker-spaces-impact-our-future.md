## The Rise of DIY Biology Labs: A New Era of Scientific Exploration

Biohacker spaces, also known as community biolabs or open science labs, are shared workspaces equipped with tools and resources for individuals to conduct biological experiments. Think of them as makerspaces, but for biology. They provide affordable bio lab access and foster a collaborative environment where enthusiasts, students, and even seasoned scientists can pursue their research interests outside traditional academic or corporate settings. This movement is fueled by the increasing accessibility of biohacking equipment and a growing desire to understand and manipulate the biological world around us.

> **EXPERT TIP:** The key to a successful biohacker space is a strong community. Focus on fostering collaboration, knowledge sharing, and mentorship among members.

The core principle behind biohacker spaces is open science. This means that research findings and data are freely shared, promoting transparency and accelerating the pace of discovery. This contrasts sharply with the often-proprietary nature of research in traditional institutions.
