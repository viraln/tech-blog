Recognizing the potential risks and rewards, international organizations and governments are developing first contact protocols to guide our interactions with any potential extraterrestrial civilizations. These protocols aim to ensure that contact is conducted in a safe, ethical, and responsible manner.

3.  **Transparency:** Communicating openly with the public about the contact.
4.  **Non-Interference:** Avoiding any actions that could disrupt or harm the alien civilization.
