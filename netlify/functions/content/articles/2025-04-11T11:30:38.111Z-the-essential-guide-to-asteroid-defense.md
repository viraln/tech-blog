## Asteroid Deflection Technologies:  How to Stop a Killer Rock

Currently, several asteroid deflection technologies are under development, each with its own strengths and weaknesses.  These methods broadly fall into two categories: kinetic impactors and gravity tractors.

**Kinetic Impactors:** This method involves launching a spacecraft to collide with the asteroid, altering its trajectory slightly but enough to avert an impact. The successful DART (Double Asteroid Redirection Test) mission in 2022 demonstrated the feasibility of this approach. ![An illustration of a kinetic impactor spacecraft colliding with an asteroid](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1571349758057-831a4b77d3df?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
*An illustration of a kinetic impactor spacecraft colliding with an asteroid*
*Photo by [Florian Olivo](https://unsplash.com/@florianolv) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/@florianolv)*

**Gravity Tractors:** This more gradual approach involves using the spacecraft's gravitational pull to slowly nudge the asteroid off course over an extended period.  This method requires early detection and a longer lead time but offers a gentler, more controlled deflection.

Other methods under consideration include nuclear deflection (using nuclear explosions to alter an asteroid's trajectory), ion beam shepherding, and even painting the asteroid to alter its solar radiation pressure.
