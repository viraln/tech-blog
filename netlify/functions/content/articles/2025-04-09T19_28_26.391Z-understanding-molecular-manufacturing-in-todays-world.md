## What is Molecular Manufacturing?

Molecular manufacturing, also known as molecular nanotechnology, is the concept of building complex structures and devices by precisely manipulating individual atoms and molecules.  It's a bottom-up approach, in stark contrast to traditional top-down manufacturing which starts with larger materials and subtracts or shapes them. The core concept revolves around the use of molecular assemblers – nanoscale robots capable of arranging atoms and molecules with atomic precision. ![Illustration of a molecular assembler in action](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1469289759076-d1484757abc3?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
