## Understanding Self-Identity Coherence: More Than Just Feeling Good

Self-identity coherence refers to the internal consistency and harmony between different aspects of your self-concept. It's about the alignment between your beliefs, values, behaviors, and goals.  When your identity is coherent, you experience a strong sense of self, clarity of purpose, and a greater sense of control over your life.  Conversely, a lack of coherence leads to feelings of confusion, internal conflict, and a diminished sense of self-worth.
