## 2.  A Glimpse into the Unexplained: Examples of Astronomical Anomalies

The universe is rife with puzzles.  Let's explore some prominent examples:

* **Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs):** These incredibly powerful millisecond-long bursts of radio waves originating from distant galaxies are a significant mystery. While some have been linked to magnetars, many remain unexplained.
* **Dark Matter and Dark Energy:**  These constitute the vast majority of the universe's mass-energy content, yet their nature remains elusive.  Their gravitational effects are observable, but their composition is unknown.
* **The Pioneer Anomaly:**  The Pioneer 10 and 11 spacecraft experienced an unexplained, tiny deceleration as they journeyed towards interstellar space. While a plausible explanation involving thermal recoil has been proposed, it remains a subject of debate.
* **Strange Star Clusters:**  Certain star clusters exhibit unusual density distributions or orbital dynamics that don't align with current models of stellar evolution and gravitational interactions.
* **Tabby's Star (KIC 8462852):** This star exhibits unpredictable and significant dips in brightness, leading to speculation about megastructures or other exotic explanations. While dust clouds are a more likely explanation, the mystery continues to fuel speculation.
