## Understanding the Foundations of Neural Collectives

Neural collectives, at their core, are systems where multiple intelligent agents (biological or artificial) interact and coordinate to achieve a common goal. This concept draws from various fields, including:

*   **Collective Intelligence:** The shared or group intelligence that emerges from the collaboration, collective efforts, and competition of many individuals or agents.
*   **Swarm Intelligence:** Inspired by the behavior of social insects like ants and bees, this focuses on decentralized, self-organized systems where simple agents follow basic rules, leading to complex and intelligent group behavior.
*   **Distributed Cognition:** This perspective emphasizes that cognitive processes are not confined to the individual mind but are distributed across individuals, artifacts, and the environment.
*   **Neural Networks Collective Behavior:** The emergent behavior of interconnected artificial neural networks working together. This can involve federated learning, ensemble methods, or even more complex architectures where networks collaborate on different aspects of a problem.
