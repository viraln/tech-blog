Mind-matter integration, at its core, proposes a direct or indirect interaction between consciousness (the subjective experience of being) and the physical world. Instead, mind-matter integration suggests that consciousness can, in turn, *influence* matter. This influence can be subtle, measurable through scientific instruments, or, according to some theories, more dramatic, impacting events and outcomes. The debate surrounding mind-matter integration hinges on whether consciousness is merely a byproduct of physical processes or an active force capable of shaping reality.
