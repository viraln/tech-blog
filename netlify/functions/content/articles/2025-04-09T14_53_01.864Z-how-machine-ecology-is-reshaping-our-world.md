## What is Machine Ecology? A Definitive Definition

Machine ecology, in its simplest form, is the study of interactions between autonomous machines and their environment.  It goes beyond simply connecting devices; it’s about creating self-organizing systems where machines dynamically adapt, collaborate, and evolve to achieve shared goals, much like organisms in a natural ecosystem.  Unlike traditional approaches focusing on individual machine optimization, machine ecology emphasizes the holistic performance of the entire system.  A key difference from artificial intelligence (AI) is its focus on the collective behavior and interactions of multiple agents rather than the intelligence of a single entity.
