## Understanding City Operating Systems: More Than Just Software

City Operating Systems are integrated platforms designed to manage and optimize a city's complex operations. Unlike simple city management software, COS leverage advanced technologies like AI, IoT, big data analytics, and digital twins to create a holistic view of the urban environment.  They connect various city departments – from transportation and public safety to utilities and social services – onto a single, unified platform. This interconnectedness allows for unprecedented data sharing, improved collaboration, and proactive decision-making.
