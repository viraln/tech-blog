##  The Rise of Layer-2 Solutions

Scalability remains a key challenge for many blockchains. The next decade will see a significant increase in the adoption of layer-2 scaling solutions.  These solutions, such as rollups and state channels, will enable faster and cheaper transactions without compromising the security of the underlying blockchain.  This will be crucial for the widespread adoption of cryptocurrencies and decentralized applications.
