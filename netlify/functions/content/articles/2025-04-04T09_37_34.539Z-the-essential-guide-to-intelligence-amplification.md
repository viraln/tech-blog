## What is Intelligence Amplification?

Intelligence Amplification (IA) refers to the use of technology, tools, and techniques to enhance human cognitive abilities. Unlike Artificial Intelligence (AI), which aims to replicate or replace human intelligence, IA focuses on *augmenting* it. Think of it as a partnership between humans and machines, leveraging the strengths of both to achieve superior outcomes. IA isn't about becoming "smarter" in the traditional sense; it's about becoming *more effective* in how we think, learn, and create. It's about optimizing our cognitive processes to excel in an increasingly complex world.
