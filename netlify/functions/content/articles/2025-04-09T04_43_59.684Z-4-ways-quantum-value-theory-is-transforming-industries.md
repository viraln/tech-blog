## What is Quantum Value Theory?

Quantum Value Theory, at its core, applies the principles of quantum mechanics – superposition, entanglement, and observation – to financial modeling and valuation. Unlike classical finance, which relies on deterministic models, QVT acknowledges the inherent uncertainty and probabilistic nature of markets.  It moves beyond simple present value calculations to encompass a wider range of possibilities and interdependencies, offering a more nuanced and potentially more accurate assessment of an asset's true worth.  Instead of viewing assets in isolation, QVT considers their interconnectedness within a complex ecosystem.

> **Did you know?** The concept of superposition, crucial to QVT, suggests an asset can simultaneously hold multiple values until measured (observed in the market). This challenges the classical finance assumption of a single, definitive value.

## Quantum Finance Theory: Beyond Traditional Models

Quantum Finance Theory (QFT) expands on QVT by developing new mathematical frameworks for understanding financial markets.
