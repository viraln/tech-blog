## What is Bit-Based Reality?

Bit-based reality (BBR) refers to the integration of digital information – represented in bits – into our perception and interaction with the physical world. Unlike the metaverse, which aims to create entirely virtual environments, BBR enhances our existing reality by overlaying digital data onto our physical surroundings.
