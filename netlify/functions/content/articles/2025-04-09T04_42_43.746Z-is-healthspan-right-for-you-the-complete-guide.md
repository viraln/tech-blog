## What is Healthspan? Understanding the Difference Between Healthspan and Lifespan

Healthspan and lifespan are often confused, but they represent distinct concepts.  Lifespan refers to the total number of years a person lives, while healthspan focuses on the duration of that life lived in good health and free from chronic diseases like heart disease, cancer, dementia, and diabetes.  The goal is to minimize the years spent in poor health, bridging the gap between lifespan and healthspan. ![Graph showing the difference between lifespan and healthspan](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

> **DID YOU KNOW?**  Studies show that the average person spends roughly 10-15 years of their lifespan experiencing significant health limitations due to age-related diseases.
