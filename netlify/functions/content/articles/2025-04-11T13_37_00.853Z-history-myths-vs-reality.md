##  The Power of Primary Sources:  Unlocking Historical Truths

Primary sources – documents, artifacts, and other materials created during the period being studied – are crucial for accurate historical understanding.  They provide firsthand accounts, offering a glimpse into the lives, thoughts, and experiences of people in the past.  However, even primary sources must be critically examined for biases and limitations.  They are not always objective or complete.

Compare and contrast multiple primary sources to create a more comprehensive picture.  For example, when researching a historical event, examine accounts from different participants or perspectives.  This will help you identify potential biases and construct a more nuanced understanding of the event.
