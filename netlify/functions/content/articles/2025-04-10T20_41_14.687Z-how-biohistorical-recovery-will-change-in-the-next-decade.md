## The Expanding Scope of Biohistorical Recovery

Biohistorical recovery, also known as biohistorical restoration or historical ecosystem recovery, is moving beyond simple habitat restoration.  It's increasingly incorporating sophisticated techniques like ancient DNA analysis, paleoecological reconstruction, and advanced modeling to understand past ecosystems in unprecedented detail. Several exciting trends are shaping the future of biohistorical recovery:

* **Increased focus on functional diversity:**  Moving beyond simply restoring species richness, the emphasis is shifting towards recreating the ecological functions and processes that characterized past ecosystems.
