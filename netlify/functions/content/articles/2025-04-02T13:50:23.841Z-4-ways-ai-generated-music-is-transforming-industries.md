## Common Challenges and Solutions

Despite its potential, AI music also faces some challenges:

*   **Copyright Issues:** Determining the ownership of music generated by AI can be complex, especially when the AI is trained on copyrighted material. **Solution:** Clear licensing agreements and transparent AI training practices are crucial.
*   **Lack of Human Emotion:** Some critics argue that AI-generated music lacks the emotional depth and expressiveness of human-composed music. **Solution:** Combining AI with human creativity and focusing on AI as a collaborative tool can help to overcome this limitation.
*   **Algorithmic Bias:** AI models can be biased based on the data they are trained on, leading to the creation of music that is not diverse or representative. **Solution:** Using diverse datasets and actively addressing algorithmic bias are essential.
*   **Over-reliance on AI:** There is a risk that musicians may become overly reliant on AI, stifling their own creativity and innovation. **Solution:** Using AI as a tool to augment creativity, rather than replace it, is crucial.
