## AGI vs. AI: Understanding the Distinction

The terms AGI and AI are often used interchangeably, but they represent distinct concepts.  AI, in its current form, encompasses narrow or weak AI, designed for specific tasks.  AGI, on the other hand, is broad or strong AI, possessing general intelligence comparable to humans.

| Feature        | Narrow AI (Current AI)                               | AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)                     |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| **Intelligence** | Specialized; excels in specific tasks                   | General; possesses human-level cognitive abilities         |
| **Learning**    | Limited to pre-programmed data and specific algorithms | Learns and adapts across diverse domains and tasks           |
| **Adaptability** | Low; struggles with unfamiliar situations              | High; easily adapts to new situations and challenges          |
| **Problem-Solving** | Task-specific; limited general problem-solving skills  | General problem-solving; able to reason and strategize     |
