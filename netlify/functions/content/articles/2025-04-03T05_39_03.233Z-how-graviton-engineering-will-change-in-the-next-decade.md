## Quantum Gravity Engineering: Bridging the Gap Between Quantum Mechanics and General Relativity

Quantum Gravity Engineering represents the intersection of quantum mechanics and general relativity, aiming to develop practical applications based on a unified theory of gravity. This is a grand challenge in modern physics, as these two fundamental theories describe the universe at different scales and are currently incompatible.

*   **String Theory:** Proposes that fundamental particles are not point-like but rather tiny vibrating strings, potentially unifying all forces including gravity.
*   **Loop Quantum Gravity:** Quantizes spacetime itself, suggesting that spacetime is not continuous but rather composed of discrete "loops."
