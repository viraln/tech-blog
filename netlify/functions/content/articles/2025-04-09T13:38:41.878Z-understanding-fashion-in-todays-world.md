## How to Dress for Your Body Type:  A Practical Guide

Understanding your body type is key to finding clothes that flatter your figure.  There's no one-size-fits-all approach; what works for one person might not work for another.  Focus on accentuating your best features and minimizing areas you're less confident about.  Common body types include:

| Body Type        | Characteristics                               | Styling Tips                                           |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Hourglass       | Balanced bust and hips, defined waist           | Fitted tops, A-line skirts, belted dresses           |
| Pear/Triangle    | Wider hips than shoulders                      | A-line skirts, empire waist dresses, V-neck tops     |
| Apple/Round      | Fuller midsection                             | Empire waist dresses, flowy tops, A-line skirts       |
| Rectangle/Straight| Similar measurements across shoulders, waist, hips | Structured jackets, belts to define the waist, wide-leg pants |
| Inverted Triangle| Wider shoulders than hips                      | Boat neck tops, A-line skirts, flared pants          |

> **DID YOU KNOW?**  Body positivity is a growing movement challenging traditional beauty standards and embracing diverse body types.
