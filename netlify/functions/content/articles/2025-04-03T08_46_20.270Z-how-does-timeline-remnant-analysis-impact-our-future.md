## Key Concepts and Terminology

Before we dive deeper, let's define some essential terms:

*   **Timeline:** A chronological sequence of events, typically represented visually or as a structured dataset.
*   **Remnant:** A trace or piece of evidence left behind by an event. This can be digital, physical, or even anecdotal.
*   **Correlation:** A statistical relationship between two or more events, suggesting a possible connection.
*   **Causation:** A relationship where one event directly causes another. Establishing causation is a key goal of timeline remnant analysis.
*   **Pattern Recognition:** Identifying recurring sequences or trends in the timeline, which can reveal underlying mechanisms or predict future events.
*   **Temporal Analysis:** The study of how events change over time, including their frequency, duration, and sequence.
