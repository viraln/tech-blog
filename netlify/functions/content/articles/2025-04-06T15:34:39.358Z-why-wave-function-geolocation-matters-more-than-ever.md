## Understanding the Fundamentals of Wave-Function Geolocation

Wave-function geolocation, at its core, leverages the principles of quantum mechanics to determine the precise location of an object or individual. Unlike classical GPS, which relies on signals from satellites susceptible to interference and inaccuracies, wave-function geolocation utilizes the wave-like properties of particles to achieve unparalleled precision.

The process typically involves using entangled photons or other quantum particles. By measuring the quantum state of these particles at multiple locations, and correlating those measurements, a precise location can be calculated.
