## What are Quantum Knowledge Bases?

Quantum Knowledge Bases leverage the principles of quantum mechanics to create a fundamentally different approach to data management. Unlike classical databases that store data as bits (0 or 1), QKBs utilize qubits. Qubits, through superposition and entanglement, can represent 0, 1, or a combination of both simultaneously. This allows QKBs to store and process vastly more information than their classical counterparts, opening up possibilities previously unimaginable. ![A stylized graphic depicting the difference between a classical bit and a quantum qubit.](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1731043353233-fa7e58d21b58?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

A key component of many QKB designs is the **quantum knowledge graph**.
