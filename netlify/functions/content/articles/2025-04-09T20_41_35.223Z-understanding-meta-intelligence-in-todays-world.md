## What is Meta-Intelligence? A Clear Definition

Meta-intelligence, unlike traditional AI focused on specific tasks, is the ability of a system to improve its own intelligence and the intelligence of other systems. It's about creating algorithms and architectures that can learn, adapt, and evolve their own capabilities, and even design and deploy new, more effective AI systems. ## Common Challenges and Solutions in Meta-Intelligence Research

Despite its immense potential, meta-intelligence faces several challenges:

* **Computational complexity:**  Developing and training meta-intelligent systems requires significant computational resources.  **Solution:** Advancements in hardware and more efficient algorithms are mitigating this.
* **Data requirements:**  Meta-intelligent systems need vast amounts of data to learn and adapt effectively.  **Solution:**  Leveraging techniques like transfer learning and synthetic data generation can help address this.
* **Interpretability and explainability:**  Understanding how meta-intelligent systems arrive at their decisions is crucial.  **Solution:** Research into explainable AI (XAI) is essential to overcome this.
