## What is Living Technology?

Living technology, also known as bio-integrated technology or bio-inspired technology, transcends the limitations of traditional, static systems.  It mimics the characteristics of living organisms – adaptability, self-repair, and evolution – through the integration of biological components, bio-inspired design principles, or sophisticated algorithms that simulate biological processes.
