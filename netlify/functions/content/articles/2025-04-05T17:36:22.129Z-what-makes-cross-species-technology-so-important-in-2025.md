## Understanding the Breadth of Cross-Species Technology

Cross-species technology is a broad term encompassing any technology that bridges the gap between different species. It's not just about giving animals human-like abilities, but rather leveraging the natural strengths and unique biological characteristics of different species for mutual benefit. This includes:

*   **Animal-Human Technology Interfaces:** Devices and systems that allow for direct interaction and communication between humans and animals.
*   **Cross-Species Gene Editing:** Modifying the genes of one species using genetic material from another.
*   **Interspecies Communication Technology:** Developing tools and methods to understand and interpret animal communication.
*   **Xenotransplantation:** The transplantation of living cells, tissues, or organs from one species to another.
