## The Theoretical Underpinnings of Graviton Engineering

Graviton Engineering, at its core, is the hypothetical discipline focused on manipulating the fundamental force of gravity using gravitons – the theoretical quantum particles that mediate gravitational interaction. While the existence of gravitons remains unproven in 2025, the theoretical framework surrounding them provides the foundation for this burgeoning field. The Standard Model of particle physics struggles to incorporate gravity, leading to the development of various theories like string theory and loop quantum gravity, all attempting to bridge the gap between quantum mechanics and general relativity. These theories provide mathematical models that suggest how gravitons might behave and interact with other particles and fields.
