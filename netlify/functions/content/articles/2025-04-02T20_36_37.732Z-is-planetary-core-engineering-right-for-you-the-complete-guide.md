## Planetary Core Energy Harvesting: Tapping into the Earth's Internal Furnace

The Earth's core is a vast reservoir of geothermal energy, estimated to be equivalent to billions of years' worth of solar energy. Extracting even a fraction of this energy could provide a clean and sustainable energy source for generations to come. Potential methods include:

*   **Deep Geothermal Systems:** Drilling deep wells to access high-temperature geothermal reservoirs.
*   **Heat Pipes:** Using heat pipes to transfer heat from the core to the surface.
*   **Magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) Generators:** Converting the kinetic energy of molten metal in the core into electricity.
