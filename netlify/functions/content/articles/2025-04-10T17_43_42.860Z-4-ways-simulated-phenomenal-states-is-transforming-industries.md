## What Are Simulated Phenomenal States?

Simulated phenomenal states (SPS) refer to the computational modeling and simulation of subjective experience – that "what it's like" aspect of consciousness known as qualia.  This differs from simply simulating behavior; it aims to recreate the *feeling* of an experience, the raw, unmediated sensation.  Think of the difference between a robot mimicking human walking and a robot actually *feeling* the sensation of walking.  This pursuit is closely tied to the field of artificial consciousness, aiming to understand and potentially replicate consciousness itself.
