## What is Mind Meshing?

Mind meshing, also known as direct brain-to-brain communication, refers to the direct transfer of thoughts, emotions, and sensory experiences between two or more individuals without the use of traditional communication methods like speech or writing.  ![Illustration depicting two brains connected by a neural network](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1667681362383-d9239c8fa2b7?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

Current research primarily focuses on non-invasive methods, utilizing brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) to decode neural signals and transmit them to another individual's brain via a similar interface.  The complexity lies in accurately interpreting the nuanced signals of the brain and translating them into a format that can be understood and processed by another.
