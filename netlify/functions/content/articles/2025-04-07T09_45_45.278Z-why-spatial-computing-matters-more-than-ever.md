## What is Spatial Computing? A Deep Dive

At its core, spatial computing is about understanding and interacting with the physical world through digital means. It goes beyond simply displaying information on a screen; it's about *understanding* the environment around you and using that understanding to provide relevant and immersive experiences. Think of it as making computers "spatially aware," capable of perceiving and reacting to their surroundings in real-time.

This field encompasses a range of technologies, including:

*   **Augmented Reality (AR):** Overlays digital information onto the real world, enhancing our perception of reality. Think of AR apps that let you virtually try on clothes before buying them online.
*   **Virtual Reality (VR):** Creates completely immersive digital environments, blocking out the real world. VR is often used for gaming, training simulations, and virtual meetings.
*   **Mixed Reality (MR):** Blends AR and VR, allowing digital objects to interact with the real world in a realistic way. MR is frequently used in industrial design and collaborative work environments.
