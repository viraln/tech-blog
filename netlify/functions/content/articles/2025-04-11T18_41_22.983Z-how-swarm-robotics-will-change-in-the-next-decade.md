## What is Swarm Robotics?

Swarm robotics leverages the collective intelligence of a large number of relatively simple robots, called agents, to achieve a complex task that would be impossible for a single robot to accomplish.  These robots, often small and inexpensive, communicate and coordinate with each other using local interactions, adapting dynamically to changing environments.  ![Microscopic image of a swarm of robots](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1603354350266-a8de3496163b?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

## Swarm Robotics Advantages: Why the Buzz?

The advantages of swarm robotics are numerous and compelling:

* **Robustness:** If one robot fails, the swarm can continue functioning, ensuring mission success.
* **Scalability:** Swarms can easily be scaled up or down to meet the needs of a specific task.
* **Flexibility:** Swarms can adapt to changing environments and unforeseen obstacles.
* **Cost-Effectiveness:** Individual robots are often inexpensive, making the overall system cost-effective.
* **Efficiency:**  Swarms can accomplish tasks much faster and more efficiently than individual robots.
