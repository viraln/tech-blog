## Implementation Guide: Boosting Your Brainpower Today

While the future of cognitive enhancement is exciting, there are plenty of things you can do *today* to improve your brain function.

**Step 5:** **Manage Stress:** Practice stress-reducing techniques such as meditation, yoga, or deep breathing exercises.
