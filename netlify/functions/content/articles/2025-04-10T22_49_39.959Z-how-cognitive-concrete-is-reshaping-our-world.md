## Understanding Cognitive Concrete: Beyond Traditional Materials

Cognitive concrete, also known as self-healing concrete or smart concrete, represents a significant leap forward in material science.  Unlike traditional concrete, which is inherently passive and prone to cracking and degradation over time, cognitive concrete incorporates advanced materials and technologies to enhance its performance and longevity.
