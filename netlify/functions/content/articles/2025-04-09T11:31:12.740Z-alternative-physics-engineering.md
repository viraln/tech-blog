## Understanding Alternative Physics: Beyond the Standard Model

Traditional physics, while incredibly successful, leaves many fundamental questions unanswered.  Alternative physics explores theories and concepts beyond the Standard Model, seeking to explain phenomena like dark matter, dark energy, and the unification of forces.  This isn't about rejecting established physics; it's about expanding its horizons.  We're talking about exploring avenues like:

* **Quantum Engineering:** Harnessing the bizarre properties of quantum mechanics for technological advancements.
