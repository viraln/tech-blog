##  Home Genetics Experiments: A Practical Guide

Let's outline a simple, safe home genetics experiment: DNA extraction from strawberries.

**Step 1:** Gather materials: Strawberries, zip-top bag, dish soap, salt, cold water, ethanol (isopropyl alcohol), coffee filter, test tube or small glass.

**Step 2:** Mash strawberries in the zip-top bag.

**Step 3:** Add a mixture of dish soap, salt, and water to the bag, and gently mix.

**Step 4:** Filter the mixture through a coffee filter into a test tube.

**Step 5:** Carefully add cold ethanol to the test tube.  DNA will precipitate out as a cloudy white substance.
