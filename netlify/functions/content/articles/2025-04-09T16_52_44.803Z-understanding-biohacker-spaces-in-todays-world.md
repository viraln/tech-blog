## What are Biohacker Spaces?

Biohacker spaces, also known as DIY bio labs or citizen science labs, are collaborative workspaces where individuals can engage in biohacking – the practice of self-experimentation and optimization of biological processes.  These spaces provide access to equipment, resources, and a supportive community for those interested in exploring various biohacking techniques. ## Types of Biohacker Spaces and Their Offerings

Biohacker spaces vary greatly in their size, focus, and resources. Some focus on specific areas like microbiome analysis or genetic testing, while others offer a broader range of activities.  Here are some common types:

* **DIY Bio Labs:** These spaces prioritize hands-on experimentation, often focusing on accessible projects like growing microorganisms or conducting basic genetic engineering.
* **Quantified Self Labs:**  These labs emphasize data collection and analysis related to personal health, using wearable sensors and other technologies to track various biometrics.
* **Biohacking Co-working Spaces:** These are more flexible, offering a combination of equipment access and co-working space for those working on individual biohacking projects.

The equipment available also varies significantly.  Common offerings include:

* **Microscopes:** For observing cells and microorganisms.
* **Centrifuges:** For separating components of biological samples.
* **PCR machines:** For amplifying DNA.
* **3D printers:** For creating custom labware and tools.
* **Bioreactors:** For growing cells and tissues.
