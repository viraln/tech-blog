## What are Entangled Community Networks?  Decentralizing the Internet

Entangled community networks, often built using mesh network technology, represent a radical shift from the traditional, centralized internet infrastructure. Instead of relying on a single point of failure (like a central server or cable company), these networks distribute connectivity across multiple nodes, creating a resilient and decentralized system.  Think of it as a web of interconnected devices, each sharing its internet connection with its neighbors.  This creates a robust network capable of withstanding outages and censorship, offering greater autonomy and control to the community.  ![Illustration of a mesh network with multiple nodes interconnected](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1522543558187-768b6df7c25c?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
