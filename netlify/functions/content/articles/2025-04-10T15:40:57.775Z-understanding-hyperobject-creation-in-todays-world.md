## What is a Hyperobject? A Deep Dive into Hyperobject Theory

Before diving into creation, we must understand the concept.  Hyperobject theory, pioneered by Timothy Morton, describes entities that are massively distributed in time and space, exceeding our capacity for direct perception or comprehension. Think climate change, the internet, or even plastic pollution – these are hyperobjects.
