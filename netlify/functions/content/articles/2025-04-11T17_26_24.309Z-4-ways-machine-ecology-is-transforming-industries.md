## What is Machine Ecology? A Comprehensive Definition

Machine ecology, unlike artificial intelligence which focuses on individual intelligent agents, is the study and design of interconnected systems of machines.  It's about understanding how these machines interact, evolve, and adapt within their environment – much like the ecological systems found in nature.  This includes analyzing their resource consumption, communication patterns, and overall impact on the wider system. ![A diagram illustrating interconnected machines in a machine ecology system](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1698087278392-28e512473197?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
