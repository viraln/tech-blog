## Cognitive Automation vs. RPA: Understanding the Differences

While often used interchangeably, cognitive automation and RPA are distinct technologies.  The key differences are summarized in the table below:

| Feature          | Cognitive Automation                               | RPA                                      |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Data Handling** | Handles structured and unstructured data            | Primarily handles structured data          |
| **Decision-Making** | Can make decisions based on learned patterns       | Follows pre-defined rules                  |
| **Complexity**    | Automates complex, nuanced tasks                 | Automates repetitive, rule-based tasks     |
| **Technology**    | AI, ML, NLP, Computer Vision                      | Rule-based scripting, screen scraping      |
| **Learning Ability** | Learns and improves over time                     | Does not learn independently             |
