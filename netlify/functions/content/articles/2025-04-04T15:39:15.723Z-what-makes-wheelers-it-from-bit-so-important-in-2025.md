John Archibald Wheeler (1911-2008) was a legendary physicist, a pioneer in nuclear fission, black hole physics, and quantum mechanics. ## The Participatory Universe: Our Role in Shaping Reality

Wheeler's concept of the "participatory universe" is perhaps the most intriguing aspect of "It from Bit." It suggests that we, as observers, are not merely passive spectators but active participants in the creation of reality.
