## What is Material Intelligence? A Comprehensive Definition

Material Intelligence (MI) is the application of data science, artificial intelligence (AI), and machine learning (ML) to understand, predict, and optimize the behavior and performance of materials throughout their entire lifecycle.  It goes beyond traditional material science by integrating vast datasets – encompassing chemical composition, manufacturing processes, performance characteristics, and even environmental impact – to create predictive models and intelligent decision-making tools.  This allows for a deeper understanding of material properties and their interactions, leading to more efficient and effective material selection, design, and usage.  Instead of relying solely on experimentation and intuition, MI empowers businesses to leverage data-driven insights for informed choices.  This definition encompasses everything from the initial material selection process to end-of-life recycling strategies.
