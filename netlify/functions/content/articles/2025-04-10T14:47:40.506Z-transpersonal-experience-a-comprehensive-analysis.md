## What is Transpersonal Psychology and Transpersonal Experience?

Transpersonal psychology is a branch of psychology exploring states of consciousness beyond the personal ego.  It examines experiences such as mystical states, peak experiences, spiritual awakenings, and profound connections with nature.  A *transpersonal experience*, therefore, is any experience that transcends the boundaries of the individual self, leading to a sense of interconnectedness with something greater.  This could involve a feeling of unity with the universe, a connection with a higher power, or a deep sense of compassion for all beings.  ![A person meditating peacefully in nature, symbolizing connection and transcendence](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1535223289827-42f1e9919769?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
