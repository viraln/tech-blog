## Morphological Rights Infringement: Recognizing and Addressing Violations

"Morphological rights infringement" occurs when a competitor uses a trademark that is morphologically similar to your registered mark, and that use is likely to cause consumer confusion.

**Signs of Morphological Rights Infringement:**

*   A competitor uses a brand name that shares a common root or meaning with your trademark.
*   The competitor's products or services are similar to those offered under your trademark.
*   Consumers are likely to associate the competitor's brand with your brand.
*   The competitor is intentionally trying to capitalize on your brand's reputation.

**Addressing Morphological Rights Infringement:**

1.  **Gather evidence:** Document the infringing use, including screenshots, product samples, and consumer complaints.
2.  **Send a cease-and-desist letter:** Demand that the infringer stop using the infringing trademark immediately.
3.  **File a trademark opposition:** If the infringer has filed a trademark application, oppose the application.
4.  **Initiate infringement litigation:** If the infringer refuses to cease use of the infringing trademark, file a lawsuit seeking injunctive relief and damages.
