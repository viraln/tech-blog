## What is Ambient Nanotechnology?

Ambient nanotechnology refers to the integration of nanoscale devices and systems into everyday environments. Unlike traditional nanotechnology applications, which often involve targeted interventions, ambient nanotech focuses on creating pervasive, unobtrusive networks of nanosensors and actuators that continuously monitor and interact with their surroundings.
