## What is Stream Intelligence? A 2025 Perspective

Stream intelligence, in its simplest form, is the ability to analyze and derive actionable insights from continuous data streams in real-time.  Unlike traditional batch processing, which involves collecting data over a period and then analyzing it later, stream intelligence processes data as it arrives, providing immediate feedback and enabling instantaneous responses.
