## What is Oscillation Computing?

Oscillation computing leverages the predictable and controllable behavior of oscillating systems – like oscillators and resonators – to perform computations. Unlike traditional digital computing, which relies on binary (0 and 1) representations, oscillation computing uses the frequency, amplitude, and phase of oscillations to encode and process information.  This allows for parallel processing and potentially significantly higher computational speeds.
