## What Are Fractal Energy Networks?

Fractal energy networks leverage the principles of fractal geometry – self-similar patterns repeating at different scales – to create robust and adaptable power grids. Unlike traditional hierarchical grids, which are vulnerable to cascading failures, fractal networks are decentralized and redundant.  This means that if one part of the network fails, the system automatically reroutes energy around the affected area, maintaining power flow. ![A visual representation of a fractal energy network compared to a traditional grid](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451847251646-8a6c0dd1510c?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

Think of a tree: its branches, twigs, and leaves all share a similar structure, creating a highly efficient system for distributing nutrients and water. This inherent redundancy and adaptability make them incredibly resilient to disruptions, whether from natural disasters, cyberattacks, or localized failures.
