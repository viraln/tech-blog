## How Does Sensory Augmentation Work?

The mechanisms behind sensory augmentation vary widely depending on the specific sense being enhanced.  For visual augmentation, technologies like augmented reality (AR) glasses overlay digital information onto the real world.  For auditory augmentation, sophisticated hearing aids and cochlear implants amplify and process sounds.
