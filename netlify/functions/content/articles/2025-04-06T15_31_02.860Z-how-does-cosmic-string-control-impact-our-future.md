## Understanding Cosmic Strings: A Primer

Cosmic strings are not strings in the traditional sense, like those on a guitar. Instead, they are theoretical one-dimensional defects in spacetime that may have formed during phase transitions in the very early universe. Think of them like cracks in ice forming as water freezes. These "cracks" are incredibly thin but possess enormous density due to the energy trapped within them.
