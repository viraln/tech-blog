## What is Psychic Paleontology?

Psychic paleontology is the application of psychic abilities, such as clairvoyance, remote viewing, and empathy, to gather information about prehistoric life, particularly dinosaurs and other fossilized organisms. It posits that fossils retain an energetic imprint of the living being they once were, and that skilled psychics can access this information to learn about the creature's appearance, behavior, environment, and even the circumstances of its death. This field often incorporates elements of archaeometry, the application of scientific techniques to archaeological problems, but focuses on the psychic aspect of information retrieval.

## Core Methods Used in Psychic Paleontology

While psychic paleontology is not a standardized field with universally accepted protocols, several common methods are employed by practitioners:

*   **Remote Viewing:** This involves attempting to perceive information about a distant target (in this case, a fossil or the environment it came from) using only psychic abilities. *   **Clairvoyance:** This is the ability to gain information about an object or event through extrasensory perception. A clairvoyant psychic might hold a fossil and receive visual images, sounds, or emotions related to the dinosaur.
*   **Empathy:** This involves feeling the emotions or sensations of another being. In psychic paleontology, an empathic psychic might attempt to connect with the emotions or experiences of the dinosaur that the fossil represents.
*   **Psychometry:** This is the ability to obtain information about an object by touching it. A psychometric psychic might hold a fossil and receive impressions about its history and the life of the creature it represents.
*   **Automatic Writing/Drawing:** Some psychics use automatic writing or drawing as a means of channeling information from the fossil. They allow their hand to be guided by an unseen force, producing text or images that are believed to relate to the dinosaur.
