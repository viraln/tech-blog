## Emotional AI: Understanding and Responding to Human Emotion

Emotional AI aims to enable computers to understand, interpret, and respond to human emotions.  This involves analyzing various data points like facial expressions, vocal tone, and even physiological signals (heart rate, skin conductance) to gauge emotional states.  Applications include:

* 💻 **Mental health support:** AI companions can provide personalized support and monitor emotional well-being, offering timely interventions when needed.
* 🧑‍⚕️ **Improved customer service:**  Chatbots and virtual assistants can detect customer frustration and adapt their responses accordingly, leading to more positive interactions.
* 🎮 **Adaptive gaming experiences:** Games can adjust their difficulty and narrative based on the player's emotional responses, creating a more engaging and personalized experience.
