## 4.  Programming for Environmental Modeling and Simulation

Environmental modeling involves creating computer simulations to understand and predict environmental processes.  Popular modeling techniques include:

* **Agent-Based Modeling (ABM):** Simulating the interactions of individual agents (e.g., animals, plants) to understand emergent patterns.
* **System Dynamics Modeling:** Modeling complex systems with feedback loops and interdependencies.
* **Hydrological Modeling:** Simulating water flow and water quality in rivers, lakes, and aquifers.
* **Climate Modeling:** Simulating the Earth's climate system to predict future climate change scenarios.
