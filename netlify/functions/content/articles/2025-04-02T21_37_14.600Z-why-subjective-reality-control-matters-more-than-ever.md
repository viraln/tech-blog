## The Law of Assumption: A Powerful Tool for Manifesting Reality

The Law of Assumption, popularized by Neville Goddard, states that whatever you assume to be true, no matter how improbable, will eventually manifest in your reality. This isn't about wishful thinking; it's about embodying the *feeling* of already having what you desire.

To use the Law of Assumption effectively:

1.  **Decide on Your Desired Outcome:** Be clear and specific about what you want to manifest.
2.  **Assume the Feeling of the Wish Fulfilled:** This is the crucial step. Don't just think about having it; *feel* as if you already do. What would your life be like? How would you feel?
3.  **Persist in Your Assumption:** Doubt and negative thoughts will inevitably arise. When they do, gently redirect your focus back to the feeling of your wish fulfilled.
4.  **Live From the End:** Make decisions and take actions that are consistent with your desired reality.
