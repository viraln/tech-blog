Artificial wilderness, or simulated nature, encompasses the creation of environments that mimic the aesthetic and experiential qualities of natural wilderness areas. ## Artificial Wilderness Tourism and Ethical Considerations

The potential for artificial wilderness tourism is immense.  It offers a sustainable alternative to traditional tourism, reducing pressure on fragile natural ecosystems. However, ethical considerations are paramount.  Transparency is key – visitors should be clearly informed that they are experiencing a simulated environment.  The potential for "greenwashing" – falsely presenting artificial wilderness as a conservation effort – must be carefully avoided.  The focus should be on using these spaces to raise awareness of environmental issues and promote conservation efforts in the real world.
