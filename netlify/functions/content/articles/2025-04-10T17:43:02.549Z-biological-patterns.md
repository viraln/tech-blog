## Biological Pattern Formation: Mechanisms and Models

The formation of biological patterns is a complex process involving intricate interactions between genes, proteins, and the environment. Several key mechanisms contribute to pattern formation:

* **Reaction-Diffusion Systems:** These mathematical models describe how interacting substances can create spatially organized patterns, as seen in animal coat markings.
* **Genetic Regulation:**  Genes control the production of proteins that influence cell differentiation and tissue organization, leading to the formation of specific patterns.
* **Mechanical Forces:** Physical forces, such as cell-cell adhesion and tissue tension, can shape the patterns observed in developing tissues and organs.
* **Environmental Cues:** External factors, such as temperature, light, and nutrient availability, can influence pattern formation by altering gene expression or cell behavior.
