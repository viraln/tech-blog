The traditional engineering model, focused primarily on efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and functionality, is no longer sufficient. Consumers, employees, and investors are demanding more. Purpose Engineering goes beyond simply minimizing harm; it actively seeks to create solutions that address pressing global challenges. It's about aligning engineering skills with personal values and contributing to something larger than oneself.

## Defining Purpose Engineering: It's More Than Just "Doing Good"

Purpose Engineering is a holistic approach that embeds purpose into the DNA of engineering projects. It involves:

*   **Identifying a clear social or environmental problem:** Start by understanding the specific challenge you aim to address.
*   **Defining measurable impact:** Set clear goals and metrics to track the positive outcomes of your work.
*   **Incorporating ethical considerations:** Ensure that your solutions are developed and deployed responsibly, minimizing potential harm.
*   **Engaging stakeholders:** Collaborate with communities, experts, and other stakeholders to co-create solutions that meet their needs.
*   **Promoting transparency and accountability:** Be open about your processes and outcomes, and be accountable for the impact of your work.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Purpose Engineering isn't just about charity; it's about creating sustainable, impactful solutions that are financially viable.
