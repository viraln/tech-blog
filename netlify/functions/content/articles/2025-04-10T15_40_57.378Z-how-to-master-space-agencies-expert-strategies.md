## How Space Agencies Work: From Funding to Launch

Space agencies are complex organizations with intricate processes.  Funding is a cornerstone, often determined by national priorities and budgetary allocations.  These funds support research, development, construction, and launch operations.

**The typical process:**

1. **Research & Development:** Scientists and engineers conduct research, propose missions, and develop technologies.
2. **Mission Planning & Approval:**  Proposals are reviewed and approved based on scientific merit, feasibility, and budget.
3. **Construction & Testing:**  Spacecraft, rockets, and other equipment are built and rigorously tested.
4. **Launch & Operations:** Missions are launched, data is collected, and results are analyzed.
5. **Data Analysis & Publication:** Findings are disseminated through scientific publications and public outreach.
