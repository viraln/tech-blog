## Deciphering the Nuclear Lamina Function: A Structural Scaffold with a Regulatory Role

The nuclear lamina, a meshwork of intermediate filaments lining the inner nuclear membrane, provides structural support to the nucleus. However, its functions extend far beyond simple scaffolding.  The lamina plays a crucial role in genome organization, gene regulation, and DNA repair. Mutations in lamina proteins are linked to various diseases, including progeria and muscular dystrophy.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  The interaction between the lamina and chromatin is a critical area of research.  Focus on understanding how lamina proteins influence chromatin structure and gene expression.
