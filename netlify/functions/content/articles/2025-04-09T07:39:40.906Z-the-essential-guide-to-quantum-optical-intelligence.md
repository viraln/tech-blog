## What is Quantum Optical Intelligence?

Quantum optical intelligence leverages the principles of quantum mechanics and optics to process information in fundamentally new ways. Unlike classical computers that rely on bits representing 0 or 1, quantum optical systems utilize photons—particles of light—to encode information as qubits.  These qubits can exist in a superposition, representing 0 and 1 simultaneously, enabling vastly increased computational power and the potential for solving currently intractable problems.  This unique capability is driving innovation in diverse areas, from ultra-secure communication to highly sensitive sensing.  ![A microscopic image of photons interacting](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1516192518150-0d8fee5425e3?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
