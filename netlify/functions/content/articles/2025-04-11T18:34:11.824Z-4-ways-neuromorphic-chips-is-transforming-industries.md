## Understanding Neuromorphic Computing: A Brain-Inspired Revolution

Neuromorphic computing mimics the structure and function of the human brain. Unlike traditional von Neumann architectures, which rely on sequential processing, neuromorphic chips employ massively parallel processing inspired by neural networks. ## Neuromorphic Chip Architecture: A Deep Dive

Understanding how neuromorphic chips work requires delving into their unique architecture. Unlike traditional chips that rely on a central processing unit (CPU), neuromorphic chips are composed of interconnected arrays of artificial neurons and synapses. These components communicate through spikes of electrical activity, mimicking the way neurons communicate in the brain. ![Detailed diagram of a neuromorphic chip architecture](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1504693390394-c24671c3e8bb?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
