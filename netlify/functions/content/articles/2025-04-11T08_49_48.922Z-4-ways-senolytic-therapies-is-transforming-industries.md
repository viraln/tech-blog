## What are Senolytics? Understanding the Science Behind the Hype

Senescent cells, often referred to as "zombie cells," accumulate in our bodies as we age. While they cease dividing, they remain metabolically active, releasing harmful substances that contribute to inflammation, tissue damage, and the development of age-related diseases.  Senolytic therapies are designed to selectively eliminate these senescent cells, promoting tissue repair and potentially extending healthspan. ![Microscopic image of senescent cells](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1728996777141-82153f8d210d?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
