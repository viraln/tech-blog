## The Immortal's Code: Crafting Your Personal Moral Compass

Unlike mortal societies with established legal and ethical systems, immortals may find themselves in uncharted territory. Therefore, developing a personal *immortality moral code* is crucial. This code should be a living document, constantly evolving and adapting as you gain new experiences and insights.

**Step 1:** Define Your Core Values. What principles are non-negotiable for you? These could include compassion, justice, knowledge, creativity, or freedom.

**Step 2:** Anticipate Ethical Dilemmas. Consider potential scenarios you might face as an immortal, such as:

    *   Witnessing atrocities over centuries.
    *   Having access to advanced technologies that could be misused.
    *   Influencing the course of human history.
    *   Developing relationships with mortals who will eventually die.

**Step 3:** Develop Guiding Principles for Each Dilemma. For example, if you value compassion, how will you respond to suffering you witness over centuries? Will you actively intervene, or will you adopt a more detached perspective?

**Step 4:** Regularly Review and Revise Your Code. Your values and understanding of the world will change over time. Your immortal ethics should reflect this evolution.
