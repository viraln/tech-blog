## Building a Home on a Rock: Habitat Design and Construction

Creating a habitable environment on an asteroid presents unique engineering challenges. With no atmosphere, extreme temperature variations, and constant exposure to radiation, shielding and life support systems are paramount.

Here are a few potential habitat designs:

1.  **Internal Habitats:** Carving out space within the asteroid itself provides natural radiation shielding and temperature regulation. This requires advanced mining techniques and structural support.
2.  **Surface Habitats with Regolith Shielding:** Building structures on the surface and then covering them with layers of regolith (asteroid soil) offers a simpler, though less effective, shielding solution.
3.  **Rotating Habitats:** Creating artificial gravity through rotation is crucial for long-term health. These habitats could be built as separate modules and then connected to the asteroid.
