## Types of Folded Space Furniture: A Comprehensive Overview

The world of folded space furniture is incredibly diverse.  Here are some key categories:

* **Murphy Beds:**  These classic space-savers fold neatly into the wall, freeing up floor space during the day.  They've evolved significantly, with many now offering stylish designs and added storage features.
* **Fold-Away Tables & Desks:**  Perfect for small apartments or home offices, these tables and desks fold flat against the wall when not in use.

## Space-Saving Strategies Beyond Furniture: Maximizing Your Small Space

Folded space design isn't solely about furniture; it's a holistic approach to space management.  Here are some additional strategies:

* **Light Colors:** Opt for light and neutral colors on walls and floors to make the space feel more open and airy.
