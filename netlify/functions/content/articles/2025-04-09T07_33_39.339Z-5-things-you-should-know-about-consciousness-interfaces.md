## 2. How Does a Consciousness Interface Work?  The Science Behind the Seamless Connection

The mechanics vary depending on the specific technology.  Currently, prominent methods include:

* **Electroencephalography (EEG):** Non-invasive, measuring brainwave activity through electrodes placed on the scalp. This is the most accessible method, though its resolution is lower compared to invasive techniques.
* **Electrocorticography (ECoG):**  Invasive, involving electrodes placed directly on the brain's surface. Offers higher resolution than EEG.
* **Intracortical Brain-Computer Interfaces:** The most invasive approach, involving electrodes implanted directly within the brain's cortex. This provides the highest spatial resolution but carries significant risks.
* **Magnetoencephalography (MEG):** Non-invasive, detecting magnetic fields produced by electrical activity in the brain.  Provides good temporal resolution but is expensive and requires specialized equipment.
