## What is Dimensional Folding? Unpacking the Science

"Dimensional folding," at its core, is the theoretical manipulation of spacetime to effectively shorten the distance between two points. Think of it like folding a piece of paper so that two points far apart on the unfolded sheet become adjacent. In the context of the universe, this "folding" would involve warping spacetime itself, potentially creating shortcuts or "wormholes" that bypass conventional travel through space.
