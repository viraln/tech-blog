While genetics play a role, our lifestyle choices have a profound impact on our cognitive processing speed. This is where the revolutionary aspect comes in: we have far more control than we think.

## 3. Revolutionary Training Techniques to Supercharge Your Cognitive Speed

Beyond lifestyle changes, specific training techniques can dramatically improve cognitive processing speed. These techniques target different aspects of cognitive function, allowing for a more comprehensive and personalized approach.

Here are some revolutionary training techniques:

**✅ Dual N-Back Training:** This working memory training exercise involves simultaneously remembering and updating two streams of information – visual and auditory. Studies have shown that Dual N-Back training can significantly improve fluid intelligence and processing speed. There are numerous apps and online resources available to guide you through this training.

**⚡ Speed Reading:** Learning to read faster can enhance your brain's ability to process information efficiently. Speed reading techniques involve minimizing subvocalization (reading words silently in your head) and expanding your visual span.

**🔑 Attention Training:** Attention is a crucial component of processing speed. Attention training exercises, such as the Stroop test and the Posner cueing task, can improve your ability to focus and filter out distractions.

**🧠 Neurofeedback:** This advanced technique involves monitoring brainwave activity in real-time and providing feedback to help individuals learn to regulate their brain function. Neurofeedback has shown promise in improving attention, processing speed, and other cognitive abilities.

**🎮 Brain Training Games:** Several brain training games are designed to improve cognitive skills, including processing speed. While the effectiveness of these games is still debated, some studies suggest that they can provide benefits, particularly when combined with other training techniques.

Here's a simple cognitive processing speed exercise you can try right now:

**Step 1:** Find a random article online.
**Step 2:** Set a timer for 2 minutes.
**Step 3:** Read the article as quickly as you can while still comprehending the content.
**Step 4:** After 2 minutes, stop reading and summarize the main points of the article.
**Step 5:** Repeat this exercise daily, gradually increasing your reading speed.
