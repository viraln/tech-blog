QFT goes a step further than traditional Emotional Freedom Techniques (EFT) by incorporating the concept of quantum fluctuations. Quantum fluctuations are temporary changes in the amount of energy in a point in space, as explained by quantum mechanics. By consciously focusing our intention during the tapping process, we can influence these fluctuations, potentially creating positive changes in our reality.

## How to Perform Quantum Fluctuation Tapping: A Practical Guide

While personalized protocols are the future, here's a basic framework to get you started with quantum tapping:

**Step 1:** **Identify the Issue:** Clearly define the issue or problem you want to address. Be specific and honest with yourself. Rate the intensity of the feeling on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is no intensity and 10 is the highest intensity.

**Step 2:** **Create a Setup Statement:** Formulate a setup statement that acknowledges the issue while affirming your self-acceptance. For example: "Even though I feel anxious about this presentation, I deeply and completely accept myself." Repeat this statement three times while tapping on the Karate Chop point (the fleshy side of your hand).

**Step 3:** **The Tapping Sequence:** Tap on each of the following meridian points approximately 5-7 times while repeating a reminder phrase related to the issue. Use two fingers to tap firmly but gently on each point.

*   **Eyebrow (EB):** The inner edge of the eyebrow.
*   **Side of the Eye (SE):** On the bony ridge at the side of the eye.
*   **Under the Eye (UE):** On the bony ridge under the eye.
*   **Under the Nose (UN):** Between the nose and the upper lip.
*   **Chin Point (CP):** In the crease of the chin.
*   **Collarbone Point (CB):** Where the collarbone meets the sternum.
*   **Under the Arm (UA):** About four inches below the armpit.
*   **Top of the Head (TH):** The crown of the head.

**Step 4:** **Deep Breath and Re-Evaluation:** Take a deep breath and re-evaluate the intensity of the feeling. Has it decreased? If not, repeat the tapping sequence, adjusting the reminder phrase as needed.

**Step 5:** **Positive Affirmations:** Once the intensity has decreased, tap through the points again while repeating positive affirmations related to your desired outcome. For example: "I am calm and confident," "I am capable and successful," "I am grateful for this opportunity."

> **EXPERT TIP:** Consistency is key. Practice quantum tapping regularly, even when you're not feeling stressed or anxious, to strengthen your energy field and enhance its resilience.
