*   **The Equation:** The formula z = z² + c, where 'z' and 'c' are complex numbers, is the seed of the Mandelbrot set. We start with z = 0 and iterate this equation.
*   **Escape or Bound:** If, after repeated iterations, the absolute value of 'z' remains below a certain threshold (usually 2), the complex number 'c' belongs to the Mandelbrot set.
