## How Does Sensory Augmentation Work? A Technical Overview (Simplified)

Sensory augmentation generally follows these steps:

1. **Sensory Input:** Sensors collect data from the environment (light, sound, pressure, etc.).
2. **Data Processing:**  Algorithms filter, enhance, and interpret the sensory data.
3. **Signal Conversion:**  The processed data is converted into a form that can be perceived by the user (visual, auditory, tactile, etc.).
4. **Sensory Stimulation:**  The converted signal stimulates the relevant sensory organs or directly stimulates the brain.
