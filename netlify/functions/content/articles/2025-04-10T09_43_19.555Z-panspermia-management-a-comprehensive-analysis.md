## Understanding Panspermia: A Primer

Panspermia, a word coined in 1883, encompasses various theories proposing the transfer of life between celestial bodies.  The most prominent is *lithopanspermia*, suggesting microbes hitch a ride on rocks ejected from planets.  ![Microscopic image of extremophile bacteria](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1454165804606-c3d57bc86b40?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
