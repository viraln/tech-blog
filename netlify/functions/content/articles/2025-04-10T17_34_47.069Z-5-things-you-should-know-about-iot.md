## 4. Understanding and Mitigating IoT Security Risks

The interconnected nature of IoT devices also presents significant security challenges.  Vulnerabilities in poorly secured devices can be exploited by malicious actors, leading to data breaches, system disruptions, and even physical damage. Key security risks include:

* **Data breaches:**  Unauthorized access to sensitive data stored on IoT devices.
* **Denial-of-service attacks:**  Overwhelming IoT devices with traffic, rendering them unusable.
* **Malware infections:**  Compromising IoT devices with malicious software.
* **Man-in-the-middle attacks:**  Intercepting communication between IoT devices and the cloud.
* **Physical tampering:**  Gaining physical access to devices and manipulating their functionality.

> **EXPERT TIP:**  Prioritize security from the outset.
