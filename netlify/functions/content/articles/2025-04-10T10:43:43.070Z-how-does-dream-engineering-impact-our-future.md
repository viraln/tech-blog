## What is Dream Engineering?

Dream engineering, in its simplest form, is the manipulation and influence of dreams.  It's not about passively observing dreams but actively shaping their content, emotions, and even their physiological effects. This involves a multidisciplinary approach, incorporating elements of neuroscience, psychology, computer science, and even artificial intelligence.
