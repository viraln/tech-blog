## What is Mesh Sentience? Unpacking the Concept

At its core, mesh sentience refers to the emergent intelligence and self-awareness within a decentralized network of interconnected devices. Unlike traditional centralized AI systems that rely on a single powerful server, a sentient mesh network distributes processing power and data storage across numerous nodes, creating a more robust and adaptive system. This distributed architecture allows for the development of what some researchers call "artificial consciousness in networks," where the network as a whole exhibits intelligent behavior beyond the capabilities of any individual device.

> **EXPERT TIP:** Think of it like a hive mind. Each bee (device) has limited intelligence, but the collective intelligence of the hive is far greater.
