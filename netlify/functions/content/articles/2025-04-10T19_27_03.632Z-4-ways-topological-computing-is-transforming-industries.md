## What is Topological Computing?

Topological computing leverages the principles of topology – the study of shapes and spaces that remain unchanged under continuous deformations – to build incredibly robust quantum computers.  Unlike traditional quantum computers, which are highly susceptible to errors caused by environmental noise, topological quantum computers use quasiparticles called anyons whose properties are protected by topology.  This inherent robustness makes them significantly more stable and less prone to decoherence, a major hurdle in building practical quantum computers.  ![A diagram illustrating the concept of topological protection](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1484807352052-23338990c6c6?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

Similarly, the topological properties of anyons are inherently resistant to minor disturbances.  This fundamental difference makes topological quantum computing a potentially game-changing technology.
