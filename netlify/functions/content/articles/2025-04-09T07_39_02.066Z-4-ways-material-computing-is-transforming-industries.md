## What is Material Computing?

Material computing transcends the limitations of traditional silicon-based computing by leveraging the inherent computational capabilities of various materials.  Instead of relying solely on electronic circuits, it harnesses the properties of materials like polymers, fabrics, and even biological cells to process information and perform computations. This opens up a vast array of possibilities for creating flexible, adaptable, and environmentally responsive computational systems.  Think of self-healing circuits embedded in clothing, or smart materials that change color based on environmental conditions – these are just glimpses into the potential of material computing. ![Microscopic image of a material computing element](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
