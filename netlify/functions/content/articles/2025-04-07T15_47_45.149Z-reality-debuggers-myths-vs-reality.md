## The Spectrum of "Reality Glitches": From Cognitive Biases to Existential Dread

Before we can debug reality, we need to identify the "bugs." These glitches manifest in various forms, ranging from everyday cognitive biases to more profound feelings of existential unease. Here's a breakdown:

*   **Cognitive Biases:** These are systematic patterns of deviation from norm or rationality in judgment. Examples include confirmation bias (seeking information that confirms existing beliefs) and the availability heuristic (overestimating the likelihood of events that are easily recalled).

*   **Limiting Beliefs:** These are deeply ingrained assumptions about ourselves and the world that hold us back from achieving our full potential. They often stem from past experiences or societal conditioning.

*   **Emotional Reactivity:** Uncontrolled emotional responses to external stimuli can cloud our judgment and lead to impulsive decisions.

*   **Misinformation and Propaganda:** The deliberate spread of false or misleading information can distort our perception of reality and manipulate our behavior.

*   **Existential Dread:** A feeling of unease or anxiety about the meaninglessness of life or the inevitability of death.

*   **Algorithmic Echo Chambers:** Social media algorithms can create echo chambers where we are only exposed to information that confirms our existing beliefs, further reinforcing biases.

🔑 Identifying these glitches is the first crucial step in the debugging process.
