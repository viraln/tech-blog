*   **The Holographic Principle:** This principle, arising from black hole physics, suggests that the information describing a volume of space can be encoded on its boundary. *   **Digital Physics and Simulation Hypothesis:** The idea that the universe could be a simulation, while controversial, aligns with IBR. *   **Observer Effect:** In quantum mechanics, the act of observing a quantum system changes its state. This hints that information – the knowledge gained through observation – plays a crucial role in shaping reality.

> **EXPERT TIP:** It's important to note that evidence for IBR is not proof. It's a growing body of observations and theoretical frameworks that are consistent with the idea that information is fundamental.
