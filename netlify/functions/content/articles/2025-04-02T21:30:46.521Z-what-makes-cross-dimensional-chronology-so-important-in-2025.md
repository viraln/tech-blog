## Cross-Dimensional Time Paradox

The concept of a cross-dimensional time paradox is perhaps the most mind-bending aspect of cross-dimensional chronology. It arises when an action in one timeline affects another timeline in a way that creates a logical contradiction.

For example, imagine travelling to an alternate timeline and preventing the birth of your own ancestor in that timeline. Would you cease to exist in your original timeline? Or would the two timelines diverge, with your original timeline remaining unchanged?

Several theories attempt to resolve these paradoxes:

*   **The Many-Worlds Interpretation:** This theory suggests that every time a quantum event occurs, the universe splits into multiple universes, each representing a different outcome. In this scenario, paradoxes are avoided because each action creates a new timeline, rather than changing an existing one.
*   **Novikov Self-Consistency Principle:** This principle states that the universe will prevent paradoxes from occurring. If you attempt to create a paradox, the universe will conspire to ensure that your actions are ultimately consistent with the timeline.
*   **Timeline Protection Agencies (Hypothetical):** Some science fiction scenarios propose the existence of agencies that monitor and prevent paradoxes from occurring.

The resolution of these paradoxes remains a subject of ongoing debate and research.
