## Generative Design vs. Parametric Design: Understanding the Difference

While both generative design and parametric design utilize algorithms, they differ significantly in their approach:

| Feature          | Generative Design                               | Parametric Design                                 |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| **Design Space** | Explores a vast design space automatically.       | Designer defines the design space and parameters. |
| **Level of Control** | Less direct designer control; algorithm-driven.    | More direct designer control; parameter-driven.    |
| **Complexity**   | Handles highly complex designs and constraints.     | Limited by designer's ability to define parameters. |
| **Optimization** | Optimizes for multiple criteria simultaneously.    | Often focuses on single criteria optimization.     |

## How to Use Generative Design: A Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Define Design Goals and Constraints:** Clearly articulate the desired outcome and any limitations (materials, budget, etc.).
2. **Select Appropriate Software:** Choose a generative design tool that aligns with your project needs and expertise.
3. **Input Parameters and Constraints:** Define the relevant parameters (dimensions, materials, forces, etc.) and constraints into the software.
4. **Run the Generative Design Algorithm:** Let the software explore the design space and generate multiple options.
5. **Evaluate and Refine Results:** Review the generated designs, select the most promising options, and iterate based on your evaluation.
6. **Finalize Design and Manufacture:** Once a suitable design is selected, proceed with detailed design and manufacturing.
