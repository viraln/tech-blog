## What is a Digital Twin?

A digital twin is a virtual representation of a real-world object, process, or system.  It's a dynamic, data-driven model that continuously updates itself based on real-time information gathered from sensors, simulations, and other data sources.  Think of it as a digital mirror reflecting the state and behavior of its physical counterpart.  This isn't just a static 3D model; it's a complex, interactive simulation capable of predicting future behavior and optimizing performance. ![A graphic showing a physical object and its corresponding digital twin](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1592084284963-884b5d66f7cc?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
