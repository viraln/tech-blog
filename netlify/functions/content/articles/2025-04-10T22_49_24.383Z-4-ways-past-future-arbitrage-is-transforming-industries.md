## Understanding Past-Future Arbitrage: A Deep Dive

Past-future arbitrage (PFA) exploits inconsistencies between historical data and future projections or expectations. Unlike traditional arbitrage, which focuses on price discrepancies across different markets *at the same time*, PFA capitalizes on discrepancies across *time*.  This involves analyzing historical trends, predicting future shifts based on established patterns, and strategically positioning assets to profit from the expected convergence.  It's about identifying undervalued assets based on their historical performance relative to anticipated future value. ![A graph showing past performance diverging from future projections](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1451187580459-43490279c0fa?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
