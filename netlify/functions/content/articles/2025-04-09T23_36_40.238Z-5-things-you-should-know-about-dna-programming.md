## 1. Understanding the Fundamentals of DNA Programming

DNA programming, at its core, involves designing and synthesizing DNA sequences to perform specific functions.  This goes beyond simply reading the genetic code; it's about writing it, editing it, and using it as a programmable material. This field leverages advancements in **DNA synthesis** and **genetic engineering**, allowing us to create custom DNA molecules with unprecedented precision.
