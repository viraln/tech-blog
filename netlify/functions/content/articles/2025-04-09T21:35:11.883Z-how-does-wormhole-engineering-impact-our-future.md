Wormholes, also known as Einstein-Rosen bridges, are theoretical tunnels through spacetime predicted by Einstein's theory of general relativity. ![Artistic depiction of a wormhole](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1581092583537-20d51b4b4f1b?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

However, the existence of traversable wormholes – ones we could actually travel through – remains purely hypothetical.

> **DID YOU KNOW?** The theoretical possibility of wormholes was first suggested in 1935 by Albert Einstein and Nathan Rosen.
