Dark matter, comprising roughly 85% of the universe's mass, remains one of the biggest unsolved mysteries in physics. ## How Dark Matter Engines Work (Theoretically)

Current models suggest several theoretical mechanisms for dark matter propulsion.  These are still highly speculative, but they offer a glimpse into the potential:

1. **Weak Interaction Mediated Propulsion:** This approach focuses on using highly sensitive detectors to capture dark matter particles. The interaction of these particles with specially designed materials could generate a small, but potentially cumulative, thrust.  ![Diagram illustrating weak interaction mediated propulsion](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1511406361295-0a1ff814c0ce?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)

2. **Annihilation/Decay Propulsion:** This method proposes harnessing the energy released when dark matter particles annihilate each other or decay. This energy could be converted into thrust through carefully designed systems. The efficiency of such a system remains a significant challenge.

3. **Dark Matter Gravitational Manipulation:**  This highly speculative approach explores the possibility of manipulating the gravitational field generated by concentrated dark matter. This could potentially provide a means of propulsion, although the energy requirements and technological hurdles are immense.
