## The Rise of In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) in Selenian Architecture

ISRU is revolutionizing lunar construction. Instead of transporting every building material from Earth (an astronomically expensive endeavor), we're focusing on utilizing resources found on the moon itself.  This includes the lunar regolith, water ice trapped in permanently shadowed craters, and potentially other minerals.
