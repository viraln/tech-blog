## What is Time Banking?

Time banking is a reciprocal system where individuals exchange their skills and services for "time credits."  Instead of using money, members earn credits for the time they contribute to the community and then redeem those credits for services they need.  One hour of service equals one time credit, regardless of the perceived monetary value of the task.  This creates a truly equitable system, valuing contributions from all members, regardless of their income or background. ![A diverse group of people participating in various time banking activities](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1434030216411-0b793f4b4173?q=85&w=1200&fit=max&fm=webp&auto=compress)
