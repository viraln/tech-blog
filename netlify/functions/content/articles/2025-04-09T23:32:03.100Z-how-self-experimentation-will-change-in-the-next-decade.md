##  How to Self-Experiment Safely: A Practical Guide

**Step 1:** Define a clear hypothesis and measurable outcome.
**Step 2:** Design a structured experiment with a control group (if possible).
**Step 3:** Choose reliable methods for data collection and tracking.
**Step 4:** Implement the experiment consistently, maintaining detailed records.
